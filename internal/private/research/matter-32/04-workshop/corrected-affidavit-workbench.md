## Two different counterfactuals

### Pleading counterfactual

Assume only well-pleaded, legally cognizable factual allegations must be accepted under the governing Rule 12 standard. Identify which specific affidavit facts the current FAC actually contests, whether the factual basis is sufficiently particularized, and whether the asserted contradiction is a real contradiction rather than a misread label.

### Evidence counterfactual

Using only supported intentional/reckless corrections, reconstruct the entire affidavit. Maintain all unaffected facts, add material known omissions, and ask whether the remainder still establishes probable cause to obtain blood evidence; do not use the criminal conviction standard or a mere possibility of innocence.

## Conclusion gate

No completed corrected-affidavit text is approved in this workshop. A filing-ready conclusion requires exact paragraph citations, full original-video review, authenticated affidavit copy, careful treatment of all unaffected facts, and a separate causal explanation for each defendant and deprivation.

If the remainder still supports the warrant, revise or abandon the judicial-deception theory rather than delete additional facts without support. Preserve any independently viable door-entry, search, or arrest claim on its own legal footing.
