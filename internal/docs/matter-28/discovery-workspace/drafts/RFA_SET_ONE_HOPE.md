1. **PLAINTIFF** means Brendan Ngehsi Newanforbi.
2. **YOU** or **HOPE** means the responding Defendant.
3. **SMITH** means Defendant Shannon Smith, LCSW.
4. **PALACIOS** means Katie Palacios.
5. **CDCR/DAPO** means the California Department of Corrections and Rehabilitation, Division of Adult Parole Operations.

9. Lack of information is a proper response only after a reasonable inquiry and when information known or readily obtainable remains insufficient.
10. A response must fairly address the substance of the request.
