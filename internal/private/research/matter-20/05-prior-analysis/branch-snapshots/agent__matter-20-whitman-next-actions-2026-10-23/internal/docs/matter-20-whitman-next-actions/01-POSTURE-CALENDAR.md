## Motion-to-compel deadline — conditional, urgent
Code Civ. Proc. **2031.310(c)** ordinarily requires notice of a **further-response** motion within 45 days of service of the verified response (or later specific date agreed in writing). On an October 23 assumed verification, 45 calendar days expires **Monday December 7, 2026**, before service extensions. An applicable 2-court-day electronic-service extension could make **December 9**, under Golf & Tennis Pro Shop. Keep **December 7 as protective internal target** unless actual verification/service/extension proves another date. Mere meet-and-confer does not toll it. If only an unverified response was served, or if CHP promised but failed to produce documents, the procedural lane changes (see 03).
