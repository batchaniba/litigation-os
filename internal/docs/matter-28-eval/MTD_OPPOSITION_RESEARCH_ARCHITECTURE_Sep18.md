## 7. Proposed opposition architecture (section order)

Mirror their motion if possible; if they lead with preclusion, **do not** open with *Merrill*.

### Caption / introduction (½–1 page)

- Case identity; M&C exhausted (Martinez Sep. 11).
- Theme sentence: state court decided a **jurisdictional precondition**, not Plaintiff’s claims; federal complaint states *Monell* / individual / Bane theories grounded in § 3008 and a pleaded blanket policy.
- Ask: deny in full; alternatively deny as to Counts I–III and grant leave on IV–V if particularity found wanting.

### Argument I — Res judicata fails (load-bearing)

**A. Legal standard** — *Migra* / § 1738; California elements (*Robi* / *DKN*): same party, final judgment on the merits, same cause of action.

**B. Split defendants** — table: HOPE vs Smith (party / judgment / merits / claim).

**C. No final judgment** — *Berri*; *Valvo*; CCP § 581d; *Kay* (2007) finality snapshot. Lodge register + minute order + tentative (RJN / judicial notice).

**D. Not on the merits (stays dead)** — quote minute order “jurisdictional requirement” / “prior to the filing of his FAC.” *Perez v. City of San Bruno*; *Heath*, 708 F.2d at **1380 n.4**; *Costello*; Restatement § 20(2). Distinguish *Goddard* / *Keidatz*. Defense-drafted formal order cannot enlarge the court’s basis.

**E. Smith** — *DKN*; *Taylor* (no virtual representation); CEO ≠ privity; notice ≠ control.

**F. Same claim (*Boeken*)** — concede only as fallback; do not lead. Bane/fraud overlap vs HOPE is the exposure *if* they manufacture a merits judgment.

**G. Issue preclusion** — narrow concession: exhaustion before state FAC only; *Patsy* for § 1983; PLRA “prisoner” carve-out inapplicable (verify before briefing).

**H. Matter 6** — disclose Rule 41(a)(1)(A)(i) dismissal without prejudice; *Semtek*; never voluntary-dismiss this action.

**Requested findings** — copy UNIFIED § 6 ¶¶ 1–4 into proposed order language.

### Argument II — State action

Pleadable under *West* / *Jensen* / *Halvorsen* / *Rawson*. Frame gravamen as joint participation in parole machinery. Distinguish *Merrill* (and *Gross*) on pleaded joint action / Form 3043 / CTM / mandatory referral facts. Dkt. 11’s decline of § 1915 screening is **not** a ruling on the claims — do not overclaim it.

### Argument III — Count I *Monell* (HOPE)

Express-policy case (*AE v. Tulare*; *Tsao* **698** F.3d — fix reporter). Manual quotes; 16 locations; Smith as policymaker; discovery admissions. Do not drift into failure-to-train (*Brown*).

### Argument IV — Count II individual capacity (Smith)

Personal involvement / policymaker (*Starr*); knowledge (*Dwyer* findings + Manual persistence). QI only if raised → threshold ineligibility pocket (§ 6 above).

### Argument V — First Amendment retaliation

*Watison* / *Rhodes* elements; Aug. 19, 2025 statements to agents; falsity of reports as adverse action. Address chill for a parolee of ordinary firmness.

### Argument VI — Due process / liberty

Reject *Kildare*. *Neal* line + § 3008 discretion removed by blanket rule. *In re Dwyer*: cite **findings** only; petition was **denied**; relief under § 3008(d)(2) disapproval — never “habeas granted.” Quote Judge Hill’s order from Matter 19 Ex. B; do not rely on ACSOL secondary attribution fights in the brief body.

### Argument VII — Bane Act

*Cornell* / *Reese*; specific intent; six specificity elements from case-law memo. No $25k private penalty. Meet *Shoyoye* (deliberate falsification, not clerical error).

### Argument VIII — Fraud / negligent misrepresentation

Rule 9(b) particularity grid (speaker / date / document / recipient / content). *Randi W.* for intermediary reliance. Economic-loss rule weak (no contract). Consider offering leave to amend on particularity rather than dying on the vine. Negligent misrep: secondary or drop if duty fails (*Bily*).

### Argument IX — RICO (lean)

Person/enterprise (*Cedric Kushner* — verify). Pattern / predicates with 9(b) dates. Standing (*Diaz* / *Horn*) vs proximate cause (*Anza*) — do not collapse. One footnote: *Schreiber* is **1986**, reversed dismissal, leave to amend. Cluster lock: do not hang the case on treble damages.

### Conclusion / proposed order

Deny; alternatively, deny I–III, leave on IV–V; write the four preclusion findings.
