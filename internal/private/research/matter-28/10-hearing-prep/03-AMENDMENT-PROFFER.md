# Targeted amendment proffer

Internal planning, not a proposed amended complaint. Do not tell the judge facts can be supplied unless the user can truthfully support them. Rule 15(a)(2) governs leave/consent when amendment as of course is unavailable; check service and prior amendments before asserting any Rule 15(a)(1) right. A valid preclusion bar is not cured by more detail alone.

## Core proffer

“If the Court finds the current pleading insufficient, I request leave to separate defendants' acts, identify the particular reporting and decision chain, specify the adverse action and resulting injury, and supply supported communication-level particulars. I would also correct the regulatory pinpoints and the two legal points already acknowledged in the opposition.”

## Requested amendment timetable

Request 28 days from entry of the order for a complete amended complaint, subject to the Court's direction and any concrete need arising from lawful production. This is a proposed period only. If the Court selects another deadline, record it exactly. If facts cannot be supported within the allowed scope, refine the pleading accordingly; do not promise discovery will retroactively repair an unsupported allegation.

An amended complaint should be complete in itself and follow Civil L.R. 15-1 and the actual leave order. A model-redline protective-order requirement is separate from any redline requirement for an amended complaint.
