                                                     CAUSE
By way of background, Mr. Sandoval commenced his term of supervised release on July 18, 2023; however,
earlier this year he was summoned to appear before Your Honor for a violation hearing on January 24, 2025. Mr.
Sandoval was found in violation that same date, admitting to patronizing websites where sexually explicit images
of adults were primarily available, and he was continued on supervised release. Additionally, Your Honor
extended his supervision to its original term of 10 years. Since then, Mr. Sandoval was permitted to obtain a
monitored cellphone.


On August 18,2025, the undersigned reviewed Mr. Sandoval's internet activity via computer monitoring software
and discovered that Mr. Sandoval was viewing images that reflected the lascivious exhibition of female genitalia
and pubic area. The activity revealed numerous female adult women in limited clothing, showing bare female
genitalia, topless, and completely nude. The same day, the undersigned met with Mr. Sandoval to confront him
regarding the activity on his monitored phone. Initially, Mr. Sandoval denied viewing any explicit material;
however, he later admitted that approximately one week ago, he was consuming alcohol heavily and may have
stumbled across nude images of females. Mr. Sandoval acknowledged that when he consumes alcohol, it may
impair his judgement. Mr. Sandoval admitted he does have an issue with alcohol and requested probation's
assistance to modify his conditions and add an alcohol abstinence condition. Based on the above information, the
probation officer seized his monitored device for further forensic examination relating to the images he was
viewing. The forensic exam results are pending.


On August 22, 2025, the undersigned met with Mr. Sandoval. Mr. Sandoval was in agreement to the above noted
modification. Based on Mr. Sandoval's willingness to combat his alcohol issues, the above noted modification
is recommended. In addition to the alcohol monitoring device and alcohol abstinence, Mr. Sandoval will undergo
a substance abuse assessment and, if needed or recommended, he will also be receiving substance abuse treatment.
These additional conditions will benefit Mr. Sandoval to address his ongoing substance abuse issue, his cognitions
in decision making, and overall success while on supervised release. Therefore, it is recommended the conditions
of supervised release be modified as outlined above. Mr. Sandoval is in agreement with the modification request
as evidenced by his signature on the attached Waiver of Hearing to Modify the Conditions of Supervised Release.
