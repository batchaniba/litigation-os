# Mutual Home Recording Consent (standalone — NOT part of the premarital agreement)

> **Check before using — parole and registration risk.** Parole conditions for registrants often bar possessing
> sexually explicit material. Bedroom recordings of intimate activity could be treated as that material, and could also
> be seized in a parole search. Get the conditions in writing from your parole agent or attorney **before** any bedroom
> camera is installed. If the conditions bar it, use Option B in §3.

## Why this is a separate document

- Consent to recording can be withdrawn at any time. If recording continues after withdrawal, it is a crime
  (Pen. Code § 632 for confidential communications; § 647(j) for viewing or recording a person in a private area without consent).
  A prenup term can't make the consent permanent.
- If recording were a condition of the marriage contract, her side could argue the whole prenup was coercive.
- A separate, mutual, revocable consent signed with no pressure is better evidence that both of you agreed freely.

## 1. Consent

Each party, freely and voluntarily, consents to continuous audio and video recording within the areas listed in § 2
of the residence at [ADDRESS] (the "Residence"), on the terms below. Each party signs this agreement separately and dates it.

## 2. Recorded areas

| Area | Video | Audio |
|---|---|---|
| Entrances, exterior | Yes | Yes |
| Living room, kitchen, hallways | Yes | Yes |
| Primary bedroom | See § 3 | See § 3 |
| Bathrooms | **Never** | **Never** |
| Guest rooms | **Never** while occupied | **Never** while occupied |

## 3. Primary bedroom (choose one)

- [ ] **Option A — Continuous.** Bedroom cameras record continuously. Each party may turn them off at any time by a visible
  physical switch. Turning them off is not a breach and creates no inference against that party.
- [ ] **Option B — Audio-only consent log.** No bedroom video. Instead, either party can start a short audio recording at the
  beginning of an intimate encounter to capture verbal consent. Either party can stop it, and either party can
  withdraw consent at any point.
- [ ] **Option C — None.** No bedroom recording. Common areas only.

## 4. Withdrawal

Either party may withdraw consent, in whole or for specific areas, by written notice (text message is enough). Recording
in the withdrawn areas stops immediately, and the system is reconfigured within 24 hours. Withdrawal has no effect
on the premarital agreement or on property rights.

## 5. Third parties

- Visible signs at each entrance: "Audio and video recording in progress."
- Guests are told about recording, and audio in common areas is muted when guests are present unless every guest agrees.
- No recording of any minor in any bedroom or bathroom.

## 6. Storage and access

- Encrypted storage. Both parties hold credentials. Neither party may delete footage alone.
- Retention: **[30] days** rolling auto-delete. Footage is preserved longer only if either party gives written notice of a dispute,
  police contact, or court proceeding.
- No copying, sharing, uploading, or showing footage to anyone, **except** to a party's attorney, law enforcement, or a court
  in connection with a claim or defense. Distributing intimate footage is a crime (Pen. Code § 647(j)(4)).
- Bedroom footage (if Option A) is stored separately and accessible only by both credentials together, or by court order.

## 7. No waiver

Nothing in this agreement is consent to any sexual act. Consent to sexual activity is given separately each time and can be
withdrawn at any time. Recording consent is only consent to being recorded.
