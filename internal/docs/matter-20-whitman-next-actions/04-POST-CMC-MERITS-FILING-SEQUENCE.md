## What goes in the principal writ-motion package?
1. **Notice of Motion and Motion** — identify both remaining respondents, Count 1 only, exactly requested form of relief, statutory basis, hearing/reservation and grounds; comply with CRC 3.1112.
2. **Memorandum of Points and Authorities** — specific statutory elements and the April 20 certification-denial abuse-of-discretion question; use controlling order, governing standard, adverse authority, and evidence. CRC 3.1113 ordinarily imposes a **15-page** opening memorandum limit absent court leave/other applicable rule; >10 pages requires table of contents and authorities. Do not assume the entire evidentiary appendix counts toward memo page limit.
3. **Declarations and authenticated exhibits**, if allowed by the court's writ hearing procedure — denial (Petition Ex. 1), original certification request, actual stamped Answer admissions, original Oct 23 CHP production by AGO Bates, complete and authentic video with accurate timestamp transcript, laboratory evidence, custodial records and crucial April 9–20 decision-file evidence. Explain foundation and service, not just Bates. Use precise exhibit-number/page/paragraph/line citations under CRC 3.1113(k).
4. **Proposed judgment and peremptory writ / proposed order**, lodged and **served separately with moving papers**, rather than attached to the memorandum, per CRC 3.1113(m) and 3.1312; form and timing subject to Dept. 25's instructions. The proposed writ should describe the exact commanded action and any lawful remand, not purport to decide disputed facts.
5. **Proof of service** establishing the motion, supporting documents, applicable petition service, lodged separate proposed order, and notice were served in accordance with the controlling schedule; check court-specific electronic document-handling/video lodging.
6. **Separate Request for Judicial Notice**, only if needed for judicially noticeable items, not a device to establish truth of disputed police/report facts; CRC 3.1113(l).

The filed/served package should be court-paper compliant and should **not contain repository URLs, confidential review materials, or any speculative memo description as evidence of the memo's contents**.

## Merits argument — objective and fallback
- **Question presented:** did the April 20, 2026 CHP refusal, concerning Petitioner's Form I-918 Supplement B request, violate the conditional statutory certification duty or constitute abuse of discretion under PC §679.10 and CCP §1085?
- **Primary conditional relief:** certification **only if** the legally relevant elements and record leave CHP no lawful discretion to deny.
- **Alternative more conservative relief:** vacatur of the denial and remand for lawful, full consideration under the correct qualifying-activity and helpfulness standards **upon proof of legal error/abuse**, without substituting the court's own unresolved fact findings for CHP.
- **Hard distinction:** The statutory no-prosecution rule and later 0.073 BAC do not alone prove qualifying criminal activity. A §851.6 certificate does not itself adjudicate no probable cause. The defense can rely on the empty alcohol container, reported odor, red eyes, HGN, Romberg, admitted driving, and totality of evidence. Address these fairly.
- **Core evidence:** source-pinned decision file and what Serrato possessed/actually considered by April 20. The May 9 internal video-review/Meza interview memoranda were created **after** the denial; do not attribute their contents or conclusions to Serrato as of April 20.

## Key gate on procedural statute (do not misstate)
- **CCP §1088:** With due notice, a peremptory writ may issue in the first instance; at least ten days' notice under this section is not a substitute for otherwise applicable noticed-motion timing under §1005(b) or a court-ordered schedule. A writ is not awarded by default.
- **CCP §1088.5:** In trial court, without seeking an alternative writ, proof of service of the filed petition must be lodged **before a hearing or action**.
- **CCP §1094:** The noticed motion for *judgment on the peremptory writ* applies when a §1088.5 petition **presents no triable issue of fact or rests solely on an administrative record**. Do **not** assert the October 12 verified Answer necessarily eliminates or establishes a triable dispute without analyzing the actual response and record.
- **CCP §1090:** If the return presents material factual questions meeting the statute's requirements, the court **may** in its discretion order those questions tried before a jury. Raise the procedural issue; do not assert all factual conflicts mandate a jury or automatically defeat a writ.
- **CCP §1005(b):** Default motion briefing lead time is 16 court days, with service-method adjustments; reconcile the prior portfolio's projected **Dec. 11** deadline with California holidays, mode of service, any court order and actual Jan. 8 hearing reservation after the CMC. Not a court-certified deadline.
