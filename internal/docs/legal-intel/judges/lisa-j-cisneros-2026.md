## (c) Observable patterns

1. **She is the district's tech-discovery specialist.** Referral docket clusters around AI/copyright
   (Andersen, Mosaic LLM, the Anthropic case that passed through), trade secrets (Auris,
   Rippling/Deel), and ESI edge cases (WeChat on personal devices abroad). Her Andersen orders
   show comfort with unsettled law — she explicitly declined to "prematurely limit" fair-use
   discovery in generative AI.
2. **A copyright/fair-use through-line in consent cases too:** Stebbins (pro se copyright),
   Shafiei (DMCA/fair use), Goldberg (IP-adjacent defamation). She writes full doctrinal analyses
   (the Shafiei order runs the complete four-factor fair-use test) rather than minute-ordering
   them away.
3. **Measured sanctioner.** She'll punish misconduct (Stanley $30k; Kiselev fees) but trims
   overreach (cut Uber's $168k demand; used a tentative order + forced meet-and-confer in
   Rippling/Deel instead of ruling outright).
4. **Pro se case management is hands-on and patient:** the Stebbins docket shows Hague Convention
   service guidance via minute entries, multiple R&Rs, and a case carried for over two years; the
   5/15/26 protective-order ruling gave a pro se plaintiff extra time to find counsel while still
   protecting defense discovery.
5. **Immigration-delay and Social Security appeals are part of the consent mix** (Sareminaeini
   N-400; M.D. benefits remand) — standard magistrate fare, and she ruled against the government
   in both observed instances.
6. **Reputation signal:** Judge Orrick called her Andersen protective-order ruling "very thorough"
   when denying relief from it (8/29/25); Judge Lin adopted her Stebbins R&R "in every respect."
   District judges trust her paper.
7. **Relevance to the portfolio matters:** her Dkt. 11 posture in 3:26-cv-07504-LJC (declining sua
   sponte screening, reserving preclusion to defendants) fits the broader pattern — she makes
   parties carry their own burdens rather than doing their work for them, but she won't hesitate
   to decide a fully-briefed dispositive issue (see Sareminaeini MTD denial).
