## Statute text used here

Checked October 31, 2026.

- **Civil Code § 1786.20**, leginfo.legislature.ca.gov, amended Stats. 2010, ch. 481, effective January 1, 2011.
  - **(a)** Reasonable procedures to avoid § 1786.18 violations and to limit furnishing to § 1786.12 purposes. Users must identify themselves, certify purposes, and make the § 1786.16(a)(4) certifications. The agency keeps a record of the stated purpose. The agency may not furnish a report unless it has a **written agreement** that the report will be used only for § 1786.12 purposes.
  - **(b)** When the agency prepares a report, it shall follow reasonable procedures to assure maximum possible accuracy of the information about the individual. It shall **retain the report for two years** after it is provided.
- **Civil Code § 1786.50**, same site, amended Stats. 2003, ch. 146.
  - **(a)** Actual damages or $10,000, whichever is greater (the $10,000 alternative is unavailable in a class action), plus costs and fees.
  - **(b)** Punitive damages if the court finds the violation grossly negligent or willful.
  - **(c)** No liability where the failure to comply results in a **more favorable** report than if there had been no failure.
- **15 U.S.C. § 1681e(b)**, Cornell LII: when a CRA prepares a consumer report it shall follow reasonable procedures to assure maximum possible accuracy.
- **15 U.S.C. § 1681i(a)**, Cornell LII: reasonable reinvestigation before the end of the 30-day period beginning on receipt of the dispute; written notice of the results not later than five business days after completion, including a statement that the reinvestigation is completed, the revised report, and notice that a procedure description is available on request; the description itself is due not later than 15 days after the consumer requests it. Subsection (a)(7).
- **15 U.S.C. § 1681k(a)**, Cornell LII: a CRA that furnishes an employment report containing public-record information likely to have an adverse effect shall **either** (1) at the time of reporting, notify the consumer that public-record information is being reported and give the name and address of the person to whom it is reported, **or** (2) maintain strict procedures designed to insure that the item is complete and up to date. Up to date, for convictions and similar items, means the current public-record status at the time of the report.

House OLRC pages for the three FCRA sections returned HTTP 404 in this session. Recheck OLRC before filing. The ICRAA text is from the official legislature site.

Case-law application (Spokeo / TransUnion concrete injury, Safeco willfulness, ICRAA preemption under § 1681t) was **not** re-briefed in this session. Flag those as **UNVERIFIED** before a motion for leave. Do not invent citations.

The 2025 Target San Joaquin request uses `BRENDAN` / `NFORBI` and ordinary aliases (`NGWANFORBI`, `NGWA`, `NGWA-NFORBI`). Extract p. 52, lines 1637–1648.
5. The October 28 letter says the updated report removes that AKA. `Closing_Letter_1028.txt` p. 2, lines 20–26. The revised face drops the alias and drops it from the name-searched lists. Extract p. 193, lines 2999–3000; national-criminal names at p. 198, lines 3229–3236.

**What the count must say.** Accurate prepared a consumer report. A name variant that Plaintiff did not supply was labeled as applicant-provided and was used as a search name, including as the principal name sent to the Santa Clara vendor. Reasonable procedures would have kept a first name and a last name from being split and relabeled that way. The report was furnished, or made for furnishing, to Amazon. Plaintiff was injured by the distorted search during the hiring hold.

**What not to allege as established.**

- That the alias caused the July 27 shift to disappear. The alias was in the July 7 searches, so timing allows the theory. Causation is **INFERENCE** until Amazon’s status messages are produced. RFP 19 / Set Two No. 31.
- That "CURRENTLY ON PAROLE" is false. The words are on a December 2, 2020 release line inside a report updated October 14, 2026. Extract p. 19. Currency is a fair discovery subject (RFP 14, Set Two No. 25). It is not yet a pleaded false statement.
- That the three San Joaquin cases were wrongly omitted. Omitting a date-of-birth-only hit can be a reasonable accuracy procedure. Charge text in the JSON is `N/A`. And § 1786.50(c) withholds ICRAA liability if the failure made the report more favorable. Do not build the count on a failure to report worse news.

**Injury.** A false alias in a report prepared for an employer is the concrete harm to plead, tied to the furnishing and to the searches that used the name during the pending hire. Do not rest the count on statutory damages alone. TransUnion-style standing was not re-briefed here (**UNVERIFIED** application).

**Willfulness.** Plead § 1681n and § 1681o in the alternative. The willfulness facts are the day-one use of the scrambled name, the "Provided by Applicant" label, and whatever Request 32 and Interrogatory 19 show about prior notice. Do not plead "Amazon settled the identical § 290.46 claim."

Preemption under 15 U.S.C. § 1681t, as distinct from the § 1681h(e) defense Accurate actually pleaded (Dkt. 21, Eighth Defense), is **UNVERIFIED** here. Plead the state count. Be ready for the defense. Do not drop ICRAA on a memo.

### Hold — § 1681k

The Santa Clara component reports a felony disposition. That is public-record information likely to affect employment, so § 1681k(a) can apply if the report was furnished for employment. Furnishing is what RFP 3 and Interrogatory 2 are for. The October 28 letter says a revised report was forwarded to the employer. That is some evidence of furnishing. It is Accurate’s statement, not Amazon’s receipt log.

The duty is **one or the other**. A notice at the time of reporting satisfies the section even if procedures were not "strict." The repo contains `documents/matter-29/Accurate Background - FCRA 1681k Public Record Notice to Consumer (Amazon HVH, Ref 496623722, Oct 14 2026).pdf`. The report shows Electronic Subject Notification and Electronic Subject Notification - CRA completed on 2026-10-14. Extract pp. 22–23, lines 930–951. This review did not re-open that notice PDF. Do not plead "no notice was sent."

A § 1681k count becomes plausible only if, after the PDF is read, the notice lacks the user’s name and address, or it was not sent at the time of furnishing, **and** Accurate cannot show strict procedures. The "CURRENTLY ON PAROLE" line, if it was not the current public-record status, is evidence on the strict-procedures alternative. It is not enough while the notice alternative may already be met.
