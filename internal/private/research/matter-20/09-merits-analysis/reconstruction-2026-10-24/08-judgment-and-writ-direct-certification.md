## 2. Findings [for the Court to make, strike, or modify]

[The Court finds as follows:]

(a) Respondent California Highway Patrol is a certifying entity and Captain G. Serrato was a certifying official within the meaning of Penal Code section 679.10, subdivisions (a) and (g)(1).

(b) Petitioner submitted a request for Form I-918, Supplement B certification on April 9, 2026, which Captain Serrato denied on April 20, 2026.

(c) On November 1, 2024, respondents' officers intentionally restrained petitioner, against his will, by handcuffing him, transporting him to the Dublin Area Office, and holding him in custody through November 5, 2024.

(d) The restraint lacked lawful authority: the facts known to the officers at the time of arrest did not supply reasonable cause to believe petitioner had driven a vehicle while under the influence of alcohol or a drug, and no circumstance under Vehicle Code section 40300.5 independently authorized the warrantless arrest. [The Court states the facts and circumstances it weighed, including the corrected walk-and-turn record and the section 40300.5 circumstance-by-circumstance findings, in the space below or in an attached statement of decision.]

(e) The restraint therefore constituted false imprisonment under Penal Code section 236, a qualifying crime under Penal Code section 679.10, subdivision (c)(19) [and, in the alternative, unlawful criminal restraint under subdivision (c)(18) / substantially similar activity under subdivision (d), as the Court finds].

(f) Petitioner was directly and proximately harmed by the qualifying criminal activity within the meaning of Penal Code section 679.10, subdivision (o)(2).

(g) Petitioner has been, is being, or is likely to be helpful in the investigation or prosecution of the qualifying criminal activity within the meaning of Penal Code section 679.10, subdivision (g)(1), [with the rebuttable presumption of subdivision (h) applied as the Court finds applicable].

(h) No ground under Penal Code section 679.10, subdivision (k), bars certification on the facts found.

(i) On the findings above, certification is the only lawful outcome. No reasonable alternative remains within respondents' lawful discretion.

## 3. Judgment

IT IS ORDERED, ADJUDGED, AND DECREED that the petition for writ of mandate is GRANTED as to Count One, and a peremptory writ shall issue as stated below. [The April 20, 2026 denial is vacated as a consequence of this judgment.]
