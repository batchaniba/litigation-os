15   I, Brendan Ngehsi Newanforbi, declare:

16   1. I am Petitioner. The statements below concern my own experience, records I prepared or

17   received, and documents I can identify. I do not have personal knowledge of CHP's undisclosed

18   deliberations. I would testify to the facts stated below if called.

19   2. On November 1, 2024, I was in a rented Tesla Model Y on the shoulder of eastbound

20   Interstate 580 near Isabel Avenue in Livermore. I had stopped because I was tired and fell asleep.

21   The officers contacted me there. I described my trip and why I had stopped. My account of

22   tiredness is not an expert opinion about a blood-alcohol concentration.

23   3. I prepared the transcript submitted as Exhibit 5 to my petition by listening to the incident

24   recording and recording the words I heard with timestamps. Bracketed descriptions are my

25   descriptions of visible or audible events, not spoken quotations. I offer the transcript as an aid to

26   comparison with the original recording; where they differ, the recording should control.

27   4. I answered the officers' questions and participated in the eye-movement and time-estimation

28   exercises. I am not qualified to interpret HGN, and I do not claim that my own impression of
