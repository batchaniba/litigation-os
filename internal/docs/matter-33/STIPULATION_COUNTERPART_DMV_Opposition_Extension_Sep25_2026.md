## Recitals and terms

Plaintiff Brendan Ngwa Nforbi, appearing in propria persona, and Defendant Steve Gordon, Director of the California Department of Motor Vehicles, in his official capacity, through his counsel of record, stipulate as follows:

1. Plaintiff's Motion to Strike Defendants' Answer (Dkt. 17) was filed September 9, 2026 and is noticed for hearing on November 12, 2026 at 1:30 p.m. Under Civil L.R. 7-3(a), any opposition was due September 23, 2026. On September 18, 2026, before that date, counsel for Defendant Gordon asked Plaintiff in writing for additional time.

2. Defendant Gordon's time to file and serve an opposition to Dkt. 17 is extended through and including **October 16, 2026**.

3. This extension applies to Defendant Gordon only. It does not apply to Defendant Diana Candelaria or to any other party.

4. Plaintiff's reply in support of Dkt. 17 is due **October 23, 2026**, seven days after the extended opposition date. Civil L.R. 7-3(c).

5. No other date or deadline in this action is modified. The hearing on Dkt. 17 and the Initial Case Management Conference remain set for **November 12, 2026 at 1:30 p.m.** The ADR certification and Rule 26(f) deadline of **October 22, 2026** and the initial disclosures and Joint Case Management Statement deadline of **November 5, 2026** remain as set by Dkt. 4. This stipulation does not extend any time to respond to the First Amended Complaint (Dkt. 6).

6. This is a single extension. No further extension is implied. Any further extension requires a separate written agreement or an order of the Court.

7. Counsel for Defendant Gordon and Plaintiff will meet and confer regarding Dkt. 17 by telephone or videoconference on or before **October 9, 2026**.

8. This stipulation does not waive any claim, defense, objection, or right of any party, including Plaintiff's right to seek default as to any pleading that is not timely answered, and including any argument under Fed. R. Civ. P. 12(h)(1). No party concedes any issue by entering into it. Plaintiff makes no representation as to good cause beyond the reason stated in counsel's declaration.

9. This stipulated request is made under Civil L.R. 6-2. Counsel for Defendant Gordon will submit the declaration Civil L.R. 6-2(a) requires and will promptly file this stipulation with the proposed order below. The proposed order is no broader than these terms. Nothing here is operative until the Court enters the order.

IT IS SO STIPULATED.
