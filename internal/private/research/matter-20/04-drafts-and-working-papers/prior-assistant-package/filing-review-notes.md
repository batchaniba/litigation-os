## Substantive corrections incorporated

- **Privilege log:** The log contains 15 entries, not 10. It includes records dated before and after the April 20 denial; it does not establish the contents of withheld documents or the date of the first video review.
- **Agency-decision nexus:** The existence of an inaccurate report entry does not itself prove Serrato materially relied on it or abused his certification authority. The motion asks for that connection to be established on admitted evidence and allows a trial-setting alternative where necessary.
- **Alternative lawful custody:** The draft addresses both Vehicle Code section 40300.5 and objectively supported alternative authority, including public intoxication, rather than assuming lack of observed driving necessarily invalidates the arrest. See [section 40300.5](https://law.justia.com/codes/california/code-veh/division-17/chapter-2/article-1/section-40300-5/) and [People v. Wolterman](https://law.justia.com/cases/california/court-of-appeal/4th/11/supp15.html).
- **PAS refusal:** Voluntary testing does not mean refusal must be excluded categorically from reasonable-cause analysis. The draft separates that issue from cooperation concerning the alleged qualifying crime. See [Espinoza v. Shiomoto](https://law.justia.com/cases/california/court-of-appeal/2017/e064252a.html) and [Penal Code section 679.10(k)(3)(H)](https://leginfo.legislature.ca.gov/faces/codes_displaySection.xhtml?lawCode=PEN&sectionNum=679.10.).
- **Release date and certificate:** The new declaration omits a disputed precise release date. Neither the certificate nor the absence of a complaint is treated as a judicial adjudication of probable cause; [section 851.6](https://leginfo.legislature.ca.gov/faces/codes_displaySection.xhtml?lawCode=PEN&sectionNum=851.6.) provides for certificates in specified release/no-pleading circumstances.
- **Remedy:** Remand is requested only upon an actual material abuse or legal error, not merely to obtain a fuller explanation. The conditional direct-certification request tracks the “only one choice” limitation in [California Correctional Supervisors Organization](https://law.justia.com/cases/california/court-of-appeal/4th/96/824.html).

## Evaluation of the present showing

The walk-and-turn discrepancy is concrete enough to test against the native recording and should not have been dismissed categorically as incapable of supporting any error. It remains different from completed proof that the whole arrest lacked lawful authority or that the certification decision was arbitrary. That distinction is why this draft makes a merits case, confronts CHP's strongest unfavorable evidence, and preserves a focused factual hearing rather than promising an inevitable certification order.
