**Judge test:** Granting direct certification now would require findings not yet established by admitted authentic evidence: criminally **unlawful** restraint, direct victimhood, assistance and a unique lawful decision. A viable writ of *reconsideration* requires an identified statutory or materially defective factual predicate—not just a terse unfavorable denial. **Opposition test:** The defense need not rely solely on roadway obstruction because *Schofield* construes the **evidence-dissipation** exception of Vehicle Code §40300.5(e). The no-observed-driving argument cannot defeat §40300.5 by itself. The outcome may turn on which impairment facts officers actually knew and whether corrected reports remove probable cause. Our earlier child branch overemphasized the roadway-obstruction argument; the **12-material-improvement-review** properly caught this. Our integrated brief retains the test but no longer presents it as presumptively dispositive. Also the 0.073±0.004 g% result was **later** and not a scientific retrograde calculation.

**Appellate test:** Writ order A may require certification only if §679.10 predicates are adjudicated and the relief is within operative pleadings/adequate notice. Remand order B cannot assume Serrato personally relied on the disputed walk-and-turn statement and must specify a proved actual error. *CV Amalgamated* supports restoring process or rescoring under identified lawful criteria, **not** guaranteed issuance of the ultimate license.
