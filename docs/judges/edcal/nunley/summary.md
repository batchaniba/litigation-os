## Rules (S2)
- **Discovery** goes to the MJ. Put "DISCOVERY MATTER" in the caption. Send no courtesy copies to Nunley.
  - Review of an MJ ruling is under L.R. 303(c), within 14 days, on a clearly-erroneous or contrary-to-law standard.
  - The MJ cannot change the scheduling order.
- **No paper courtesy copies** unless ordered.
- Memoranda are limited to 20 pages and replies to 10. Page increases must be requested 14 days before filing.
- Font: Times New Roman 12 or Courier 10.
- Cite the specific page. For unpublished cases, Westlaw cites are preferred.
- Pre-filing meet and confer applies where the parties are represented. Counsel should agree to curative amendments.
- No supplemental briefs without leave.
- Telephonic appearance: file a request and a proposed order 7 court days before the hearing, and email the order to tlnorders@caed.uscourts.gov.
- Proposed orders go in Word to tlnorders@, with "proposed order" and the case number in the subject line.
- **Ex parte** applications are usually submitted without a hearing.
  - The filer must notify the opposing party first and file an affidavit covering need, inability to stipulate, and why the motion can't be noticed.
  - Opposition is due in 48 hours, or 72 hours if the application is filed on a Friday.
- TROs: no ruling for at least 24 hours after service.
- **A stipulation alone is not good cause.** Witness or counsel unavailability usually isn't either.
- A Joint Notice of Trial Readiness is due 30 days after the last dispositive ruling.
- Removed cases must refile all state papers with the notice of removal.
- **No ex parte contact with chambers.** Contact the CRD only.
