## Documents to locate

- Recent tax returns and supporting schedules.
- Recent pay statements and employment benefit summaries.
- Bank, credit-card, loan, retirement, brokerage, and digital-asset records.
- Deeds, leases, titles, insurance policies, and beneficiary designations.
- Business formation, ownership, accounting, and tax records.
- Settlement agreements, judgments, liens, and pending-claim records.
- Records of significant gifts, inheritances, premarital property, or tracing.

## Controls

- Preserve ordinary records.
- Do not drain, hide, retitle, encumber, or destroy assets.
- Do not access an account without authorization.
- Record unusual transactions factually and preserve the source.
- Ask counsel before changing beneficiaries, insurance, residence, or major
  financial arrangements when rights may be affected.
