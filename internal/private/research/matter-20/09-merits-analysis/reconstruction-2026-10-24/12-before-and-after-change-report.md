# 12 — BEFORE-AND-AFTER CHANGE REPORT

**Question:** Why is this replacement package materially better than the current 30-page working packet — and what did it deliberately not do?

- The walk-and-turn conflict as the lead fact, the PAS/chemical distinction, the urine-cup/can distinction, and the § 851.6 date discipline.

## 2. MATERIAL IMPROVEMENTS (why the replacement is better)

1. **A decision test the judge can use.** The working packet argued toward relief; the replacement starts from the findings the Court must make (00-§ 1, 01-matrix) and ties every argument to a finding. A judge can translate the replacement into an order; the working packet required the judge to do that translation.
2. **A stage analysis instead of a misconduct narrative.** The six-stage restraint analysis (MPA § II) identifies the strongest theory (arrest + custody) and eliminates the weakest (search-as-false-imprisonment) by reasoning, not by assertion. The working packet's general misconduct framing invited the opposition's best response; the replacement's "compulsory restraint without lawful authority" framing does not.
3. **A provable Serrato defect instead of unproved "facial errors."** The working packet's successors claimed three facial errors; the 12-review correctly rejected them as unproved, and this reconstruction does not revive them. The replacement's defect — no reviewable reasoning + a materially inaccurate agency account — is provable on the decision's face and the Form 202, without any claim about Serrato's subjective knowledge. That is a narrower defect and a stronger one.
4. **Adverse authority confronted, not ignored.** Troppman (reasonable-cause belief suffices), Schofield (§ 40300.5(e) dissipation), and Espinoza (odor + demeanor totality) are the authorities CHP will cite. The working packet's Mercer-centered analysis did not answer them. The replacement distinguishes each on its proper ground and concedes what is weak (notably (e) if reasonable cause is found). A brief that answers the opposition's best cases is harder to deny than one that pretends they do not exist.
5. **Corrections that protect credibility.** "Verified Answer" → Answer to Verified Petition (counsel-signed; party admissions, not sworn testimony). Privilege log 15 entries (not 10). AGO pins refined (000031/000032/000033/000034). Western States pinpoint resolved (p. 576 sentence verified verbatim in this reconstruction). Common Cause pinpoint corrected to p. 442. Saleeby paraphrase unquoted. Sanders deleted paragraph never quoted. § 679.10(i)(2) (not (g)) for denial content; (k)(3)(E) vs. (H) kept distinct. Each correction removes a ground on which the opposition could impeach the brief's accuracy.
6. **Conditional status stated where the record is conditional.** The transcript version problem, the native-video gap, the RFP No. 8 gap, and the roadside-geometry gap are stated in the brief itself, with the completion step identified — rather than papered over with adjectives. A brief that discloses its own conditions is more grantable than one the opposition exposes.
7. **Two independently usable judgments + a narrow procedural order.** The working packet's single-judgment structure (direct-certification-only in the prior draft) forced an all-or-nothing choice. The replacement's three tiers (direct / remand / focused proceeding) let the Court grant what the record supports without denying the motion. The remand judgment binds respondents to adjudicated findings and statutory criteria without dictating an outcome the Court has not adjudicated.
8. **A declaration that does not invent testimony.** The replacement declaration contains only record-backed paragraphs plus bracketed confirmation prompts. Prior drafts' invented recollections (memory, preparation, POST communications, spelling, consent) are removed. The petitioner executes only what he can truthfully state.
9. **The federal order omitted.** The June 29, 2026 screening order is assumed-true material from a different case. Whitman's own order is stronger. Omitting it removes a full page of qualified, distracting argument and a live-status risk. (If the petitioner directs inclusion, one qualified paragraph is the maximum.)
10. **A filing audit that prevents an unfilable packet from being filed.** The working packet could not be filed as a merits-ready submission (unconfirmed hearing, no briefing order, blank execution, unverified KeyCite). The replacement does not pretend otherwise: product 11 lists every open item, and product 00's verdict is "not ready to file" until they close.
