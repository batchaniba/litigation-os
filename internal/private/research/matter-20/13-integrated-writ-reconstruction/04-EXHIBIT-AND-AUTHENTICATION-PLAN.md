## Rules and service
- [Cal. Rules Court 3.1110](https://courts.ca.gov/cms/rules/index/three/rule3_1110): first-page hearing details, captions and exhibit labeling.
- [Rule 3.1113](https://courts.ca.gov/cms/rules/index/three/rule3_1113): 15-page ordinary MPA maximum; if memorandum *exceeds 10 pages* include table of contents and authorities; pin cite exhibit and page; proposed orders served/lodged **separately**.
- [Rule 3.1300](https://courts.ca.gov/cms/rules/index/three/rule3_1300): unless court orders otherwise, §1005 moving papers schedule applies; POS filed no later than five court days before hearing. Compute later from actual court order, excluding holidays and service adjustments.
- **DO NOT FILL** proof of service for a nonparty before service occurs. For e-service confirm consent or other authority, recipient/operative email and evidence of transmission.
- The court's actual scheduling order controls a historical reservation or tentative.
- Verify hearing judge, location, date and trial setting before a real filing. These drafts intentionally keep schedule verification as a prefiling condition.
- Any federal June29 screening order is *assumed-true preliminary review*, not merits adjudication; omit from replacement MPA unless shown to add legally material value; unlike R01 it is not binding on Whitman.
- No new damages, §1983, revived reason-giving / helpfulness claim, criminal perjury accusations, or orders directing immigration status.

## Additional integration gate: §40300.5(e) / source-version priority

The critical updated authority is *People v. Schofield* (2001) 90 Cal.App.4th 968, 973–975 (metabolic dissipation within §40300.5(e)), recognized in *Troppman v. Valverde* (2007) 40 Cal.4th 1121, 1137 fn. 11. A stationary shoulder vehicle and no officer-observed movement do **not** negate all DUI warrantless-arrest grounds. The motion must test whether **the contemporaneous and reliable observations** supplied reasonable cause for DUI; the factual discrepancy cannot stand alone.
