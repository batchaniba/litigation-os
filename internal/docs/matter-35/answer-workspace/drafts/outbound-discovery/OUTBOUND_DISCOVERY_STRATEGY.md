# Matter 35 — Outbound Discovery Strategy

## Executive recommendation

Serve the preservation demand immediately. Serve one complete, defendant-specific first wave on each corporate defendant after the official DISC-001 forms and RFP production dates are completed. Keep the sets synchronized so ownership, control and intercompany allocation cannot become an excuse for nonproduction.

## First-wave objectives

1. **Preserve the best evidence.** Native surveillance footage, exports, clips, stills, metadata and audit logs are the highest-value evidence and the most vulnerable to routine overwrite.
2. **Identify the human witnesses.** Obtain DOE 1's name, employer, title and supervisor; identify the cashier, manager on duty, loss-prevention supervisor, Rachel De la Cruz and every video custodian.
3. **Pin down the asserted basis for the encounter.** Force Ross to state every observation and objective fact it contends supported suspicion, then identify the witnesses and documents supporting that position.
4. **Lock useful admissions.** The RFAs isolate the uncontested facts: no alarm, no search, no receipt request, no police, no recovered merchandise, no identified unpaid Ross item and the beverage as the sole merchandise-related basis for the interaction.
5. **Test the detention dispute.** Positioning at the exit, words used, duration and whether Plaintiff was free to leave are developed through paired RFAs, interrogatories, video requests and layout evidence.
6. **Develop entity responsibility.** Separate sets require each Ross entity to state its role in store operation, employment, surveillance control, policy creation and record custody.
7. **Build the policy and notice record.** Obtain the merchant-detention, customer-questioning, outside-beverage, receipt-check, anti-discrimination, de-escalation and training materials applicable on August 25, 2026.

## Serve now

- Standalone evidence-preservation demand.
- Requests for Admission, Set One: 34 per defendant.
- Current Judicial Council Form Interrogatories—General, Set One, checking 1.1, 4.1, 4.2, 12.1–12.7, 13.1–13.2, 14.1–14.2, 15.1 and 17.1.
- Special Interrogatories, Set One: 35 per defendant.
- Requests for Production, Set One: 40 per defendant.

Form Interrogatory 15.1 reaches each denial and affirmative defense. Form Interrogatory 17.1 makes every non-unqualified RFA response identify the supporting facts, witnesses and documents. Together they convert a bare denial into a discoverable proof map.

## Follow-up after responses

1. Build a denial chart pairing each RFA response with its Form Interrogatory 17.1 facts, witnesses and documents.
2. Compare the two corporate defendants' answers for inconsistent claims about employer identity, store operation, custody and control.
3. Amend DOE 1's true name promptly after identification and serve targeted individual discovery.
4. Notice focused depositions of DOE 1, the cashier, the manager on duty, Rachel De la Cruz and the surveillance-system custodian.
5. Use a corporate-person-most-qualified deposition for surveillance architecture and retention, store operation, loss-prevention policy, training, and the factual basis for pleaded defenses.
6. Serve narrow subpoenas on any third-party surveillance or security vendor identified in the responses.
7. Serve a second RFA set converting produced video, policies and business records into authenticity and fact admissions.
8. Meet and confer request-by-request on evasive responses, then move to compel only the evidence that materially advances liability, witness identification or preservation/spoliation issues.
