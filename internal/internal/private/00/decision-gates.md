# Matter 0 decision gates

Advance only when the present gate has been evaluated. A bad week is not by
itself a gate transition.

## Gate 0 — Monitor without escalation

Use when no immediate filing, safety, housing, or asset-preservation issue
requires action.

Outputs:

- verified chronology;
- top three relationship problems;
- top three desired outcomes;
- short list of unknowns;
- a date for reassessment.

## Gate 1 — Repair plan

Use when both spouses may still participate in repair.

Define:

- specific behaviors each person is asking to change;
- what successful change looks like;
- communication rules during conflict;
- counseling, pastoral, or mediation support if mutually acceptable;
- a 30-, 60-, or 90-day review point;
- conditions that would end or modify the repair period.

A repair plan is not an evidentiary trap or a forced confession.

## Gate 2 — Confidential professional consultation

Use before major moves involving residence, money, children, immigration,
insurance, taxes, business interests, or filing.

Prepare a short, neutral fact packet and questions. Do not send the entire
portfolio. Obtain advice about jurisdiction, characterization of property and
debt, support, disclosure duties, temporary orders, settlement process, and the
effect of any unusual facts actually present.

## Gate 3 — Structured separation

Use when continued cohabitation or informal ambiguity is no longer workable but
a negotiated structure remains possible.

Resolve in writing where appropriate:

- housing and access;
- ordinary expenses and debt payments;
- possession and use of vehicles and property;
- communication channels;
- account and record preservation;
- children or dependents, if any;
- insurance and emergency contacts;
- a mediation or counsel timetable.

## Gate 4 — Mediation or negotiated dissolution

Use when the marriage will likely end and both sides can exchange information
and negotiate safely.

Build an issue list, not an accusation list. Price the cost and time of
litigation against the actual disputed value. Preserve enough information to
verify any settlement.

## Gate 5 — Litigation

Use only after identifying the filing objective, court, jurisdiction,
date-of-separation position, immediate-relief needs, disclosure obligations,
service plan, and settlement off-ramp.

Before filing, create a separate operative family-law matter with its real
caption and case number. Matter 0 remains the private strategy and history
workspace; it does not become a pleading.
