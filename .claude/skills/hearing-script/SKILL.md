# Zoom / court speaking notes

The user appears in propria persona, often on Zoom. The camera is on his face.

A "script" is a **read-aloud with two typefaces**:

- **Bold** = stage direction. He does not speak it. Every bold line starts
  with `(don't say)` so it cannot be mistaken for dialogue.
- Regular type = the only words that leave his mouth. Complete sentences.
  Sounds like him, not like a brief.

The failure mode on August 28 was headings he could accidentally read
("If they say…") in the same type as the speech.

## Default deliverable

When he asks for a script, **render it in the chat** in that two-typeface
form. One sitting. No PDF unless he asks.

If the hearing is live or about to start: this card only. Stop.

A longer off-camera memo exists only if he asks for homework, labeled
`DO NOT OPEN ON CAMERA`.

## How to write it

**Bold / don't-say lines**

- Always begin `(don't say)`.
- Telegram. Not a sentence he would ever say in court.
- Tell him when to look at the camera, when to skip a block, what the
  other side is doing. Never "If they say X."

**Spoken lines**

- Short complete sentences. First person. His voice.
- Smart, not stiff. No "the Attorney General asks the Court to treat…"
- No string cites. One case name only if he already knows it cold.
- Opening: at most four spoken sentences, then a skip-block.
- Skip-blocks after that, each preceded by a bold `(don't say)` gate.
- Close: one spoken ask.

Keep it to what fits on one screen without hunting. If he has to scroll
to find the matching paragraph, cut it.

## Worked example — Matter 20 demurrer, August 28, 2026

Retrospective. Hearing already held. Render in chat exactly like this.

**(don't say — look at the camera, then the next three lines)**
I submit on the Court's tentative as to the first and fourth causes of action.
I'm not asking you to order the Form B signed today.
I ask that those two counts be overruled and that you set an answer date.

**(don't say — skip unless they fight count 1)**
This is a demurrer. You don't choose between the dashcam and the arrest report today.
Exhibit 5 and Exhibit 6 conflict. Exhibit 7 is CHP's own 851.6 certificate — insufficient grounds for a complaint.
An empty can and a declined PAS test don't establish DUI as a matter of law.

**(don't say — skip unless they fight count 4)**
Count four is declaratory relief under section 1060.
I'm asking how 679.10 applies to this still-pending request, including subdivision (k) — no charge and no prosecution required.
I'm not asking you to declare that the stop was a qualifying crime. Paragraph 94 says that.
I don't have to elect remedies on a demurrer.

**(don't say — skip unless they raise Serrato, Paul, or Dutra)**
Captain Serrato signed the April 20 denial. He's a proper respondent.
Lieutenant Paul processed the request. Sergeant Dutra was the assigned investigator.
If that's thin as to Paul or Dutra, I ask leave to amend — not dismissal without leave.

**(don't say — skip unless the Court asks about two and three)**
I submit on the second and third causes of action.

**(don't say — close, look at the camera)**
I ask the Court to adopt the tentative on one and four, and set an answer date. The CMC is September 2.

## Chat while a hearing is live

Two or three spoken sentences in regular type. One bold `(don't say)` line
if a skip matters. Stop.

## After the hearing

Do not invent the ruling. Retire the calendar entry as held. Record "minute
order not yet in hand" until he supplies the order or a specific holding.
