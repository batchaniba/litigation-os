
# Matter 12 — E.D. Cal. Federalization Strategy and Filing Gates

## Objective

Build a federal-ready Matter 12 theory without surrendering the February 13, 2026 San Joaquin filing date.

The federal draft is **not yet a substitute for the pending state action**.

## Proposed architecture

### Federal anchor
Civil RICO, 18 U.S.C. § 1962(c), against Diamond only unless later evidence supports additional RICO persons.

### State claims carried under supplemental jurisdiction
- UVTA — Civ. Code § 3439.04(a)(1)

- breach of contract for sale

- UCL

## Why UVTA is presently stronger than RICO

The public record now supplies objective continuity facts:
- same VIN;
- same mileage;
- same stock number;
- same price;
- same Turlock address;
- same phone;
- web migration;
- overlapping personnel;
- Diamond equipment at Alpine's Bakersfield location;
- litigation/creditor pressure during the transition.

But UVTA still needs:
- actual transfer;
- transfer date;
- consideration;
- ownership/control;
- solvency.

RICO additionally needs:
- particularized predicates;
- pattern/continuity;
- distinct enterprise;
- operation/management;
- proximate injury.

## RICO filing threshold

Do not file Count One until at least two predicates are stated with:
- date;
- actor;
- medium;
- exact misrepresentation or concealment;
- role of each defendant;
- mail/wire nexus;
- money/property induced;
- documentary support.

Public BBB complaints are witness leads, not predicates.

## Person / enterprise architecture

Do not make Diamond both the sole RICO person and sole enterprise.

Working model:
- RICO person: Diamond;
- enterprise: association-in-fact involving Diamond plus other proven participants in the continuing truck-dealer operation.

Do not name:
- Alpine as a RICO person;
- Diamond Premier Services;
- individual Osmani family members;

without actor-specific conduct.

- no Rule 9(b) predicate detail;
- person and enterprise not distinct;
- no operation/management;
- RICO limitations;
- no proximate causation;

- Alpine paid fair value;
- Diamond remains available, weakening mere-continuation theory;
- declaratory count duplicative;

- parallel state action / abstention.
