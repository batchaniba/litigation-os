### Judgment and directions
5. Judgment is granted on First Cause of Action **to the limited extent stated herein**. The April 20 refusal is vacated and set aside.

6. A peremptory writ shall direct CHP, through its authorized certifying official, within **30 calendar days of service of the writ**, to reconsider the April 9 application using the correct standards of section 679.10 and **without repeating the judicially determined error** identified in Findings 3–4. In applying factual evidence, it shall honor the Court's actually decided facts and may evaluate other matters not foreclosed by those findings according to law.

7. CHP shall separately assess qualifying victimhood and statutory helpfulness under subdivisions (g) and (h), and must not demand charges, prosecution or conviction contrary to subdivision (k)(1), or rely on a ground prohibited by subdivision (k)(3). If certification is again denied, its written notice shall comply with subdivision (i)(2). This judgment does not require a prosecution or criminal charge against any officer.

8. Within **45 calendar days** after service of writ CHP shall file and serve a verified return stating the steps taken and including the reconsideration determination or completed certification, subject to lawful privacy protections.

9. The Court retains jurisdiction to determine writ compliance; it reserves no authority to decide federal immigration status and grants no relief on dismissed counts or damages claims. Costs, if any, upon proper application.
