## Chambers and calendar (S1, S2)
- Courtroom 7, 14th floor. CRD Gabriel Michel, gmichel@caed.uscourts.gov, (916) 930-4114.
- Civil motions are heard on designated Thursdays at 1:30 pm. Email the CRD for available dates.
- Oral argument is in person. Remote requests are disfavored and are due 1 week ahead. The court may submit a motion under L.R. 230(g) by minute order.

## Briefing and motion rules (S2)
- Briefs are limited to 25 pages and replies to 15 pages, in 12-pt type. Sans-serif fonts are encouraged.
- A meet-and-confer certification is required, or the motion is summarily denied. The text is addressed to counsel.
- Summary judgment requires joint or separate statements of facts. Cross-motions use four briefs (25/35/20/10 pages).
- No proposed orders except for a TRO or preliminary injunction. Send those to DJCorders@caed.uscourts.gov.
- Ex parte applications are submitted without a hearing. Contact the CRD first.
- Sealing: email DJCorders, then the approved document goes to ApprovedSealed@.
- Extensions and continuances:
  - A request must give "specific, concrete reasons supporting good cause." Saying an extension "will promote settlement" is not enough.
  - Stipulations that continue dispositive-motion or trial dates are disfavored.

## Case management (S3)
- The plaintiff must serve within 90 days.
- A Rule 26(f) joint status report is due within 60 days of service or removal, covering items a–p, including § 636(c) consent and VDRP.
