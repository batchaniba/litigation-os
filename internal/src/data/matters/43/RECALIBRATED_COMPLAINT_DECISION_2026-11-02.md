## Important corrections to competing analyses
- **DeepSeek on the private right:** INCORRECT. Section 290.46(j)(4)(A) expressly imposes civil damages against the forbidden **user**. *Mendoza v. ADP Screening & Selection Services*, 182 Cal.App.4th 1644 (2010), holds the **screening company** merely republishing the registry data was not the statutory employer-style user; it states a prospective employer is the proper target if it actually uses the data. The case does not eliminate the private employer claim.
- **Fair Chance Count NOW:** Not in either initial state filing variant. A FEHA/Gov. Code §12952 cause generally requires CRD administrative process/right-to-sue; no such notice is evidenced. Also the stated adverse reason is **alcohol-related administrative MVR history**, not clearly a criminal conviction. The blank response date and from-letter-date language are powerful *potential* procedural facts **only if conviction-history procedures apply**; employer may have made an independent driving-eligibility judgment. *McInerney v. Regents*, B339467 (filed Sept. 18, published Oct. 13, 2026), restricts procedural-damages proof; omission of a written assessment is not automatically a violation. Preserve for charge/amendment after jurisdictional prerequisites and outcome.
- **Non-compete clause:** An accepted offer's mandatory “Employee Confidentiality & Non-Competition” phrase warrants attention. B&P Code §§16600 and 16600.5(c),(e) clearly protect **prospective employees** and authorize injunction/actual damages and fees. But without the operative covenant, do NOT allege that the named document contains an actually void restraint; a lawful confidentiality-only agreement would not qualify. Reserve count until covenant is obtained.
- **Labor Code §432.7:** An **administrative DMV suspension** is not itself an arrest record or criminal conviction. An adverse decision using it is not automatically an unlawful use of an arrest record. Do not allege the refusal necessarily represents an arrest *not followed by any conviction* without independent corroboration.
- **NSOPW:** DOJ says its public website queries participating state public registries and NSOR is restricted to authorized criminal justice entities; it does **not** conclusively show which vendor feed or lawful independent source produced HireRight's entry.
- **Hertz Global Holdings:** ADP WOTC references alone cannot prove direct employment control. Name The Hertz Corporation as offeror/customer; amend only when specific joint-employer or agency facts arise.
- **FCRA:** No evidenced standalone federal FCRA disclosure defect or failure to furnish report and summary before preliminary action. Exclude federal count; do not manufacture subject-matter jurisdiction.
- **Damages:** No verified final offer withdrawal or missed start. No assumed guaranteed 40-hour week or full-year tenure; no multiplication of statutory claims by search subrequest. ICRAA §1786.50 individual remedy is greater of actual damage or $10k *for qualifying liability*, not $10k per page. §290.46 relief follows its distinct elements and remedy structure.
- **Timing:** Vendor furnished MVR on Oct. 23 but adverse letter came Nov. 2 shortly after full report. Useful circumstantial sequence, **not proof** of registry causation. The notice's date-from-letter conflicts with California's receipt-based regulation for conviction notices. **Nov. 5 is an early response target; Nov. 9 is not asserted to be the last legal response date.**
