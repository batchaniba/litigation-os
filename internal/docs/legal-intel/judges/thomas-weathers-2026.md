## 4. Behavioral patterns — VERIFIED unless noted

- **Brand-new civil judge.** First indexed civil tentative ruling September 2, 2026 —
  roughly four weeks after taking Dept. 20. There is no multi-year track record to
  mine; the sample is 21 rulings over 16 days.
- **No demurrer record yet.** Zero demurrers among the 21 indexed rulings. The central
  Matter 27 question (sustain WOLA vs. leave to amend) cannot be answered from his
  rulings — only approached through adjacent signals.
- **Pleading-stage liberality (Slaughter).** At the pleading stage he reads allegations
  in the pleader's favor and defers fact development to discovery. A demurrer that
  asks him to weigh facts or draw inferences against the complaint runs against this
  grain.
- **Procedural rigor (Ganoe/Farrise, Wilson).** Strict compliance with service statutes;
  discovery cut-offs enforced even when the equities favor the movant. Sloppy paper
  loses regardless of sympathy.
- **High continuance rate — 38%.** The single most distinctive aggregate stat. He
  continues matters (MSJ/MSA, discovery, quash, petitions) rather than ruling on an
  incomplete record. Expect continuances, not snap denials, when briefing is thin.
- **Trial-date protection (Bryant).** Denies stays that would idle a case; prejudice
  from delay (evidence loss, costs, lost settlement opportunities) is credited.
- **Case-management enforcement (Adrow).** Non-compliance with his orders draws a
  must-appear. His CMCs issue standard pretrial/trial setting orders (*Portillo*,
  *Hunter*, *Santa Isabel* — all 9/8/2026: "court will issue its standard
  pretrial/trial setting order").
- **Defaults on the papers (Infinity Insurance).** Reviews the §585 package as
  submitted; no appearance theater.
- **Fee awards follow the contract (Gamos).** Granted the full $48,766.21 lodestar to
  prevailing defendants; equitable hardship arguments rejected with controlling
  authority.
- **Thorough, heavily cited writing.** The Slaughter, Ganoe, and Gamos tentatives each
  run multi-page with structured Background / Legal Standard / Discussion / Conclusion
  sections and 8–13 hyperlinked authorities (CourtListener, leginfo). This is an
  analytically careful writer, not a one-paragraph minute-order judge.
- **Tentative-ruling mechanics, Dept. 20 (from the rulings themselves):** tentative
  posts approximately two court days before the hearing (e.g., 9/01 for a 9/03
  hearing; 9/15 for 9/17); to contest, notify the court and all parties **no later
  than 4:00 PM one court day before** the hearing through **both** the eCourt portal
  ("Click to Contest this Ruling") **and** email to the department clerk; contested
  hearings are conducted via remote video unless the court requires in-person
  appearance. Uncontested tentatives become the order.

   CIV-141 auto-extension) will be among the first demurrers Weathers ever decides —
   there is no Weathers demurrer record to game. The two adjacent signals point in

   pleading-stage liberality helps the complaint survive a pleading attack, while the

2. **If the demurrer is sustained, expect leave to amend — but that is California
   default practice, not a Weathers data point.** Nothing in the 21-ruling sample
   shows him sustaining without leave; nothing shows him granting it either. Do not
   over-read the absence.
3. **A continuance is the modal outcome, not a tell.** With a 38% continuance rate,
   if the demurrer hearing is continued for supplemental briefing, that is his normal
   operating pattern — not a signal about the merits.
4. **His professional biography is the most plaintiff-sympathetic in the portfolio.**
   Nine years as a named partner at a civil-rights/employment plaintiff-side firm,
   then nine years as a solo. He has sat on the plaintiff's side of the table in
   employment cases and has litigated without institutional backing. DIRECTIONAL only
   — but as between Schwartz (AG's office, regulatory) and Weathers (plaintiff-side
   employment/civil-rights, solo), the professional-sympathy vector points toward
   Aliko. It does not override the procedural-rigor vector: sympathy for the position
   does not excuse sloppy procedure in his courtroom (see Wilson).
5. **Default practice is efficient and paper-based.** Matter 27's default was vacated
   by stipulation, so this is contingency intel — but if default ever re-enters the
   picture, expect the §585 package to be reviewed on submission, with the court
   policing CRC 3.110 timelines rather than letting them drift.
6. **The December 17 CMC will set the trial track.** Expect a standard pretrial/trial
   setting order and tight enforcement thereafter (Adrow). Trial dates, once set,
   will be protected (Bryant).
7. **Watch the tentative two court days before any hearing; contest by 4:00 PM the
   court day before via eCourt *and* email.** The tentative is genuinely provisional
