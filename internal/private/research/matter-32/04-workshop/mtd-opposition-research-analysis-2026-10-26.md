## 1. Executive strategy and recommended 25-page architecture

### Core theory
Treat ECF 22 as an attempt to collapse four temporally distinct intrusions into one presumed-justified encounter. Force claim-by-claim, time-specific analysis: (1) passenger-door opening; (2) cup/vehicle search; (3) investigatory detention / arrest; (4) blood-draw warrant / judicial deception. At Rule 12, disputed police-report facts challenged by specific FAC allegations are not automatically true merely because reports are attached or identified (Khoja; Grier n.1).

### Disposition ask (recommended)
1. Deny dismissal of Fourth Amendment door-entry, search, arrest, and judicial-deception theories as pleaded, or at minimum deny with leave to amend on identified defects.
2. Narrow fabrication: preserve Fourth Amendment warrant/blood-draw theories; do not concede that "no charges" categorically ends every liberty-deprivation theory without research and amendment — but do not overclaim a freestanding Due Process win on Model Instr. 9.38 alone.
3. Retaliation: preserve only if protected speech + but-for sequence can be particularized; otherwise seek leave to amend or narrow.
4. QI: oppose categorical dismissal; insist on conduct-specific analysis at pleading stage (Keates).
5. Futility: oppose; propose concrete amendments; distinguish venue/voluntary-dismissal history from merits adjudication.
6. Strike: oppose Rule 12(f) damages strike under Whittlestone; **concede** pro se §1988 fees for plaintiff's own time (Kay).
7. **Concede/correct** beverage-checkbox characterization; amend to breath-odor field.
