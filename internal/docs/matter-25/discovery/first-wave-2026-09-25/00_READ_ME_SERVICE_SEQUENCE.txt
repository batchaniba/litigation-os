3. WHAT EACH SET IS DESIGNED TO ESTABLISH

Interrogatories: lock down the exact alleged misrepresentation, when FedEx first knew it, who made the decision, every asserted reason, prior FedEx applications and hires, registry access, and the factual bases of the seventh and eighth affirmative defenses.

Requests for admission: establish the report and registry trail, separate the contemporaneous adverse-action reason from the later misrepresentation theory, and narrow disputes over prior FedEx employment and the claimed person-at-risk justification.

Requests for production: obtain the native application and audit history, all prior applications and hiring records, the complete First Advantage report and access logs, decision codes, communications, screening rules, job details, notices, preservation materials, and defense-supporting documents.

Rule 45 subpoena: obtain First Advantage's copy of the report, underlying registry/source data, order configuration, audit logs, FedEx communications, notices, matching/reinvestigation records, and the complete consumer file.
