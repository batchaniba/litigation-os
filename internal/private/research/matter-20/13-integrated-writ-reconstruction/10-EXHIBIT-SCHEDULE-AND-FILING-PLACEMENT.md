## 6. Actual filing / lodging logistics and rules checked (2026)
- [CRC 3.1110(a),(b),(f)](https://courts.ca.gov/cms/rules/index/three/rule3_1110): exhibit **index** listing identification and PDF page number; document pagination; electronic exhibit bookmarking (self-represented litigant is excepted from mandatory bookmarks under the text, but strongly recommended); clean label and page-by-page crosswalk.
- [CRC 3.1113(k),(l),(m)](https://courts.ca.gov/cms/rules/index/three/rule3_1113): memorandum **exhibit letter and specific page**, not merely “see Exhibit D”; RJN **separate**; proposed orders/judgments **lodged and served separately**, not attached to the motion.
- [CRC 3.1306(a)–(c)](https://courts.ca.gov/cms/rules/index/three/rule3_1306): ordinary motion evidence by declarations or judicial notice absent court order for testimony; separate advance written statement for proposed oral evidence; court file notice needs exact document and electronically accessible file or clerk arrangement.
- [Alameda Superior Court Civil eFiling](https://www.alameda.courts.ca.gov/online-services/e-filing/civil-e-filing): documents in eCourt may be publicly accessible; **redact protected identifiers** (CRC 1.201); confirm current EFSP size, file naming, media acceptance and local rules before transmission. Do not supply a public-access GitHub link to sensitive incident sources in court papers.
- For actual evidentiary hearing/trial exhibit **admission**, marking/filing a proposed exhibit is not itself admission. Keep demonstratives and originals distinctly identified; obtain an order authorizing native media presentation if necessary.
- **No original source bytes, root manifest, filing status, service or media files were modified** to create this register.
