## How the labels work

| Label | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **Publish** | Recommended first wave. Still needs your written yes on question 1 or 2, then review of each file. |
| **Ask Brendan** | A public filing exists, but the subject, an unfavorable order, a settlement, or a document-level problem needs your call. |
| **Hold** | Do not publish on the present facts (unfiled, no public PDF, sealed/confidential risk, other people's cases, or a record that would mainly surface sensitive history). |

Document-level holds that apply **even on a Publish matter**:

- Anything labeled draft, unfiled, or intake
- Fee-waiver applications and orders
- Change-of-address notices
- Background-check reports and vendor exhibits
- Protective orders
- Settlement numbers, payment watches, and negotiation text
- Other parties' PACER pulls (for example the *Lerma* / *Carrillo* / *Walker* papers stored under Matter 29)
- Receipts, One Legal confirmations, and blank forms
- Personal identifiers (phone, email, SSN, date of birth, license numbers)

Court captions in this portfolio use several name forms (`Newanforbi`, `Ngwa Nforbi`, `Nforbi`, `Brendan Ngwa Nforbi`). Titles always add the search name **Brendan Ngehsi Newanforbi** next to the caption as filed. That is the SEO point: Google the full name, land on the docket name the clerk used.
