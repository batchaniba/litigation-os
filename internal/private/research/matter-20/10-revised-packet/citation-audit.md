# Citation audit — working motion packet (30 pp.)

**Source audited:** `uploads/Working_Motion_Packet.pdf` (SHA-256 `03d89a03a776b3b723c4937f7d6c9e1d8260c87e8871427a266f47c96968daa0`), byte-identical to `00-user-inputs/Newanforbi_26CV191136_Writ_Motion_Packet.pdf`.

**Method:** Every statute, case, and quotation in the packet was checked against an opened primary or official source (leginfo.legislature.ca.gov; courts.ca.gov; Justia official-reporter pages; Cornell LII for 8 U.S.C. § 1101(a)(15)(U) and 8 C.F.R. § 214.14; michaelrehm.com reproduction of *People v. Jackson* (2010) 189 Cal.App.4th 1461, cross-checked to the official citation). Record sentences were checked against `Matter20_Merged_v3.pdf`, the petition exhibits, the August 29, 2026 demurrer order, and the standalone CHP Form 202 in `02-incident-evidence/`. Bates pages AGO 000029–000053 in the merged packet are image-only; content was confirmed from the standalone Form 202 and the evidence map’s visual check. Pinpoints that could not be confirmed on the Bates overlay are marked **[PINPOINT UNCONFIRMED]**. Items that could not be opened are marked **[UNVERIFIED]** and were not reused as quotations in the revised packet.

**Status key:** VERIFIED = opened and the cited proposition is on the cited page. WRONG = opened; cite or proposition is incorrect. UNSUPPORTED = the record page does not say what the packet says. PARTLY = authority exists, but the pinpoint or the packet’s use overstates it.

This audit is research, not a filing.

### Record sentences that overstate and should be cut or narrowed

1. **“The officers did not observe Petitioner driving” (Answer ¶ 34).** ¶ 34 admits the car was not moving **when observed**. It is not an admission that officers lacked a basis to believe earlier driving. Petitioner said he had driven from Oakland (transcript 01:03:47–01:03:57; Answer ¶ 46). Narrow to “not moving when observed.”
2. **PAS-only arrest.** The transcript sequence supports handcuffing immediately after PAS refusal. The Answer attributes arrest to a “chemical alcohol test” refusal. Do not collapse the two.
3. **“Passed” FSTs / no impairment.** Form 202 reports HGN clues, Romberg 43 seconds with eyelid flutters, and one-leg-stand nonperformance. Answer denies “no objective signs.” Isolate the walk-and-turn conflict.
4. **Video proves no odor.** Silence on the recording and urine identification are narrower facts (Answer ¶¶ 36, 44).
5. **Zero / negative BAC.** The produced result is 0.073 ± 0.004 g% at 02:38 a.m., not zero.
