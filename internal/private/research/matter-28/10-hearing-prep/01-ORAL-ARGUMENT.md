# Proposed oral argument - internal rehearsal draft

Use the version that matches the judge's question. This is not testimony, a filed brief, or a substitute for confirming the actual filed papers. Bracketed instructions are for preparation, not words to read aloud.

## Opening - approximately 90 seconds

Good morning, Your Honor. Brendan Ngwa Nforbi, plaintiff, appearing on my own behalf.

I ask the Court to deny the motion, or, if it identifies pleading defects, to allow a targeted amendment rather than dismiss the entire action with prejudice.

On preclusion, I recognize that the Court must consider the substance of the prior disposition. My point is not the title of the signed order or the fact that counsel prepared it. The July 15 order sustains a demurrer without leave to amend. Defendants now rely on a case-status entry to establish the additional conclusion that there was a final judgment with the preclusive scope they claim. I ask the Court to examine the operative disposition and apply claim and issue preclusion separately.

On the pleading, the complaint identifies more than a government contract. Paragraphs 18 through 25 describe the treatment provider's alleged role in the parole decision process, the policy, and the provider-agent reporting interactions. Paragraphs 54 and 58 separately connect Smith and HOPE to that policy. The retaliation allegations concern the use of treatment reporting in the parole process after protected complaints, not simply an employee using insulting words.

If the Court finds those allegations insufficient, I request leave to identify the specific communications, individual acts, adverse decisions, and causal links the Court finds missing. I also request claim-specific treatment of the fraud and RICO pleading issues. A conditional request to amend is not consent to dismissal with prejudice.

## Preclusion module

I agree that section 1738 requires application of California preclusion law. I am not asking for a federal exception merely because the complaint includes section 1983 or RICO.

Griset requires examination of substance, and I address it on that basis. Berri explains the ordinary distinction between sustaining a demurrer and entering the ensuing judgment. Here the signed instrument contains the demurrer ruling; the additional “Case Disposed” entry is what defendants rely on to bridge finality. I ask the Court to identify the operative final disposition and its actual scope before treating every claim as barred.

[If asked whether a separate judgment exists: answer only after checking the current register. If still unknown, say “I have not verified a separately entered judgment in the materials available to me,” not “there is no judgment.”]

[If asked whether the state ruling was exhaustion-only:]

The signed order uses failure-to-state-a-cause language. I should not characterize that signed order itself as exhaustion-only. Any argument about the particular issues decided needs to be grounded in the actual state ruling record.

[If the Court finds finality:]

Then I ask the Court to address the remaining preclusion elements separately, including which injuries and issues were adjudicated, and the basis for Smith's asserted privity. I recognize that simply changing legal theories or adding an officer does not avoid preclusion.

## State action module

The relevant alleged function is participation in mandatory treatment-completion decisions and reporting used in the parole process. Paragraph 18 alleges the agents' reliance on HOPE's completion determination. Paragraph 21 identifies the alleged policy, paragraph 34 the specific agent-directed attendance entry, and paragraphs 31 through 35 the reporting chain.

West shows that professional judgment and contractor status do not categorically remove a provider from section 1983. I recognize that West involved prison medical care. My argument here depends on the particular delegated role and interactions alleged, not on equating every outpatient clinician with a prison physician. If more detail is required, I request leave to specify the decisionmakers, communications, authority, and resulting decision.

## First Amendment module

The protected activity is the complaints identified in paragraph 38. Paragraphs 39 and 40 describe the alleged response in the containment-team process, and paragraphs 57 through 59 connect the alleged policy and reporting to injury. I understand that criticism alone is not enough. The claimed adverse action is the retaliatory use of reporting or decisionmaking power with parole consequences.

[Identify an existing pleaded consequence if the judge asks. If the complaint does not sufficiently identify one, acknowledge the particular gap and request leave; do not give new sworn facts from the podium.]

## Due process module

I am not challenging the conviction, initial classification, or original treatment requirement. I recognize that Neal rejected the classification claim of the plaintiff who already had a sex-offense conviction. The claim I seek to plead concerns later treatment retention and materially inaccurate inputs used in that decision process.

I also recognize that state procedural requirements alone do not establish a federal liberty interest. If the Court finds the interest or the denied process inadequately identified, I ask for leave to specify the actual restraint or decision, the source of the claimed entitlement, what notice or opportunity was missing, and how the available review process failed to address that deprivation.

Patsy addresses exhaustion. It does not itself prove that the process was inadequate.

## Fraud / RICO / Bane module

The opposition already states that I do not rely on section 1519 as a RICO predicate and do not seek the public-enforcement-only $25,000 Bane penalty. Those corrections do not withdraw the remaining claims.

For fraud, the issue is which identifiable representation caused which discretionary decision and resulting loss. For RICO, any amendment must identify actual mail or wire transactions and the money-or-property scheme, person/enterprise distinction, and direct economic injury. I request leave for supported particulars rather than a ruling that requesting leave itself establishes incurability.

[Do not promise that records will prove fraud. Do not offer to drop a claim unless the user separately chooses that course.]

## Thirty-second closing

I respectfully request denial. If the Court finds pleading defects, I ask it to identify the affected claims and allow a targeted amended complaint, with a definite deadline. If any claims proceed, I request a prompt plan for the protective order and lawful re-production of the existing discovery, followed by focused discovery and ADR. I am prepared to address the schedule in the joint statement after the Court's ruling.
