# Citation Index

Mechanical catalog of every legal citation in the document corpus, keyed to the page
it appears on. Generated by `internal/scripts/build-citation-index.py`; machine-readable
output in `internal/docs/citation_index.json`. Both files are written together, so the
figures below always describe the committed index.

Companion to `CITATION_AUDIT.md` (July 12, 2026), which hand-verified 147 citations
across 24 matters and found two that appear fabricated. That audit sampled motion
practice and identified documents by filename. This one is exhaustive and records page
numbers, so a citation in a draft can be traced to a source page rather than trusted.

none — those documents are unsearched until OCR'd. Blank form-library templates,

manuals are listed in `documents[]` but not scanned for citations — their
boilerplate (and OCR noise) is not this litigant's authority.

## Known limitations

- Reporter series is occasionally dropped where source spacing is unusual (`47 Cal App 214`
  for `47 Cal.App.4th 214`) — numbers right, series not.
- Pairing refuses to join a case name to a reporter across an intervening case name, so an
  unparseable reporter leaves the authority unpaired rather than joined to the next case's
  numbers. An index that invents citations would be worse than none.
- Blank `_form-library/` templates, `service_admin` forms, and HOPE discovery
  manuals are listed in `documents[]` with `citationScan: skipped` and contribute
  zero citations. They remain in the search index.
- Nothing here verifies a citation exists or says what it is claimed to say. This locates
  and counts; verification remains the audit's job.
