### Definitions and instructions

“Plaintiff” includes the name variants used in Plaintiff's relevant hiring and screening records. “Accurate” means the responding defendant. “Amazon” means the Amazon entities or client accounts involved in Plaintiff's 2026 application. “Order” means Accurate screening reference 496623722, orderY94611404, and its related search, package-review, notification, correction and dispute components. “Dispute” means Plaintiff's October 2026 dispute associated with service request 20261014-15421955 and any continuation of it. “Subject Systems” means the platforms, interfaces, workflows and screening package used for the Order.

Produce responsive nonprivileged documents and ESI within your possession, custody or control. Vendor material is included to the extent within your control, including through contractual retrieval rights; this instruction does not assume control over every vendor record. A document includes emails, messages, attachments, database records, API payloads, portal records, audit logs, reports and existing structured exports.

Unless stated otherwise, the transaction period is July 1, 2026 through the date of production. Governing agreements or policies created earlier are included if operative for the Order. Institutional requests36–40 have their own narrower periods. These requests do not require creation of nonexistent records. If identical material has already been produced, identify its Bates numbers or other unique production identifiers and the request it satisfies; produce any omitted attachments, versions or metadata.

Produce email with attachments and message relationships preserved; spreadsheets in native format; structured order, vendor and event records as existing native exports or reasonably usable CSV/JSON preserving field names, event IDs, timestamps and time zones. Produce ordinary documents as searchable PDFs unless native form is necessary to preserve material properties. Supply existing data dictionaries needed to interpret the production. If a requested form is objected to, state the form proposed under Rule 34(b)(2)(D).

State specific objections, whether responsive material is being withheld, what will be produced, and when production will be completed. Identify and describe privilege claims under Rule 26(b)(5)(A), subject to any agreed or ordered protocol. Produce segregable nonprivileged material. Nothing here requests privileged legal advice or protected attorney mental impressions. Supplement as required by Rule 26(e). Respond within Rule 34's applicable period.

### Requests

**REQUEST21 — Governing procedures beyond those actually followed.** Produce the policies, matching criteria, field-mapping specifications, name-normalization rules and vendor specifications that governed, were required to govern, or were actually used in processing the Order or its component searches, including searches496623723,496644204 and 496644200. Produce versions operative from July 1, 2026 through completion of the October 2026 Dispute, whether or not followed. Include criteria for selecting/transmitting name variants, matching identifiers, excluding results, verifying disposition/supervision status, and distinguishing no match from an incomplete, unavailable or failed search. This request is limited to Subject Systems and does not request source code or materials exclusively concerning unrelated products.

**REQUEST22 — Complete governing agreement and certification.** Produce the complete executed master/services agreement governing the Order, including incorporated terms, statements of work, addenda, amendments, schedules and package specifications operative for the Order or correction; applicable account/order instructions; and all end-user certifications, acceptance records and receipt timestamps on which Accurate relies for furnishing the Order. Include applicable adjudication criteria and the allocation of disclosure, authorization, rights-summary, public-record notice, adverse-action, dispute and correction duties. Earlier documents are included if operative for this Order.

**REQUEST23 — Completed reinvestigation and version provenance.** Produce nonduplicative records of receipt, routing, source contacts, source responses, searches rerun, factual findings, corrections and propagation of corrections for the Dispute, through production. Include records explaining the October 19 initiation entry, the revised report's continued display of an October 14 update date, and assembly of the file-disclosure package bearing an October 27 cover date. Include the revised report's generation and delivery records. This request supplements First Request 18; it does not withdraw it.

**REQUEST24 — Additional Amazon business communications.** Produce nonprivileged communications between Accurate and Amazon concerning Plaintiff's adjudication, eligibility, recommendations, review flags, pending/delayed screening's effect on onboarding or shift availability, the conviction-question response, and dispute/correction handling, from July 1, 2026 through production. Include business communications through account representatives, portals, APIs or counsel acting as business intermediaries, to the extent nonprivileged. This request supplements First Request 19.

**REQUEST25 — Decision configuration and actual outputs.** Produce the Subject Systems' Amazon account configuration, applicable adjudication/eligibility matrices, decision tables, status mappings and thresholds operative for the Order, together with each actual recommendation, adjudication result, eligibility code, hold/release event or pending-status instruction generated for or transmitted concerning the Order. Include existing records distinguishing Accurate's actions from Amazon's actions. Do not produce material exclusively concerning unrelated job packages.

**REQUEST26 — Disclosure and authorization presented to Plaintiff.** Produce each disclosure, consent, authorization, scope-of-investigation notice, copy-election form and amendment presented to or obtained from Plaintiff for the Order, exactly as rendered or recorded, including linked/incorporated text and the governing version. This includes forms maintained on Accurate's own systems and any Amazon-supplied forms within Accurate's control.

**REQUEST27 — Assent and copy-election evidence.** Produce the existing electronic-signature, clickstream, checkbox-state, version-association, presentation and acceptance records for the documents in Request 26, including timestamps and evidence identifying the form actually presented and the response actually submitted. Include records showing whether a report copy was elected and who was assigned to send it.

**REQUEST28 — Rights-summary and public-record-notice compliance.** Produce the actual rights summary Accurate provided with or before furnishing the Order, and its provision records; the actual public-record notice relied upon for this Order, with dispatch/delivery-failure records; and the procedure version Accurate relies upon if asserting strict-procedures compliance instead of contemporaneous notice under 15 U.S.C. § 1681k(a). Include the actual rendered destinations for any [LINK]/[ENLACE] placeholders in notices provided to Plaintiff, with existing version and link-resolution records.

**REQUEST29 — Additional intake streams.** Produce raw information obtained directly from Plaintiff, imported from another Accurate file, generated by an address/identity source, or entered by Accurate personnel for the Order, insofar as not encompassed by First Request 4's Amazon-origin data. Include original name/alias and conviction-question inputs, relevant user-interface defaults and validation records, and records of transformation, attribution and correction. Preserve the distinction between submitted values, defaults and later derived values.

**REQUEST30 — Tauxbe source and query evidence.** Produce nonduplicative Tauxbe Data records within Accurate's control for search 496623723 and any rerun/correction, including the exact request/response payloads, identifiers and name variants used, timestamps, jurisdiction/source coverage, source URLs or database labels, returned candidates or errors, result filtering, and result-code interpretation. Include records showing whether any underlying California registry record was retrieved, retained, excluded or communicated. This requests records whether they show a hit, no hit, or failed/incomplete search.

**REQUEST31 — InformData/SJV transaction evidence.** Produce nonduplicative InformData/SJV records within Accurate's control for Santa Clara search 496644204, including submitted identifiers and variants, acknowledgments, assignments, retries/holds, court retrievals, raw returns, attachments and communications concerning the processing interval or completion. Include records showing whether the first/last-name fields in the raw request affected routing, search scope or processing time.

**REQUEST32 — Infinity and omission decisions.** Produce nonduplicative Infinity Court Research & Retrieval records within Accurate's control for San Joaquin search 496644200, together with Accurate's decision records for each of the three case entries in the raw return, definitions of the matching/disposition flags used, and any mapping, deduplication, reporting restriction or verification rule that determined the final reported result. This request does not assume any particular entry was Plaintiff's or reportable.

**REQUEST33 — Vendor obligations and retrieval rights.** Produce the agreements, statements of work and specifications with Tauxbe Data, InformData/SJV and Infinity that governed their work on the Order, including operative provisions on query parameters, source restrictions, identity matching, accuracy, retention, dispute forwarding, corrections, audit access and Accurate's right to obtain underlying records. Unrelated customer transactions are not requested.

**REQUEST34 — Currentness and source-to-report mapping.** Produce existing source and mapping records establishing how the Order's Santa Clara filing date, charge-disposition date, sentence, release date and current-parole statement were selected and verified. **REQUEST35 — Correction delivery and access.** Produce nonduplicative event and acknowledgment records showing when each corrected report or corrected component was generated, posted, transmitted, accepted, accessed or downloaded by an Amazon account, including report/version IDs, payloads, account/recipient identifiers, channel and time zone. **REQUEST36 — Relevant prior complaint index.** For January 1, 2023 through October 31, 2026, produce existing complaint registers, regulator-notice indexes or quality-incident summaries concerning the Subject Systems' erroneous alias attribution/name mapping; criminal-record identity matching; current supervision/disposition status; public-record notice timing; or failure to propagate an accepted correction. Redact unrelated consumer identifiers. No creation of a new index is required; if none exists, produce existing records sufficient to identify substantially similar incidents and their resolution. **REQUEST38 — Applicable training and implementation.** Produce the training materials and attendance/acknowledgment records applicable to the personnel or teams that handled the Order or Dispute concerning the five categories in Request 36 and employer certifications. **REQUEST41 — Retention and available audit history.** Produce the retention, overwriting and event-logging policies operative in 2026 for the Order's intake data, vendor request/response records, report versions, client access logs and Dispute records.
