## Authorities that change the response

- **Griset, 25 Cal.4th at 698-700:** defeats a categorical “no separately titled judgment can ever be final” argument. Distinguish its actual complete adjudication and address the instrument's substance.
- **Berri, 43 Cal.2d at 859-860:** supports the usual distinction between sustaining a demurrer and ensuing judgment; it was a section 583/mandamus case, not a universal claim-preclusion decision.
- **DKN, 61 Cal.4th at 824-828:** keep claim and issue preclusion distinct; acknowledge its discussion of derivative corporation/employee liability.
- **West, 487 U.S. at 54-57:** professional judgment does not categorically defeat state action; prison medical-care delegation is materially different from outpatient parole treatment and must be bridged with pleaded facts.
- **Neal, 131 F.3d at 830-831:** includes a direct adverse holding for the convicted plaintiff Martinez. The proposed duration/procedure distinction is an argument requiring independent legal support.
