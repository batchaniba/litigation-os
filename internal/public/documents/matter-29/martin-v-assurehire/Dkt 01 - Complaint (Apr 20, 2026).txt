15                                FACTUAL ALLEGATIONS
16          6.      In or around April 2024, Plaintiff applied to work as the Assistant Vice
17    President of a local credit union (the “Bank”).
18
            7.      As part of the application process, the Bank requested a consumer
19
20    report from AssureHire as part of a routine background check, and AssureHire sold
21    to the Bank a consumer report concerning the Plaintiff on or after April 25, 2024.
22
            8.      The report furnished by AssureHire was for employment purposes.
23
24           9.     This consumer report contained at least one item of information which
25
      was a matter of public record and of the type of information that was likely to have
26

            10.    Defendant AssureHire has been reporting, and did here report,
 4
 5    derogatory and inaccurate statements and information relating to Plaintiff and

 6    Plaintiff’s criminal history and record to third parties (“inaccurate information”).
 7
            11.    The inaccurate information includes, but is not limited to, a criminal
 8
 9    record that has been fully expunged by Court Order dated March 22, 2017.
10          12.    The report sold by AssureHire included the expunged record, and did
11
      not indicate or state anywhere on the report that the record has been expunged.
12
13          13.    Specifically, the inaccurate information grossly disparages the
14    Plaintiff and portrays her as a convict. There is perhaps no greater error that a
15
      consumer reporting agency can make.
16
17          14.    The derogatory inaccuracies appear to be caused by a misreading of
18    public records. Any rudimentary inspection of the data would reveal the
19
      inaccuracies. Defendant obviously failed to employ such a procedure.
20
21          15.    Plaintiff was subsequently denied employment at the Bank and
