# Strategy note — Count 1 peremptory-writ motion

**One page. Research only. Do not file.**

## Chosen theory

The strongest path is **legal error in the standard** Captain Serrato applied on April 20, 2026. Penal Code section 679.10(g)(1) uses “shall certify” once two predicates exist: qualifying victimhood and helpfulness. The denial letter rejects “qualifying” and “substantially similar” activity in five sentences, without applying section 236, section 679.10(c)(18)–(19) or (d), or the non-prosecution rule in (k)(1), and without identifying helpfulness as a defect. Traditional mandate under Code of Civil Procedure section 1085 may compel an official to exercise remaining discretion **under a proper interpretation of the law** (*Common Cause v. Board of Supervisors* (1989) 49 Cal.3d 432, 442). That is the lead argument.

The supporting record argument is **abuse of discretion on a material factual predicate**: CHP Form 202 states Petitioner “chose not to perform” the walk-and-turn “and related it was difficult,” and then folds “poor performance on the FST’s” into the 23152(a) / 40300.5 arrest rationale. The petitioner-prepared transcript at 01:10:14–01:10:30 records willingness and Officer Melano declining the test because the shoulder was uneven. Combined with the verified admissions (stationary car when observed; urine cup; no spoken odor or slurred-speech accusation; Livermore / sign-reading; handcuffed transport; no DUI charges; no investigating-entity information request), the no-qualifying-activity conclusion cannot rest on that report sentence. This is the pathway Judge Whitman already described at demurrer (Aug. 29, 2026 order, p. 4) — still a merits question, not a finding.

## Fallback

**Vacate and remand with instructions**, not a demand that the judge sign Form I-918. Instructions: apply (c), (d), (g), (h), and (k); do not require charges, prosecution, or an open investigation; do not treat the optional PAS refusal as unhelpfulness on the alleged restraint ((k)(3)(H) as a guardrail); correct the walk-and-turn attribution if the native recording corroborates the transcript; issue a written determination. **Direct certification** is preserved only if the Court finds both statutory predicates and that no other lawful disposition remains (*California Correctional Supervisors Organization, Inc. v. Department of Corrections* (2002) 96 Cal.App.4th 824, 827 — standard only; that court affirmed denial). A focused evidentiary proceeding is the last rung, not the lead request.

## What was cut, and why

- **Independent Count 2 / Count 3 theories** (written-denial specificity; helpfulness presumption as a free-standing duty). Whitman sustained both without leave. (h) and (i) remain only as Count 1 tools.
- **Mercer / “no observed driving.”** Pinpoint not opened; Petitioner said he drove from Oakland. Narrowed to “not moving when observed.”
- **Sanders / parole-search chronology.** Pinpoint not opened; it is not the certification theory. Door-opening kept only as encounter progression, not as a U-visa offense.
- **Zero-BAC, “passed all tests,” “video proves no odor,” PAS-only-arrest admission.** Each overstates the record (0.073 ± 0.004 g% at 02:38 a.m.; HGN/Romberg reported; Answer ¶ 38 uses “chemical” not PAS).
- **Evid. Code 1553, Veh. Code 23610, AG bulletin page cites.** Wrong or unopened. Stay out.
- **Three proposed instruments (judgment + writ + alt. hearing order).** Replaced by one proposed order with a clean choice: certify, or vacate and remand with the listed instructions. Hearing-setting is a residual checkbox, not a third pleading.
