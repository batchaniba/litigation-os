## Claim-by-claim posture

- **FCRA / ICRAA accuracy:** Removing NEWANSORBI supports the inference that the October 14 alias should not have been in the report. It is not an admission of unreasonable procedures, willfulness, furnishing to Amazon, or causation. Obtain the actual intake and transformation history. FCRA § 1681e(b) addresses reasonable procedures for maximum possible accuracy (15 U.S.C. § 1681e(b): https://uscode.house.gov/us/usc/t15/s1681e/).
- **Reinvestigation:** The October 14 dispute covered the alias and NSOR search. The October 28 letter claims timely completion and removal/confirmation; investigate what was done, including the October 14 acknowledgment, October 15 claimed receipt, and October 19 result note. The unchanged parole line, disposition dates, and links were not within this two-item dispute. Section 1681i: https://uscode.house.gov/us/usc/t15/s1681i/.
- **File disclosure:** The packet contains a revised report and form notices, not a clearly identified full-file response. Separate a statutory § 1681g request for information in the file at the time of request, sources and recipient identities from litigation discovery for historical versions, audit logs and workpapers. Do not claim § 1681g automatically requires every historical PDF version. Section 1681g: https://uscode.house.gov/us/usc/t15/s1681g/.
- **Other report defects:** “CURRENTLY ON PAROLE” requires a dated CDCR status record; the filing/disposition-date coincidence requires the Santa Clara court record. The stale October 14 header is a version-integrity question, and [LINK]/[ENLACE] are potential notice-template defects, not proof that a conviction is inaccurate. If the stated December 2020 release date is correct, changing the parole-status phrase alone does not necessarily take the case outside a seven-year period. The conviction-question “No” needs the native application before taking a position.
- **Amazon user duties / Fair Chance:** The current snapshot lacks a confirmed preliminary or final denial and the decision file. A conviction-based preliminary disqualification implicates individualized assessment and written notice under Gov. Code § 12952; FCRA employment adverse-action duties turn on a decision based in whole or in part on a consumer report. Verify both the decision and its source before asserting that a missing notice proves a violation. Official California statute copy: https://emsa.ca.gov/wp-content/uploads/sites/71/2024/02/GOV-12952.pdf; 15 U.S.C. § 1681b(b)(3): https://uscode.house.gov/us/usc/t15/s1681b/.
- **Counts 5–7:** Amazon's Rule 12 motion concerns the pleaded privacy, UCL and IIED claims. The October 28 packet postdates the complaint; do not treat it as pleaded fact at the November 18 hearing. Verify whether the September opposition was filed, since this snapshot has no ECF number.
- **Fair Chance procedural gate:** The open-questions list has not located a CRD complaint or right-to-sue notice. Verify the record independently; this is distinct from discovery merits.
