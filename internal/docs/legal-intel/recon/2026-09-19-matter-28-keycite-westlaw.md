## Deep-dive verdicts (2026-09-19 ~02:45 PDT — treatment passages read on Westlaw; seat released)
- **Perez → KEEP.** The "superseded by statute" flag (Sanders v. Walsh, 219 Cal.App.4th 855; Pitzen v. Superior Court, 120 Cal.App.4th 1374, both via Rosse v. DeSoto Cab Co.) concerns only Perez's collateral-estoppel exception to Sanderson (27 Cal.3d at 883–85), undermined by CCP § 116.770(c) ending trial-de-novo small-claims appeals. The memo's prematurity≠merits holding at 882–83 is untouched.
- **DKN → KEEP.** Samara v. Matar is 5 Cal.5th 322 (filed June 25, 2018, not July 26 as KeyCite's summary had it); its pushback on DKN concerns only the claim-vs-issue-preclusion distinction vis-à-vis Skidmore, not the privity holding at 824–26 — which Samara quotes favorably. Current KeyCite tab no longer shows "Questioned by" for the Supreme Court Samara (it shows the Court of Appeal Samara as "Distinguished by").
- **Neal → KEEP.** Garcia v. Henry, 13 Fed.Appx. 579 (9th Cir. 2001) is an UNPUBLISHED memo; the "disagreement" is only about the burden of proof at sex-offender-designation hearings — a question Neal expressly left open (131 F.3d at 831 n.14). Garcia assumed Neal's liberty-interest/due-process framework.
- **Costello → KEEP.** U.S. v. Daifullah, 11 F.4th 888 (8th Cir. 2021) called into doubt only Costello's loose use of "jurisdictional" for the affidavit rule (post-Henderson clarification), not the Rule 41(b) precondition≠merits holding at 284–88 — and Daifullah itself notes Costello distinguished the affidavit rule from true subject-matter jurisdiction.
