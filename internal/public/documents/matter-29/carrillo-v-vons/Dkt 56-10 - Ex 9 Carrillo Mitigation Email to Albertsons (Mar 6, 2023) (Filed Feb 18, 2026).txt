  At your request the following is written to provide my opinion on the 'receptivity' to treatment evidenced by your
  client and my patient, Mr. Roberto Sandoval, a man with whom I have worked in sexual offense-specific treatment
  over the course of approximately the last 16 months.


  I am a forensic psychologist licensed by the State of California to practice psychology. I own a private practice
  outpatient clinic in San Diego, and treat approximately 200 criminal offenders weekly. Twelve years ago I was
  awarded the private bid contract to treat the highest risk sexual offenders in San Diego County, those men released
  to the community after stay in prison hospital attendant to California Welfare and Institutions Code Sexually Violent
  Predator Statutes. I continue to work with these offenders. In 2014 I was awarded the contract to treat adult males
  investigated for or charged with crimes of child pornography possession, by United States Federal Probation. In the
  same year, San Diego County of Probation awarded me the contract to evaluate adult male offenders released from
  prison to county probation, attendant to California Assembly Bill 109. I have completed thousands of psychological
  evaluations on criminal offenders, have offered my opinions to state and federal courts on thousands of occasions,
  have testified frequently in criminal court, and have published articles in peer-reviewed professional journals.
  Finally, I was trained in the assessment of psychopathy by Robert D. Hare, Ph.D. the world's expert in the construct,
  in his laboratory at University of British Columbia, in 1997.

   I have found Mr. Sandoval to be an apt, thoughtful, intelligent patient. He admits to his behavior in the controlling
  crime, recognizes its gravity, and has demonstrated empathic concern for young girls involved in crimes of child
  pornography. He is neither antisocial nor psychopathic, and I believe that his sexual recidivism risk can be reduced
  by treatment intervention.

  Thank you for your consideration. Should you desire further information please do not hesitate to contact me.
