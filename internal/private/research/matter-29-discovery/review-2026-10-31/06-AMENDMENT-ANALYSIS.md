# Amendment analysis — new Accurate duties, not a general rewrite of Amazon's case

Private analysis. Evidence keys and primary authorities resolve in the source register. Proposed allegations below are an outline, not an amended complaint or a representation that leave has been granted.

## 1. Procedure and timing

The complaint predates the October report and dispute. Accurate's answer and Amazon's motion were served/filed September 22; the answer's proof of service records that date. The foundation's October 13 date is the twenty-one-day calculation, but any applicable Rule 6(d) mail addition depends on valid service method and consent. **Either calculation has expired by October 31.** Proceed under Rule 15(a)(2), with the opposing parties' written consent or leave; seek Rule 15(d) permission alternatively or additionally for post-complaint events. Do not rely on the complaint's reservation of amendment rights as leave. [C ¶29, PDF pp. 9–10; A p. 40, lines 2410 onward; MTD19-2; FRCP 6(d),15(a),(d).]

Ask at 26(f) whether defendants will consent to a proposed amendment on a specified schedule. Prepare the proposed **complete** amended complaint, clean copy and redline against the last operative pleading, a short explanation of newly obtained facts, and a proposed response schedule. Krishnan § 4 requires the redline as an attachment when an amended pleading is filed; attach it to the leave package as well to make the change reviewable. Civil L.R. 15-1(a) independently requires both the complete proposed pleading and a redlined or highlighted comparison as exhibits to the leave motion; it prohibits incorporating the earlier pleading by reference. Under (b), file the amended pleading within seven days after leave unless otherwise ordered; (d) addresses service within fourteen days when service is not accomplished through the court electronic system or written-consent electronic means. If a Rule 16 scheduling deadline has been entered in the meantime, address good cause to modify it; no amendment deadline is identified in the reviewed Dkt. 9 or live clocks. [D9 pp. 1–2; CLOCK; ASK § 4,p. 2; FRCP 15,16(b)(4); CLR15-1.]

Explain that the source materials arrived after the original complaint and identify each new event without describing every internal hypothesis as proven. The October 27 cover date does not prove when all contents were compiled: the packet includes an October 28 letter. Do not claim discovery of an authenticated October 27 snapshot. [FD pp. 1,179, lines 1–27, 2450–2497.]

Keep Amazon's existing pre-adverse-action theory distinct from Accurate's own accuracy, certification, public-record and dispute duties. Do not voluntarily concede the July claims in order to plead October claims; do not falsely backdate October conduct to explain July causation. Adding statutory duties to an existing numbered count may be technically possible, but separate counts/subcounts give clearer notice of the defendant, act, date and injury. Amendment on these new facts is safer than asserting that a generic “FCRA” heading already pleaded every statutory duty. [C ¶¶22–25,54–70; FRCP 8,15.]

## 3. What each disclosure item actually contributes

### NEWANSORBI and “Provided by Applicant”

The best proposed factual paragraph is narrow: the Original Report attributes the identified AKA to the applicant; Plaintiff disputed that attribution; Accurate later removed it. Add Plaintiff's own truthful factual statement about whether he ever provided it. The raw request shows propagation into at least the Santa Clara request, but it also contains correct historical variants. Do not allege every search was performed solely under NEWANSORBI. [O14 p. 2; FD p. 31; FD pp. 39–42; CL p. 2.]

Then specify the alleged procedural defect **only if supported**: failure to preserve original inputs, mapping a surname to a first-name field, treating a derived value as applicant-supplied, failing to validate before transmission, or failure to correct downstream data. These are alternative investigative hypotheses, not presently verified findings. Ask for intake and transformation evidence first if Plaintiff cannot provide a nonconclusory basis. [RFP 4–6; Set Two 21,29,31; ROG 3–4,13.]

### Tauxbe / NSOPW / no record

The cover sources establish Accurate identified Tauxbe Data Inc. and the National Sex Offender Public Website for the Amazon search. They do **not** establish a California result or employment use of California website-derived information. NSOPW may be an entry point to jurisdiction data, but the actual source chain must be proved, not assumed from an aggregate label. A search yielding no record is not equivalent to communicating an affirmative registrant status. [FD p. 2,lines 49–62; FD p. 25,lines 952–962; A Defense 2,p. 38; CL p. 2.]

Do not turn the lawsuit into a claim that Accurate owed a duty to make the report more adverse. If the theory is defective searching rather than a missing adverse fact, identify the detrimental result: misattributed identity, a material misleading impression, measurable delay, or another concrete consequence. None follows automatically from a no-hit. For ICRAA, the favorable-report bar is especially material. The 2025 Target no-reportable-records result is a separate transaction, not proof that an identical query or a reportable 2026 California hit existed. [FD pp. 49–50,lines 1549–1621; CA50(c).]

### San Joaquin entries

Use the exact three entries and boolean values as grounds to discover how the system decides matches and reportability. Do not plead a false **reported** San Joaquin criminal accusation when the final report lists no record. The `FMISC` names and `N/A` charge descriptions need court records and code definitions before labeling them convictions, unrelated persons, or reporting violations. A list of raw candidates in a consumer disclosure is not necessarily an employer report of those candidates. [FD pp. 18,28–30,lines 718–733, 1124–1245.]

### “CURRENTLY ON PAROLE” and disposition dates

The report pairs a historical release date with a current-status statement and repeats October 25, 2017 as both filing and charge-disposition date. These justify targeted verification. This repository text does not independently establish the correct disposition date or that Plaintiff was off parole on the reporting date; several Santa Clara source pages are image-only in the extract. An accurate but unfavorable current fact is not an accuracy violation merely because it is unfavorable. Plead a particular incorrect date or unsupported materially misleading currentness representation only after checking the source images and competent status evidence. [O14 pp. 4–6,lines 114–167; FD pp. 18–19,32–37; CL pp. 17–18.]

## 4. Proposed count outline

**Accurate FCRA certification claim (retain and sharpen).** Identify employer procurement, consumer report, furnishing event, and the exact missing/deficient certification under§ 1681b(b)(1)(A)(i) or(ii), or absent rights-summary provision under(B), if supported. Do not merely allege Accurate failed to guarantee Amazon's compliance. Plaintiff does not currently possess the certification; plead on information and belief only with an identified factual basis and information asymmetry, not certainty that it never existed. [C ¶61; RFP 16; FCRA-b(b)(1).]

**New Accurate § 1681e(b) count.** State the particular report/version; the exact erroneous or materially misleading alias/attribution and any separately verified disposition/currentness defect; why procedures were unreasonable; the third-party communication or other concrete injury; and causation. Plead negligent liability under§ 1681o and, if supported by particular knowledge/recklessness facts, willful liability under§ 1681n in the alternative. An adverse employer decision is not the only possible injury, but an isolated typographical alias does not establish actionable harm merely because it appears in a report. [O14; CL; FCRA-e/n/o; TRANSUNION; SAFECO.]

**New § 1681i count, only upon a supported defect.** Identify receipt date, the exact dispute, relevant information supplied, the particular investigation failure and surviving inaccurate/incomplete information or notice defect, and resulting injury. Separate statutory deadline from reasonableness. If only an additional(a)(7) request later goes unanswered, plead that later event with its actual receipt/deadline; do not invent it now. [DISPUTE; CL; FCRA-i.]

**New § 1681k count, only upon a supported notice-and-procedures theory.** Identify the actual adverse public-record furnishing; explain why no qualifying contemporaneous notice was given and why strict completeness/currentness procedures were absent or deficient. Plead in the alternative where the facts support it, without alleging that the statute demands both compliance methods. A missing signed acknowledgment is not alone proof no notice was sent. [O14; RFP 17; FCRA-k.]

**Recast Accurate ICRAA count.** Add§ 1786.20(b) with the same independently verified accuracy defect. Separately analyze§ 1786.20(a)'s user-certification/written-agreement duties if the contract evidence supports them; do not substitute it for the requested(b) theory. Correct¶¶67–70, separating the procurer's pre-procurement disclosure/authorization, elected copy duty, CRA duties, and user adverse-action advice. State§ 1786.50 remedies accurately and address its favorable-report exception. [C ¶¶64–70; CA16/20/40/50.]

**Do not silently add:** a CCRAA count, a federal investigative-consumer-report theory based solely on public-record research, or§ 1681g violations merely because discovery material is absent. Consumer-file disclosure and Rule 34's broader document discovery are different obligations. A missing audit log or authorization in this production does not itself prove a file-disclosure violation. [FD p. 1; FCRA-g; RFP definitions.]

## 5. Injury, defenses and relation to July events

The complaint alleges a July 27 removal of the assigned shift. The report and reinvestigation at issue are dated October. Lost wages from the July event cannot be attributed to an October typo without an earlier communication, delay mechanism, later continuing loss or other supported causal explanation. Keep separate ledgers of actual events/harm, without importing settlement figures. Identify out-of-pocket loss, lost opportunity or actual emotional/reputational consequences truthfully and specifically; do not manufacture them from a statutory citation. [C ¶¶22–25; O14 p. 1; CL p. 2; A Defenses 3–4,p. 38; TRANSUNION.]

Third-party dissemination can matter under *TransUnion*,594 U.S.413,432–42, but dissemination of a harmless wrong alias is not automatically equivalent to the stigmatizing false designation there. Each claim requires a concrete-harm analysis. Statutory damages do not eliminate ArticleIII. Negligent FCRA liability requires actual damages under§ 1681o. Do not promise statutory recovery “per field” or automatic multiplication of overlapping theories. [TRANSUNION; FCRA-n/o.]

Accurate's Defense 8 invokes§ 1681h(e); its reference is not an automatic bar to every state statutory cause. Assess common-law/privacy theories under the provision's actual categories and exceptions, and assess any subject-specific§ 1681t preemption independently, including its grandfather provisions. Accurate's “no hiring decision” defense does not eliminate independent CRA obligations, but it may defeat an employer-specific duty or unproved employment-loss causation. [A pp. 38–39; FCRA-h(e),FCRA-t; C counts2–4.]

Rule 15(c) relation back must be assessed for each new theory; the same screening transaction supports an argument for accuracy allegations, but a later reinvestigation is a post-complaint event appropriately addressed under 15(d). Do not treat relation back as automatic or a substitute for the applicable limitations analysis. The reviewed clocks contain a conservative outer watch, not a ruling on accrual. [CLOCK entry29-sol-fcra-icraa; FRCP 15(c),(d); FCRA-p.]

## 6. If leave is denied

The records may still be used within the **existing** theories: alias/intake evidence tests what report existed and who communicated it; the contract tests certification; source paths test registry-use allegations; correction history tests actual furnishing, good faith, causation and claimed continuing injury. That does not authorize a new accuracy/reinvestigation recovery under a generic FCRA caption after leave is denied. The UCL is not an automatic backdoor: its remedy, standing and federal equitable-remedy defects remain. Privacy/IIED claims likewise do not inherit liability from any report error. [C ¶¶28–29,61–70 and counts5–7; A Defenses 3,5,8–12; MTD pp. 3–7; OPP lines 807–877.]
