# N.D. Cal. judge knowledge base

Profiles of the five Northern District of California judges on Plaintiff's federal matters. Each folder holds the judge's current standing orders, downloaded from cand.uscourts.gov on **Oct. 29, 2026**, plus a cited `summary.md`.

**Research method**
- **Sources:** Judge pages, standing orders, the Civil and ADR Local Rules, and orders from each judge published in GovInfo's USCOURTS collection (https://www.govinfo.gov).
- **Citations:** Every claim is cited. "Not found" means the searches did not locate support. It does not mean the practice is absent.
- **Not used:** PACER and CourtListener's authenticated API were **not** used.
- **Excluded on purpose:** No settlement figures, private data, or Zoom passcodes are included.

## Per-judge filing checklists

Each folder's `summary.md` has the full checklist with citations. Condensed versions:

### Breyer (CRB)
- [ ] Brief ≤15 pp.; summary of argument if >10 pp.
- [ ] Proposed order attached (motions and oppositions). Word copy to crbpo@.
- [ ] Paper chambers copy delivered to the Clerk's Office, 16th Fl., 450 Golden Gate, by noon the next court day. 3-hole punched, ECF header on top, tabs on the right.
- [ ] Redline with any amended pleading.
- [ ] Discovery: notice of discovery dispute (MJ referral), not a motion.

### Martínez-Olguín (AMO)
- [ ] Extension sought ≥7 days before the deadline, by motion or stipulation.
- [ ] Every motion opposed (silence = consent).
- [ ] AI-verification certification on any submission containing AI-generated content.
- [ ] Redline or highlight with any amended pleading, filed as a stand-alone entry. No incorporation by reference.
- [ ] Exhibits as separate ECF attachments.
- [ ] Chambers copy within 2 days (resolve the SO/page conflict conservatively).
- [ ] Zoom only by 7-11 motion ≥7 days ahead. Appearance list to amocrd@ ≥2 court days ahead.

### Cisneros (LJC)
- [ ] Meet and confer, with certification, before **any** motion or non-stipulated request.
- [ ] Proposed order in Word to ljcpo@ on the day of filing.
- [ ] Chambers copy only if Court action is needed and the filing is >50 pp. (48 hrs; TRO/PI 24 hrs).
- [ ] Citations verified (AI errors imputed to the signer).
- [ ] Personal appearance at the Zoom CMC. Statement due 7 days before.
- [ ] Discovery: formal meet and confer, then a 5-pp. joint letter.

### Krishnan (ASK)
- [ ] SO served within 7 days of each appearance, plus a certificate of service.
- [ ] Consent/declination filed.
- [ ] No chambers copies.
- [ ] Redline with any amended pleading.
- [ ] No discovery before the 26(f) conference. Disputes: meet and confer, then a 5-pp. joint statement.
- [ ] Joint CMC statement 7 days before the Zoom CMC.

### Wise (NW) (only if the assignment is confirmed)
- [ ] SO and All-Judges order served within 5 days.
- [ ] Hearing reserved jointly with the CRD before filing any noticed motion. File within 3 business days.
- [ ] Joint CMC statement 18 days before, with full schedule, ADR selection, and standing.
- [ ] In-line redline with any amended pleading.
- [ ] Extension requests ≥3 business days ahead.

## Civil L.R. 3-12 (related cases): applies to all five

Source: https://cand.uscourts.gov/rules/civil-local-rules

- **Definition, 3-12(a):** Cases are related when (1) they concern substantially the same parties, property, transaction, or event, **and** (2) separate judges would likely produce unduly burdensome duplication of labor and expense or conflicting results.
- **Filing, 3-12(b):** Whoever knows of a possibly related case must promptly file an Administrative Motion to Consider Whether Cases Should Be Related (Civil L.R. 7-11). It is filed in the **lowest-numbered** case, with a "Notice (Other)" filed in each other case. 3-12(d)–(e) set the contents and the 7-11 opposition.
- **Decision, 3-12(f):** The judge assigned the lowest-numbered case decides, within 14 days after the response is due.
- **Effect, 3-12(g):** If the cases are related, the CMC is rescheduled and any noticed motion hearings are vacated and must be re-noticed.
- **Related rule:** Civil L.R. 3-13 (notice of pendency of other actions).
- **Judge-specific 3-12 practice:** **Not found** for any of the five.
- **Numbering:** Among the cases above, 4:26-cv-05953-CRB has the lowest number. This is an observation only, not a recommendation to relate any cases.

## ADR Local Rules: default for all five

Source: https://cand.uscourts.gov/rules/adr-local-rules

- **3-2 / 3-3:** Most civil cases are automatically placed in the Multi-Option Program: ENE, mediation, or a magistrate-judge settlement conference, or private ADR (3-4).
- **3-5(a)–(b):** Meet and confer, and file an ADR Certification, presumptively 21 days before the initial CMC.
- **3-5(d):** The judge selects the process at the CMC.
- **3-7:** The session is held within 90 days of referral.
- **7-2:** Settlement conferences.
- The Clerk provides forms to pro se parties.

## Other local rules cited

Source: https://cand.uscourts.gov/rules/civil-local-rules

- **5-1(d)(7):** No chambers copies unless a standing order or the judge requires them.
- **7-1(b):** A motion may be decided without oral argument.
- **7-2 / 7-3:** 35 days' notice. 25-pp. motion and opposition; 15-pp. reply. Opposition due in 14 days; reply in 7.
- **7-11:** Administrative motions: 5 pp., 4-day opposition.
- **7-13:** Notice after 120 days under submission.
- **6-1 to 6-3:** Changing time.
- **1-4 and 3-9(a):** Sanctions. Pro se parties are bound by the rules.
- **3-11:** Address changes.
- **4-2:** Serve standing orders with the complaint.
