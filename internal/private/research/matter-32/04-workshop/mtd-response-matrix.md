## Procedural integrity

The MTD asks the court to draw factual inferences adverse to plaintiff from allegations about challenged records. Khoja/Grier offer a focused answer, not permission to ignore unfavorable allegations or legally operative documents. [Khoja](https://cdn.ca9.uscourts.gov/datastore/opinions/2018/08/13/16-56069.pdf); [Grier](https://cdn.ca9.uscourts.gov/datastore/opinions/2023/01/20/21-16702.pdf).

Whittlestone is a useful answer to the Rule 12(f) mechanism, not a freestanding right to punitive damages. Likewise, a demand for amendment must specify the corrections and new allegations rather than assert that every historical dismissal was irrelevant. [Whittlestone](https://cdn.ca9.uscourts.gov/datastore/opinions/2010/08/17/09-16353.pdf).
