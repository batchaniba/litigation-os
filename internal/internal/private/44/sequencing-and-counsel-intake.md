## Order of operations

1. **Consent.** Confirm she wants this work and name the outcome she actually wants (safety, no contact, divorce, money, criminal process, immigration help, or some mix).
2. **Present danger.** If there is an immediate threat, that is an emergency-services and advocate problem, not a git problem.
3. **County and residency.** Forum is not optional. Venue for divorce is where the plaintiff resides. Protective-order filing follows the statutory nexus. Foreign marriage or property does not make Maryland automatic.
4. **Most recent act.** Write one dated row: conduct, injury, county, witness, existing record. If that row is not a Family Law § 4-501 listed act, a protective order is not the first paper.
5. **Preserve, do not create.** Receipts, photos already taken, medical records she already has, bank statements she already controls, copies of texts she already possesses. No new secret recordings. No shared-account logins. No tracking.
6. **Limitations map on that row, then the next.** One-year civil assault. Three-year general tort. Four-year CICB statutory clock (reconcile COMAR). Criminal clocks are separate.
7. **Maryland family counsel first** if divorce, support, or a protective order is in play. **Separate civil/limitations counsel** before any damages demand. **Victim advocate / criminal counsel** before any application for statement of charges.
8. **Money last.** A tort or SAFE Act demand can collide with a divorce monetary award, CICB offsets, restitution, and insurance. Sequence them; do not stack the same dollar four times.
