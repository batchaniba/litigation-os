# Matter 28 — Discovery service sequencing memorandum

*Newanforbi v. Shannon Smith, LCSW, et al.*, N.D. Cal. No. 3:26-cv-07504-LJC  
Prepared October 14, 2026 (PT). Internal work product.

## Objective

Assuming every pleaded claim proceeds, discovery should build four linked records:

1. the HOPE rule and individualized reason applied to Plaintiff;
2. the people, systems, and evidence behind each decision;
3. what HOPE transmitted to CDCR/DAPO and what the government did with it; and
4. the resulting constitutional, employment, and economic consequences.

The package is drafted broadly enough to preserve Monell, Smith individual liability, retaliation, due process, Bane Act, misrepresentation, and RICO theories. Service should be staged to obtain the direct causal record before company-wide comparator and financial discovery.

## Proportionality and leverage

The strongest first-wave requests are Plaintiff-specific and event-specific. Comparator, compensation, and company-wide billing discovery should use “documents sufficient to show,” defined date ranges, and de-identification.

For RICO, seek Plaintiff-linked invoices, transmission records, rate schedules, and direct payment records before demanding enterprise-wide revenue data. For Monell, seek the operative policy, historical versions, approval chain, and defined completion data before seeking individual client files.

## Final service checklist

Before service:

- confirm the protective order's entry status;
- confirm no stay or discovery order appears in docket entries 16–27;
- insert the correct caption and party names;
- choose the actual requests being served and renumber them consecutively;
- check that each interrogatory set stays within Rule 33(a)(1);
- remove internal drafting notes;
- add the correct date and signature block;
- select a service method authorized by Rule 5 and any written agreement;
- prepare a proof or certificate of service;
- preserve an exact served copy;
- calendar responses 30 days after service, adjusted under Rule 6; and
- calendar Judge Cisneros's privilege-log deadline: absent agreement or order, the log is due no later than 14 days after the response or disclosure is due.

For any discovery dispute, complete the formal in-person or video meet-and-confer required by Judge Cisneros before using the joint-letter procedure.
