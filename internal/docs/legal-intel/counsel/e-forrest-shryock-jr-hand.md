## 5. Verified Case Appearances

1. **Linton v. County of Contra Costa**, Cal. Ct. App., First Dist., Div. 1,
   No. A153188 — filed 1/23/2019, certified for partial publication.
   "Vogl Meredith Burke, E. Forrest Shryock, Jr." represented defendants/respondents
   Greater Richmond Inter-Faith Program and Dennis Jauregui (alongside Lewis Brisbois
   and Beach Cowdrey Owen). **Won:** the court affirmed denial of the plaintiff's
   attorney-fee motion under the Disabled Persons Act and Unruh Civil Rights Act,
   holding that a settlement without a liability finding does not trigger statutory
   fee liability (*Doran* analysis of a CCP §998 offer).
   Source: https://law.justia.com/cases/california/court-of-appeal/2019/a153188.html

2. **Van Dusen v. City of Oakland, et al.**, N.D. Cal. Case No. 3:13-cv-05023-VC
   (Judge Vince Chhabria) — counsel for defendants Fix Our Ferals, Dairne Ryan,
   Linda McCormick, Susan Hoffman, and Ellen Lynch (with Corie A. Edwards). Order
   granting motion for clarification of the 7/30/2014 order, filed 9/26/2014
   (Doc. 143).
   Source: https://cases.justia.com/federal/district-courts/california/candce/3:2013cv05023/271465/143/0.pdf

   defendants; leave to amend denied as futile).

## 6. Recent Press (2023)

**Carmel Mission Foundation vs. Diocese of Monterey**, Monterey County Superior Court
(suit filed May 8, 2023). Shryock, as "Foundation attorney," defended the nonprofit
against the Diocese's fraud / breach-of-fiduciary-duty claims over ~$6.3M in Downie
Museum renovation funds. Quoted: *"There can be disputes about how projects should be
run, but it's one thing to have a difference of opinion and quite another to accuse
someone of fraud because you disagree with how they're running the project."* At a
July 21, 2023 hearing, Judge Vanessa Vallarta expressed doubts the Diocese had proved
its case.
