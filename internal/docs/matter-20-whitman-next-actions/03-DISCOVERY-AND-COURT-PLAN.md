## Step 2: choose procedure, not an omnibus motion
- **No code-compliant response / absent required verification:** consider CCP §2031.300 and response/objection implications, after verifying timing/service.
- **Verified but evasive/incomplete response or untenable objection:** CCP **§2031.310 further-response** motion; specific good cause, meet-and-confer declaration under §2016.040, timely notice, separate statement under CRC **3.1345** (or authorized concise outline). Track the conservative Dec 7 deadline if Oct 23 was service of verification.
- **Promised compliance but omitted identified records:** CCP **§2031.320 compliance** motion. Do not mechanically apply .310's time limit to a pure .320 violation.
- **Personnel / citizen-complaint investigation** (especially AGO 000001–000021, 000026–000028, 000055–000060): ordinary Set One RFP 8 expressly excluded Pitchess records. Consider a **separate** noticed Evidence Code §§1043–1045 request supported by sworn materiality, officer/custodian and transaction nexus, seeking judicial in-camera review; ask for statutory disclosure only where authorized. Inspect Penal Code §832.7(b) 2026 public-record exceptions by exact category; do not assert a blanket exception or use a discovery motion to get content unseen.
- **Records exclusively possessed by lab, county DA or magistrate:** identify actual custodian and evaluate appropriate third-party subpoena/business-records process, not fictional CHP "control".
