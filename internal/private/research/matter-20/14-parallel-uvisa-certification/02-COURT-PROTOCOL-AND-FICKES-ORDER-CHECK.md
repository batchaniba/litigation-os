# Judicial-certification route: Fickes order retrieval and Alameda court administrative inquiry
**PRIVATE, UNSENT and NOT A MOTION.** Do not send case-merits materials privately to a judicial officer or staff. An administrative inquiry can ask for public court procedures; a substantive certification request should follow the actual protocol, service and ex parte rules.

## Stage 1 — retrieve June 18, 2026 order
Retrieve authenticated filed/court-copy order from *Newanforbi v. Meza-Gonzalez*, Alameda Sup. Ct. No. 26CV168913, Judge Mark Fickes, Dept. 518. Existing 89-page request in user files is titled `Motion for U Visa Certification.pdf`; portfolio's Matter 24 federal Dkt. 18 at 11:17–18 is quoted as saying Fickes denied on June18. The *actual signed order and grounds remain to be seen.* Determine whether denial was a refusal on merits, procedural defect, stay, lack of evidence, or any ruling on preclusion. Compare any new Oct23 CHP production and whether it would be admissible/relevant before pursuing another judge.

## Stage 2 — procedural clerk question, not a merits submission
**Subject:** Administrative Procedure Inquiry — Civil-Court Form I-918 Supplement B Requests (Alameda Superior Court)

To the Civil Division Clerk / Appropriate Court Administration:

I seek only publicly available procedural information concerning requests for judicial Form I-918 Supplement B certification under California Penal Code section 679.10 and 8 C.F.R. section 214.14.

Does the Alameda County Superior Court have an established procedure or designated judicial officer for judicial U-visa certification requests arising from civil proceedings? If so, where is that protocol published, are requests assigned administratively or filed in the pending case, and what notice and service procedures are required?

I do not seek an ex parte discussion of pending litigation's merits. I would comply with the applicable procedures and give required notice to parties.

## Stage 3 — if protocol and procedural basis established
Prepare a **noticed** request or protocol-specific application only if a judicial official had responsibility for detecting relevant conduct through actual judicial proceedings and there is a **new, truthful evidentiary basis** beyond the Fickes motion. Preserve the Fickes order and disclose pertinent procedural history as required. Do not assert that Judge Whitman's August 29 demurrer order is a merits finding or that a judge must sign because a plaintiff alleges criminal restraint. Consider whether judicial certification by the same judge reviewing the §1085 writ raises disqualification/neutrality concerns and use whatever administrative reassignment mechanism is actually available.

For a model—not binding Alameda protocol—see SF Superior Court https://sf.courts.ca.gov/divisions/criminal/u-visa . USDA/USCIS 2026 Resource Guide explains judges can sign based on qualifying crimes detected in civil proceedings. Do not attempt to obtain a signature from a court that has no relation to the incident or proceedings.
