## B. Mandatory disclosure audit before Counts One or Two

Confirm whether the complete flow contains:

- clear and conspicuous disclosure;
- disclosure before procurement;
- a document consisting solely of the disclosure;
- statement that a consumer report may be obtained for employment purposes;
- written/electronic authorization.

### ICRAA § 1786.16

Confirm whether a standalone California disclosure contains the required items, including:

- notice an investigative consumer report may be obtained;
- permissible purpose;
- character / reputation / personal-characteristics / mode-of-living scope;
- actual investigative consumer reporting agency's name, address, and telephone number;
- nature and scope of investigation;
- statutory rights summary;
- privacy-practices information;
- any required copy-request mechanism.
