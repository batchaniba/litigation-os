## Collection protocol

1. Obtain Njiubu's authorization and use lawful access only.
2. Preserve the original; work from a copy.
3. Record acquisition date, source, device/account, exporter, and method.
4. Compute a cryptographic hash when practical.
5. Export complete threads and metadata, not only selected screenshots.
6. Keep a contemporaneous chain-of-custody note.
7. Use redacted descriptions in git and a secure private locator for originals.
8. Do not record or intercept communications unless Maryland counsel confirms
   legality for the specific setting and jurisdiction.
9. Do not delete, edit, annotate, rename destructively, or publish originals.
10. Issue preservation requests or subpoenas only through authorized counsel or
    lawful process; do not impersonate Njiubu.

## High-priority third-party records to identify

Police/911/CAD; medical and counseling records; photographs; shelter/advocate
records; court files; employment and wage records; property/repair records;
bank/tax/title records; phone/email/platform exports; location or device logs;
insurance notices; and witnesses. Identification is not authorization to obtain.
