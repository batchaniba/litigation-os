## Hearings vs. ruling on the papers
- Personal appearance is required unless the court says otherwise. The court may take a matter under submission under L.R. 230(g) by minute order. There are no tentative rulings (S2).
- In practice, Sacramento pro se cases go to the magistrate judge for all pretrial matters under L.R. 302(c)(21) (S3). So most motions in M38 and M39 will be decided first by Riordan or Delaney through F&Rs, and Coggins reviews them.

## Briefing rules (S2)
- Briefs are limited to 25 pages and replies to 15 pages, in 12-pt type. Ask for more pages at least 7 days ahead.
- No sur-replies without leave.
- An amended pleading must be filed with a redlined or highlighted comparison version.
- Cross-motions for summary judgment use four sequential briefs (25/25/20/15 pages).
- Separate pretrial statements are allowed where a party is pro se.
- The plaintiff must serve the standing order on the other parties.
- The pre-filing meet-and-confer certification applies when the parties are represented.

## Proposed orders, courtesy copies, ex parte, sealing (S2)
- Proposed orders only for administrative, ex parte, or TRO matters. Email them in Word to DCorders@caed.uscourts.gov.
- Administrative relief goes through L.R. 233. Ex parte applications, except initial extensions under L.R. 144(c), are "rarely appropriate and therefore discouraged."
- TRO papers require a courtesy copy to chambers. No other courtesy-copy requirement was found.
- Sealing requests go to Judge Coggins, not the magistrate judge.

## F&R review habits (S5, R1)
- Four 2025 orders in pro se cases each adopted the F&R in full after de novo review. The time from F&R to adoption order was:

  - Coelho: May 20 to Jun 13, 2025 (about 3 weeks)
  - Smith: Mar 27 to Jun 4, 2025 (about 10 weeks)
