1. Consult `../08-audit/inventory.md`, `manifest.json`, and `verification.md` for provenance and retrieval limitations. The source archive's original count and hashes remain untouched; the research memo is supplemental.
2. Compare every material fact to the original PDF, CHP's Answer, agency evidence, and where possible the native dashcam. The user-prepared transcript is **not** a certified CHP transcript. Later October discovery does not automatically prove what Serrato knew or considered in April.
3. Do **not** conflate the conditional §679.10(g)(1) certification duty with a conclusive entitlement to the signed form. Direct certification needs proved statutory predicates and no remaining lawful discretionary outcome. Vacatur/remand needs an *identified* material statutory or factual defect, not the shortness of the April 20 letter.
4. Written denial is governed by §679.10(i)(2), not (g). A complaint about absence of an extensive reasoned letter cannot simply resurrect a dismissed separate claim.
5. Verify every authority, pinpoint, and procedural assertion against current primary sources and actual orders before moving it into a filing. The memorandum is research for a self-represented litigant; its “privileged-style” description does not itself establish legal privilege.
