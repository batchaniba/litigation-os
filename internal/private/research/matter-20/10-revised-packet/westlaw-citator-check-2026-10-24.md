# Westlaw citator check - Matter 20 (Oct 24, 2026)

**Source:** Westlaw Patron Access for Pro Se. Read on screen; KeyCite History/Negative Treatment/Citing Refs checked. Flags as of Oct 24, 2026.

## Use

- **Saleeby v. State Bar (1985) 39 Cal.3d 547** - yellow flag, negative treatment on other points only (due process/fees). Read 559-568.
  - 561: traditional mandamus is the vehicle.
  - 562: "mandamus will lie to correct an abuse of discretion or the actions of an administrative agency which exceed the agency's legal powers" (preceding sentence: won't compel a particular method of exercising discretion).
  - 565-567: findings "sufficient to apprise interested parties and the courts of the bases for the administrative action"; "Some findings are necessary to make that review meaningful."
  - 568: remand "for such further consideration as may be appropriate" / "We express no view on the merits."
- **Crestwood Behavioral Health v. Baass (2023) 91 Cal.App.5th 1** - 0 negative treatment.
  - 15: quotes Common Cause 442 proper-interpretation rule.
  - 16: mandamus "is a proper remedy to challenge agency discretionary action as an abuse of discretion."
  - 20-21: arbitrary, capricious, fraudulent, or without due regard for rights.
  - Petitioner lost on facts.
- **Alameda Health System v. ACERA (2024) 100 Cal.App.5th 1159** - 0 negative treatment.
  - 1177: Common Cause rule; arbitrary, capricious, entirely without evidentiary support, or failed to conform to procedures required by law.
  - Deferential; quasi-legislative context.
- **CV Amalgamated LLC v. City of Chula Vista (2022) 82 Cal.App.5th 265** - yellow flag (modified on denial of rehearing Aug 12, 2022).
  - Remedy sentence is at 286 ("we will direct the trial court to issue a writ of mandate requiring the City to exercise its discretion to rescore...") - quote exactly; it is longer than the paraphrase.
  - Ministerial writ at 283.
  - Negative: S.D. Cal. 2025 (distinguished); Feah, LLC v. City of El Monte (2026 WL 171385) unpublished/noncitable, distinguished on ministerial point only - not a problem.
- **Vichy Springs Resort v. City of Ukiah (2024) 101 Cal.App.5th 46** - quotes CCSO 827 "only one choice" rule; legal error within arbitrary/capricious review. Star page not captured.
- **Water Audit California v. Merced Irrigation Dist. (2025) 111 Cal.App.5th 1147** - yellow flag; Common Cause rule. Skimmed.
- **Asgari v. City of Los Angeles (1997) 15 Cal.4th 744, 752 & fn. 3** - yellow flag. Officers immune for malicious prosecution "but not for false arrest and imprisonment"; false arrest is one way of committing false imprisonment. Supports qualifying-crime point.
- **Hagberg v. California Federal Bank (2004) 32 Cal.4th 350** - false arrest is "merely one way" false imprisonment occurs. Headnote only; 14 negative entries unreviewed - **verify before use**.

## Caution / do not cite

- **Assn. of Deputy DAs v. Gascon (2022)** - RED flag. Avoid.
- **Gillan v. City of San Marino (2007) 147 Cal.App.4th 1033** - RED flag; disapproved by Leon v. County of Riverside (2023) 14 Cal.5th 910 on Gov. Code 821.6. Avoid unless checked against Leon.
- **People v. Agustin (2025 WL 3564125)** - only California case citing 679.10; unpublished, red flag, noncitable.
- **Sanders and Mercer** pinpoints previously flagged as miscites - keep out.

## Federal / adverse

- **Chairez v. Mayorkas, 168 F.4th 1227 (9th Cir. 2026)** - yellow flag.
  - 1234: guidance on discretion makes the whole judgment call unreviewable under 1252(a)(2)(B)(ii); overrules ANA Int'l methodology.
  - Does not cite Perez Perez.
  - Bea concurrence flags Poursina/Nakka split.
- **Perez Perez v. Wolf, 943 F.3d 853 (9th Cir. 2019)** - yellow flag, only distinguished. Likely survives Chairez as review of threshold eligibility criteria (our read, unconfirmed; only skimmed).
- Federal limits are on federal court review of USCIS; they don't control state mandamus over a state officer's 679.10 duty, but expect CHP to cite them.
