## Rules (S2)
- Motions and oppositions are limited to 20 pages and replies to 10. Extra pages, supplemental briefs, or sur-replies filed without leave "will not be considered." Requests are due 7 days before filing.
- 12-pt font minimum, including footnotes. Exhibits must be internally paginated (Exh. A-1, A-2…).
- MSJ statements of undisputed facts go in a numbered table format. Cross-MSJs use four briefs (20/30/30/10 pages), and the parties must meet and confer 28 days before the deadline.
- **Remote appearance** is disfavored absent hardship. "A pro se party without means to travel to the courthouse" is an example. File a written request **10 days** before the hearing. All-Zoom if granted; no hybrid hearings.
- He may submit motions under L.R. 230(g). A hearing is guaranteed if an attorney with fewer than 5 years' experience will argue.
- **Discovery:**
  - Joint statement 20 pages; 251(d) affidavit 15 pages; 251(e) motions 20/20/10.
  - Where everyone is represented, conferral must be in person, by phone, or by video, and is "strictly" enforced.
  - Informal telephonic conference: give dates 4 to 14 days out. File a 2-page single-spaced joint letter 48 hours before.
- Proposed orders in Word to SCRorders@caed.uscourts.gov, with "proposed order" and the case number in the subject line.
- Deposition transcripts: attach excerpts only; email the full transcript to SCRorders@.
