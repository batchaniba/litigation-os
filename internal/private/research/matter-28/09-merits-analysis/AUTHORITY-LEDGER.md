## Application discipline

Use the regulation's actual language and temporal version. Do not treat a state procedural entitlement as a federal substantive liberty interest without the additional constitutional analysis. Do not treat a state contract, an exclusive provider allegation, or a bad report alone as a conclusive finding of state action. The oral script identifies these as arguments and amendment questions.
