# Plaintiff-protective clause library

Drop-in language. Adapt the bracketed terms; keep the operative words.

## 1. Continue the event only

> The [initial case management conference] currently set for [date] is continued to [date certain]. **No other date or deadline in this action is modified by this stipulation.** All deadlines not expressly continued above remain in effect as previously set.

Use whenever the defense proposes moving a hearing or conference. This is the single most important clause in the library.

## 2. Date certain, never a floating anchor

> The following deadlines are reset as follows: [obligation] — [calendar date]; [obligation] — [calendar date].

Never accept: *"reset to run from the continued conference date,"* *"reset accordingly,"* or *"to be determined following the Court's ruling."* If the defense insists on tying dates to an event, add an outside date:

> …provided that in no event shall [obligation] occur later than [calendar date].

## 3. Not a stay of discovery

> This stipulation continues [the conference / the deadline] only. It is not a stay of discovery, does not stay or defer any party's discovery obligations, and no party waives any right to seek or oppose discovery.

## 4. No further extensions

> The parties agree that no further extension of [these deadlines / this date] will be sought absent order of the Court on a showing of good cause.

## 5. Reciprocity — simultaneous exchange

> [Obligation] shall be exchanged simultaneously on [date]. Neither party is obligated to serve before receiving the other's.

Use for initial disclosures, expert designations, and settlement demands. It converts a one-way giveaway into a trade.

## 6. Price the continuance

> As consideration for the continuance, Defendant shall [serve initial disclosures under Rule 26(a)(1) / produce the documents described in Exhibit A / accept service on behalf of (defendant)] on or before [date certain].

## 7. Reservation of rights

> Nothing in this stipulation constitutes a waiver of any claim, defense, objection, or right, including any right to seek [discovery / amendment / relief from the schedule], and no party concedes any issue by entering into it.

## 8. Pending motions unaffected

> This stipulation does not modify the briefing schedule or hearing date on [Dkt. __], which remain as set.

## 9. Per-document e-signature authorization

Never grant standing authority. Authorize one file, in writing, after reading it:

> You are authorized to file [exact filename] with my electronic signature, /s/ [NAME]. This authorization is limited to that document as attached and does not extend to any revised version.

And require the filer's attestation:

> Pursuant to Civil Local Rule 5-1(i)(3), [filer] attests that concurrence in the filing of this document has been obtained from each signatory.

## 10. Tolling — if you must

> The parties agree that the period from [date] to [date] shall be excluded from the computation of any applicable limitations period as to [claims]. This agreement is limited to the period stated, applies only to [claims], and is not an admission that any claim is or is not timely.

Never open-ended. Never silent on which claims.

## 11. Withdrawing consent before entry

A stipulation binds only once entered as an order. Before entry:

> Plaintiff respectfully notifies the Court that he withdraws his consent to the stipulation filed as Dkt. __. The circumstances that prompted it have changed: [the joint statement is now on file and initial disclosures have been served]. Plaintiff is prepared to proceed on [date].

Use sparingly. Withdrawing consent to something you signed costs credibility, and costs more if you expanded it.
