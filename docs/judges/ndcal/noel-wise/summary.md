## ⚠️ Two traps
1. **Motions by reservation only.** Meet and confer on dates, then **jointly** contact the CRD to reserve; file within **3 business days** of the confirmation email or the reservation expires. "Any motion filed without a reserved hearing date **will be stricken**." Max two motions per party per hearing without leave. Administrative (7-11), 6-1 time, and pro hac vice motions are not heard. (SO § C)
2. **Plaintiff must serve this SO and the All-Judges CMC order within 5 days** of filing, reassignment, or a new party (SO § B).

## Calendar
CMCs Tuesdays 9:00 a.m. via Zoom; civil L&M Wednesdays 9:00 a.m. in person (by reservation); pretrial Wednesdays 2:00 p.m.; trials Mon–Thu. Filing deadlines 5:00 p.m. unless ordered (SO § C; judge page).

## Filing checklist (Wise)
1. Within 5 days: serve SO + All-Judges CMC order.
2. Before any noticed motion: meet and confer on dates → jointly reserve with CRD → file within 3 business days.
3. Joint CMC statement 18 days before CMC with full schedule, ADR choice, standing statement; no incorporation by reference.
4. Redline/highlight amended pleadings in-line.
5. Extension requests ≥3 business days ahead with particularized good cause.
6. Courtesy copies / proposed-order emailing waived while self-represented (§ S.7 fn. 1) — confirm still waived in any later revision.
7. File hearing/CMC materials on ECF within 7 days (§ S.8).
