## Execution guardrails (Fam. Code § 1615)

An agreement that fails these is presumed involuntary and can be thrown out whole:

- [ ] Fiancée has **independent counsel**, or signs a separate written waiver after being advised in writing.
- [ ] **7 calendar days** between presenting the *final* agreement (plus advice to seek counsel) and signing.
- [ ] If she is unrepresented: written explanation of the terms and of rights waived, in a language she is fluent in.
- [ ] **Full financial disclosure** from both sides (schedules of assets, debts, income, pending lawsuits, crypto wallets).
- [ ] Not signed under duress, close to the wedding date, or while she lacks capacity.
- [ ] Any spousal-support limit: she **must** have independent counsel (§ 1612(c)).

Target signing date = wedding date minus at least 30 days. Keep a dated log of every draft handed over.
