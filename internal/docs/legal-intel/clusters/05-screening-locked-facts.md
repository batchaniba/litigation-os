## Statute

Penal Code § 290.46(j)(2)(E) bars use of information disclosed on the Megan’s Law site for employment purposes (with the “person at risk” exception).

§ 290.46(j)(4) is **not** a $25,000 automatic minimum. It permits actual damages, an additional amount of at least $250 up to three times actual damages, fees, and exemplary damages or a civil penalty **up to** $25,000. Plead it as written.

## *Lerma*

*Lerma v. Amazon.com Services, LLC*, C.D. Cal. 8:22-cv-01727: Megan’s Law / § 290.46 claim **dismissed** because Accurate’s report showed court-record sourcing and National Sex Offender Database Search “NO RECORD FOUND.” Case later settled on the surviving Fair Chance Act claim against Amazon only. Never write “Amazon settled the identical § 290.46 claim.”
