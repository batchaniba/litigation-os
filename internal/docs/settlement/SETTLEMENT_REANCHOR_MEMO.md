## 3. Hard rules

1. **After the opening, no move without their number.** Silence, "conferring with client," and requests for time are not counters.
2. **Never withdraw a figure without a replacement,** and never pair a price cut with extra defense time.
3. **No unearned discount.** Every concession buys something identifiable and recorded:
   - a defense number;
   - a document;
   - a held clock;
   - a materially narrower release;
   - verified prompt payment;
   - valuable non-cash relief.
4. **Reprice on events, not pressure.** Events include:
   - motion rulings;
   - report production;
   - admissions;
   - discovery results;
   - coverage;
   - trial proximity.

   A deadline, a default motion, rent, or a promised payment is not an event.
5. **No demand while their dispositive motion is pending,** unless Plaintiff would hold that figure after winning. Do not settle the week of a hearing just to settle.
6. **Write the walk-away before each send.** If a drafted figure is higher than the one about to go out, stop and reconcile.
7. **Never trade a default, clock, or waiver for procedure alone.** Trade it for money or a counter.
8. **Tracks are separate.** No cross-case or global release unless the combined figure expressly prices every released matter and party.
9. **Allocate shared losses once.** SLB's May 23, 2026 rejection already argued that Plaintiff's Matter 1 declaration ties lost employment to the CHP DUI arrest. Keep one shared-loss ledger for the DMV/suspension cluster. The same lost year is not claimed in three captions.
10. **Unfiled captions are not priced.** No unfiled screening case (Uber/TaskUs, Home Depot, Swift, AT&T, Best Buy) is priced off any settlement. File first, or do not talk numbers.
11. **The consumer report is the valuation event** for FedEx, UPS, Amazon, and Accurate. Without a registry-source line, the case is in *Lerma* territory.
12. **Quote, don't characterize.** Do not rest a figure on a defense "admission" unless the exact sentence is quoted with its docket cite. Example: UPS Dkt. 14 on registry use.

## 8. Remedies: what the brackets assume

- **Pen. Code § 290.46(j)(4)(A):** actual damages, an additional amount of at least $250 up to treble actuals, attorney's fees, and exemplary damages **or** a civil penalty **up to** $25,000. It is a cap, not a $25,000 coupon.
- **ICRAA, Civ. Code § 1786.50:** actual damages or $10,000, whichever is greater (non-class), plus costs and fees. Punitive damages are available for grossly negligent or willful violations. The violation still has to be proved.
- **FCRA:** $100–$1,000 statutory damages per consumer only for willful noncompliance (15 U.S.C. § 1681n), plus punitive damages. Negligent noncompliance gets actual damages only (§ 1681o). That band is not the case value.
- **Statutory maxima do not add** across claims. Recovery depends on proof, causation, defenses, damages, and collectability.
- **Pro se:** Plaintiff's own time is not recoverable as attorney's fees. Comparables that include plaintiff-side fees overstate take-home. Example: *Santee* ~$17,240 all-in on a $10,000 offer.
- **Unverified:** a claim that 2026 California appellate decisions let a plaintiff pursue ICRAA's $10,000 alternative without additional actual injury arrived without case names. Do not use it in a demand until the cases are named and cleared through `citation-verify`.

## 9. Deal terms: release hygiene and payment gates

Every term sheet:

- **Parties and claims:**
  - Named defendants in that caption only.
  - Claims arising from that application, that report, and that denial only.
- **Releases:**
  - No Civil Code § 1542 waiver that reaches unfiled captions or other matters.
  - No covenant limiting Plaintiff's participation in his own other cases.
- **Confidentiality:** limited to the amount, not the fact of settlement, once a public stipulation is on file.
- **Signatures and authority:**
  - A complete written agreement signed by all necessary parties.
  - Written confirmation of authority for every releasing defendant.
- **Allocation:** wage vs. non-wage split stated in the agreement. Tax treatment reviewed before signing.
- **Payment:**
  - Payment within a fixed number of days after execution.
  - A remedy for late or failed payment.
- **Non-cash relief** (certification, record correction, vacatur, rehire, references): in writing, objectively verifiable, valued, and with a completion deadline.
- **Dismissal:**
  - Dismissal with prejudice only after funds clear and non-cash relief is complete.
  - CCP § 664.6 retention of jurisdiction requested on the dismissal.
- **Clauses to narrow or price:** non-disparagement, no-rehire, tax-indemnity, and broad confidentiality. Each is a concession.
