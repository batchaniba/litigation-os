Before the Court is Defendant Amazon.com Services LLC’s (“Amazon”) Motion to
Dismiss Plaintiff Brendan Ngehsi Newanforbi’s (‘Plaintiff’) Fifth, Sixth, and Seventh Claims for
Relief Pursuant to Federal Rule of Civil Procedure 12(b)(6) (the “Motion”). Having considered
the Motion, the Parties’ briefing, any arguments made by the Parties at oral argument, and the
record before it, the Court GRANTS the Motion in full. The Court DISMISSES Plaintiff's Fifth,
Sixth, and Seventh Claims for relief against Defendant Amazon.com Services LLC.

IT IS SO ORDERED.
