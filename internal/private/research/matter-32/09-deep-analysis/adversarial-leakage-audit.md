## The governing distinction

Use three separate channels: private risk analysis, targeted court-facing response, and amendment/proof development. A point can be important in the first channel and unnecessary or counterproductive in the second.

- **Private risk analysis:** Research adverse law, test every source, identify missing proof, prepare responses, and preserve an honest assessment of claim strength. Do not turn that assessment into an admission merely by exporting it.
- **Court-facing response:** Address the actual motion, applicable pleading rules, and the elements genuinely in issue; use plaintiff-favorable supported allegations and explain why dismissal is unwarranted. Include necessary corrections and applicable legal treatment without volunteering an entire new defense.
- **Amendment/proof development:** Develop a concrete truthful cure and gather evidence. Do not imply that unavailable trial proof necessarily makes the present complaint insufficient.

Accuracy is not optional: Rule 11 applies to an unrepresented party presenting or later advocating a paper, requires reasonable inquiry, permits nonfrivolous arguments for changing the law, and distinguishes current evidentiary support from specifically identified support likely after investigation or discovery. It does not say that an opposition must attach every adverse document in the private research room. ([Federal Rule of Civil Procedure 11(a), (b)(2)–(4)](https://www.law.cornell.edu/rules/frcp/rule_11))

“Not raised” in this audit means no developed ground was identified in the reviewed ECF 22 memorandum, not that the argument is permanently waived, that a court could never consider the law, or that a particular disclosure/candor obligation disappears. No attorney-specific professional-rule determination, privilege opinion, or discovery-withholding instruction is being made.

## Highest-risk leakage in the research engine

The quoted fragments below are verbatim excerpts, not invented descriptions; source anchors resolve through `adversarial-leakage-index.json`. Numbered audit IDs are finding identifiers, not an assertion of legal liability or outcome probability.

### A01: Supplying a parole-search defense

**Location:** Decision memorandum, “The parole boundary is both an opportunity and a major defense.” **Verbatim:** “while creating a substantial alternative justification for later searches”

ECF 22 does not develop active parole as an independent search exception. Publishing the condition/knowledge requirements, the 01:07:29 log, and Payne's broad authorization together would give defendants a new justification and an evidence-retrieval plan.

**Treatment:** Keep the full analysis internal. Use chronology to prevent retroactive justification if necessary, but do not concede valid conditions, timely knowledge, authorization, or scope; disclose facts where an actual obligation or chosen necessary argument requires it.

**Narrow drafting synthesis:** “The earlier intrusion must be justified by the facts and legal grounds available when it occurred.” This does not invent a new parole concession.

### A02: Volunteering the Lingo defense in proposed argument language

**Location:** Decision memorandum's civil-exclusionary-rule discussion and blueprint's first sample module. **Verbatim:** “Lingo forecloses that shortcut”

The internal distinction is useful, but ECF 22 does not cite Lingo or separately develop a civil exclusionary-rule defense. The sample paragraph's affirmative disclaimer risks becoming a concession about later arrest or damages before the necessity of that concession has been established.

**Treatment:** Retain the research and avoid making an incorrect poisonous-fruit argument. Ordinarily state the independent early-search theory affirmatively; analyze whether any Lingo citation or distinction is necessary only if the actual theory, applicable legal duties, or motion makes it necessary. Lingo's civil exclusionary-rule holding does not itself establish that the later arrest was lawful on these disputed facts. ([Lingo](https://cdn.ca9.uscourts.gov/datastore/opinions/2016/08/08/14-35344.pdf))

### A03: Providing Rule 41 and preclusion research

**Location:** Decision memorandum's historical section. **Verbatim:** “Rose Court identifies four requirements for the federal two-dismissal rule”

The motion invokes history to resist amendment, not as a developed two-dismissal defense. Giving it the cases, transactional analysis, event checklist and potential adjudication effect would construct a different dispositive motion, even where the internal chronology presently appears favorable.

**Treatment:** Keep the audit internal. Do not add a Rule 41 or preclusion section just to announce an issue and defeat it; answer the actual futility argument with supported distinctions and proposed cures. Do not recommend a new voluntary dismissal or stipulation as a housekeeping step.

### A04: Constructing compensatory-damages and mitigation defenses

**Location:** Decision memorandum's economic-loss section and matrix E13–E15. **Verbatim:** “independent parole or medical restrictions, renewal records, pay records, work availability, mitigation”

These are legitimate investigation subjects. In a response to the actual punitive/fee motion, however, publishing medical-expiration, renewal, ordinary-license-versus-CDL, mitigation, or alternative-employer explanations supplies defenses not developed against the compensatory claims.

**Treatment:** Keep the detailed causal and financial audit internal. Preserve supported claimed categories and the opportunity to prove them without certifying an unsupported total; do not stipulate to an alternative cause or reduced damages period based on a snapshot.

### A05: Introducing adverse toxicology

**Location:** Decision memorandum's four findings and matrix E10–E11. **Verbatim:** “It is not a negative alcohol result”

That correction must remain in the research, and no filing should call the result negative. But ECF 22 does not develop this laboratory report; volunteering the ethanol concentration or marijuana-metabolite result could invite a corroboration/credibility argument and expand the record without helping the pleaded time-of-search issue.

**Treatment:** Stop the inaccurate negative-test claim now. Do not equate that obligation with a need to attach or narrate all laboratory results in the opposition; assess proper record use and any disclosure obligations separately. Do not infer driving-time BAC or impairment from metabolite status.

### A06: Writing the magistrate's adverse affidavit remainder for the defense

**Location:** Decision memorandum, actual-affidavit inventory and materiality assessment. **Verbatim:** “does not demonstrate that the actual odor, initial confusion, recent-driving and FST narrative fails probable cause”

Materiality is actually challenged, so it cannot be ignored. The leakage is turning an internal assessment into an affirmative concession that the challenged affidavit is sufficient, or volunteering every adverse assertion as a true fact when the actual court-considerable record has not been determined.

**Treatment:** Keep the complete corrected-affidavit analysis as an internal truth test. In the filing, identify the actual challenged statements, supported allegations and materiality argument using the proper record; if needed, offer a targeted amendment without conceding that the present record establishes lawful probable cause. Chism requires materiality, not adoption of the officer's disputed account. ([Chism, amended opinion](https://cases.justia.com/federal/appellate-courts/ca9/10-35085/10-35085-2011-11-07.pdf?ts=1411064324))

### A07: Handing over a new Moore/protected-activity route

**Location:** Decision memorandum, Count VI. **Verbatim:** “Moore's treatment of silence and retaliatory investigation is adverse enough”

The motion's developed Count VI ground is but-for causation, followed by general QI. A new extended protected-activity/QI presentation can supply a different route to dismissal rather than answering the stated causation attack.

**Treatment:** Research and evaluate internally; do not assert the statutory right automatically supplies First Amendment protection. Whether Moore must be addressed depends on its relevance to the exact argument and applicable legal duties, not simply on its absence from the defense brief. Do not substitute an unpleaded criticism/supervisor-request story.

### A08: Creating a comparator burden in response to a causation argument

**Location:** Decision memorandum, Count VI element list. **Verbatim:** “identify the actual objective evidence needed for an exception”

The Nieves exception workbench is useful preparation, but ECF 22 does not develop a Nieves exception/comparator deficiency as a separate ground. Presenting a comparator-proof deficit as already dispositive would volunteer an adverse ruling and may skip the plaintiff's dispute that probable cause existed.

**Treatment:** Answer the actual causation attack first. If a probable-cause bar and exception become necessary, use the correct conditional framework rather than concede probable cause or announce that exact comparators are indispensable; Gonzalez rejects an overly restrictive comparator demand. ([Gonzalez v. Trevino](https://www.supremecourt.gov/opinions/23pdf/22-1025_1a72.pdf))

### A09: Arguing a broader constitutional-channel dismissal than the motion develops

**Location:** Decision memorandum's Count II channeling discussion. **Verbatim:** “Galbraith, a defense-cited authority, is adverse to simply repackaging the same pretrial seizure injury”

This is partly responsive: the motion expressly calls Count II duplicative and cites Galbraith elsewhere. The excess would be supplying a new extended Albright/Graham/Galbraith dismissal framework while giving inadequate attention to the existing separate license/property allegation.

**Treatment:** Lead with the alleged distinct injury and the criminal-only premise's limits. Keep a fuller channeling analysis internal unless needed; do not concede every fabrication use is merely a duplicate warrant claim.

### A10: Promoting a proof workbench into a pleading prerequisite

**Location:** Amendment/proof proffer's DMV records row. **Verbatim:** “No completed DMV but-for chain or continuous-loss certification”

This is a sensible limit on what the research can certify, not a rule that a plaintiff must possess a final administrative record, authenticated MP4s, an expert reconstruction and every payroll record to oppose dismissal. Copying proof gaps into the response could ask the court to reject a pleading because trial preparation is unfinished.

**Treatment:** Label them internal proof gaps. Separately test existing allegations under the actual pleading standard and Rule 11's support provisions; do not convert the project's quality gates into extra pleading elements. ([Rule 11(b)(3)](https://www.law.cornell.edu/rules/frcp/rule_11))

### A11: Replacing causation law with a more demanding self-imposed test

**Location:** Evidence matrix's causal-chain worksheet. **Verbatim:** “Why outcome changes without it”

A counterfactual is an investigation tool, not an admission that strict but-for causation is the only possible fabrication standard. The engine already includes a Richards qualification, but an isolated table export could erase it and hand the court an unnecessarily demanding test.

**Treatment:** Keep the qualification attached. Preserve the supported materiality argument and distinguish the administrative facts without silently conceding the broader causation question; Richards's fair-trial holding does not automatically decide the DMV variant either. ([Richards](https://cdn.ca9.uscourts.gov/datastore/opinions/2022/06/24/19-56205.pdf))

### A12: Giving the court dismissal-ready claim rankings

**Location:** Decision memorandum priority table and concurrent synthesis QI table. **Verbatim:** “QI likely”

An internal “Last,” “weakest,” “dismissal likely,” or “high risk” label is an assessment, not a pleading or legal concession. A filed version would hand the court its conclusion and could undermine a reasonably supportable request for denial.

**Treatment:** Keep rankings internal. Request claim-specific denial where supportable and alternative leave where appropriate, without predicting that the claim should lose.

## Additional places where the analysis goes too far

### A13: Treating a count heading as a dispositive merits ruling

**Location:** Decision memorandum's Count IV defendant discussion. **Verbatim:** “any proposed expansion requires an express amendment”

The heading map should remain accurate, but the categorical statement risks deciding the legal significance of the entire complaint from the heading alone. Read the body and actual personal-participation allegations before conceding a defendant or theory is absent; conditional leave is not an admission that every contrary construction is unavailable.

**Treatment:** Replace in a filing with a precise description of pleaded conduct and, only if needed, an alternative request to clarify defendant attribution. Do not silently expand or withdraw a claim.

### A14: Requiring the plaintiff to negate every hypothetical alternative offense

**Location:** Priority table, Count III. **Verbatim:** “address all genuinely available indicia and alternative offenses”

The wording can turn a useful totality check into an instruction to invent every arrest offense or exception the defense might have asserted. The current motion does put shoulder parking and DUI in issue, but that is not an invitation to produce an encyclopedia of unrelated offenses.

**Treatment:** Address the actual developed grounds and applicable legal framework. Keep other researched possibilities internal unless a concrete reason makes them necessary.

### A15: Adopting the officer's innocent explanation for PAS timing

**Location:** Decision memorandum, arrest analysis. **Verbatim:** “a voluntary test might otherwise have supplied exculpatory information”

The motion already argues lawful discretion and inadequate retaliatory causation, so the response must engage that. The research nevertheless adds an officer-benefiting exculpatory-test hypothesis and risks making it the accepted reason for the arrest instead of a competing inference.

**Treatment:** Preserve the actual advisement, statement and temporal allegation. Argue the plaintiff-favorable inference from the facts without saying a lawful motive has been established or that timing alone conclusively proves liability.

### A16: Announcing “missing elements” before testing the existing pleading

**Location:** Decision memorandum, Count VI. **Verbatim heading:** “Separate the missing elements”

The heading prejudges the result and encourages copying an adjudication rather than an issue list. The same problem occurs when a table calls the existing joint-investigation allegations “conclusory” without first considering the specific conduct already pleaded.

**Treatment:** Use “Elements to evaluate” internally and argue actual sufficiency in the brief. Do not invent facts to fill a gap, but do not concede a gap merely because more detail could improve an amendment.

### A17: Overbuilding the government's search exception

**Location:** Decision memorandum's cup analysis. **Verbatim:** “any legitimately known odor supplied automobile-search probable cause”

Emergency aid and purported crime-related entry are actually raised, so some exception analysis is responsive. The leakage is supplying additional cases, reconstructed combinations of contested facts, or a complete doctrinal fit the motion does not articulate, especially when the research uses “legitimately known” as if truth and timing had been decided.

**Treatment:** Identify the motion's asserted justification and its missing factual or legal link. Avoid accepting disputed odor, consent, visibility, lawful vantage, or scope; research alternative fits privately.

### A18: Leading with an inventory of plaintiff errors

**Location:** Decision memorandum's relief hierarchy and proposed modules. **Verbatim:** “Credibility concession”

Narrow correction is necessary where the brief would otherwise perpetuate a known mistake. A broad opening concession that the FAC is defective, followed by a catalog of every immaterial duration, transcript or form problem, does unnecessary persuasion for the other side.

**Treatment:** Correct the specific proposition where relevant, then state the surviving argument. Do not equate a form-label correction with a concession of lawful entry, truthful odor observations, valid warrant, no retaliation, or futility.

### A19: Supplying withdrawal or abandonment language without authorization

**Location:** Concurrent synthesis, warrant decision tree. **Verbatim:** “abandon or substantially narrow Count 1”

That can be a conditional internal decision option after the facts and law are checked, not a drafting instruction or present authorization to surrender a claim. Similar “drop,” “omit,” or voluntary-dismissal language in the historical drafts must not migrate into a filed document by default.

**Treatment:** Preserve the option internally with a decision gate. No actual withdrawal, dismissal, amended omission, stipulation, or waiver occurs without an informed choice and procedural review.

### A20: Narrowing punitive damages by claim label rather than alleged conduct

**Location:** Concurrent synthesis, remedies. **Verbatim:** “preserve punitive damages only against an officer for whom deliberate deception, deliberate fabrication, or intentional retaliation remains plausibly alleged”

The motion actually challenges punitive damages, so a response is necessary. The quoted formulation may unnecessarily restrict the available conduct theories to three labels and concede relief for other sufficiently culpable conduct before that issue has been analyzed.

**Treatment:** Respond using the actual punitive standard and supported conduct, not a claim-label limitation. Do not turn the Rule 12(f) response into a concession about compensatory damages or treat a prior withdrawal reservation as automatic present entitlement.

### A21: Making binding-law research more restrictive than the law requires

**Location:** Count VI element list. **Verbatim:** “Identify a controlling pre-incident basis”

Pre-incident specificity is an important internal research check, but the phrase can become a demand for an indistinguishable case, or a statement that an argument for a nonfrivolous legal extension is forbidden. The merits question, clearly-established-law question, and factual-support question must remain distinct.

**Treatment:** Analyze the applicable QI test without announcing that absence of an identical PAS case necessarily defeats every relevant theory. Preserve only legally supportable arguments; Rule 11 expressly permits nonfrivolous extensions of existing law, which is not the same as saying such an extension defeats QI. ([Rule 11(b)(2)](https://www.law.cornell.edu/rules/frcp/rule_11))

### A22: Treating an internal audit as an unlimited permission to omit

**Location:** Concurrent synthesis's existing leakage table. **Verbatim:** “Do not mention toxicology or adverse transcript passages unless Defendants properly place them before the Court or the Court converts the motion under Rule 12(d)”

The anti-volunteering objective is sound, but the instruction is too categorical if read as a legal rule. A plaintiff's own necessary reliance, proper incorporation, an actual disclosure order or an obligation to avoid a misleading presentation can matter; proper consideration does not invariably require conversion, and “not raised” is not a universal withholding entitlement. ([Khoja](https://cdn.ca9.uscourts.gov/datastore/opinions/2018/08/13/16-56069.pdf); [Rule 11](https://www.law.cornell.edu/rules/frcp/rule_11))

**Treatment:** Use a relevance/record/duty gate, not a blanket ban. Do not continue advocating a known false proposition while waiting for amendment authorization, and do not describe private repository storage or an “internal” label as a privilege determination.

## What should not be removed as supposed leakage

- **The actual form wording:** Correct the physical-beverage misreading. That correction does not concede the truth of reported odor or the legality of any intrusion.
- **The actual warrant identity:** Do not knowingly call one document another. Determine proper court use before making an evidentiary submission.
- **The current FAC map:** Maintain accurate paragraphs and defendant attribution. Accuracy does not require admitting a disputed legal interpretation of the whole pleading.
- **The response to the no-charges premise:** Costanich and the operative Instruction 9.38 alternatives are plaintiff-serving analysis, not a concession that Count II must be abandoned. Respond to the motion's actual premise while identifying the distinct alleged deprivation. ([Costanich](https://www.courtlistener.com/opinion/180346/costanich-v-dept-of-social-and-health-services/); [Instruction 9.38](https://cdn.ca9.uscourts.gov/datastore/jury-instructions/civil/9-38-particular-rights-fourteenth-amendment-due-process-deliberate-fabrication-of-evidence.pdf))
- **Necessary materiality and QI analysis:** Both are raised. Avoid adverse factual assumptions and unneeded defense construction, not the governing standards themselves.
- **The narrow pro se fee correction:** Do not seek compensation for the plaintiff's own pro se time as though it were retained-counsel fees. Preserve other properly available relief without a gratuitous merits concession.
- **A truthful amendment alternative:** “If the Court finds a defect, leave would permit the following supported clarification” is not the same as “the Court should dismiss because we admit the defect.”

The following are proposed synthesis, not quotations from a filed paper. Use only with the correct pleaded facts and verified law.

These replacements are framing options, not guarantees that an allegation is sufficient or permission to avoid a necessary element. The source analysis remains available for deciding whether the proposed sentence is supportable.

## Publication gate and implementation

Before moving a paragraph from research into the opposition, record the motion section it answers, the issue it advances, its factual basis, the proper record mechanism, and whether it contains an admission. If the only benefit is teaching an unraised defense or supplying an adverse factual finding, keep it internal unless an applicable duty or concrete strategic need requires otherwise.
