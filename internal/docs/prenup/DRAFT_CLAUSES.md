# Working Clause Language (draft — not reviewed by counsel)

## Article 4 — Litigation Recoveries

4.1 **Separate Property.** All right, title and interest of [CLIENT] in every claim, cause of action, charge, complaint,
appeal or proceeding arising from employment, application for employment, or termination, including without limitation
claims premised on Penal Code section 290.46, subdivision (j), and the matters identified on Exhibit A (the
"Litigation"), together with every judgment, award, settlement, fee or cost award, sanction, interest, and other
proceeds of the Litigation, whenever the claim accrues and whenever paid, shall be and remain the separate property of
[CLIENT], notwithstanding that any portion compensates for wages, earnings, or harm attributable to any period during
the marriage.

4.2 **Waiver of Reimbursement.** [SPOUSE] waives any community claim or right of reimbursement for community funds, time,
effort, or labor expended in prosecuting, settling, or collecting the Litigation.

## Article 5 — Litigation-Derived Investments

5.1 **Separate Property.** All assets acquired with proceeds of the Litigation, including digital assets and
cryptocurrency held in the accounts and wallets listed on Exhibit B or any successor account or wallet (the "Litigation
Investments"), and all rents, issues, profits, appreciation, staking or yield rewards, airdrops, forks, and proceeds of
them, shall be the separate property of [CLIENT], regardless of the time, skill, effort, or labor of either party
during the marriage. The parties expressly waive any apportionment under *Pereira v. Pereira* or *Van Camp v. Van Camp*
and any similar doctrine.

5.2 **Segregation.** [CLIENT] shall hold Litigation Investments in accounts and wallets titled or controlled solely by
[CLIENT]. Commingling shall not transmute any Litigation Investment. Any community funds deposited shall create, at
most, a reimbursement claim for the dollar amount deposited, without interest or appreciation.

## Article 6 — Designated Real Property (1515 Cat Tail Drive)

6.1 **Separate Property.** If [CLIENT] acquires any interest in the real property commonly known as 1515 Cat Tail Drive,
Stockton, California 95204 (APN [________]) (the "Property"), whether before or during the marriage, and whether
acquired with separate funds, Litigation proceeds, community funds, credit, or any combination, the Property and all
rents, issues, profits, appreciation, and proceeds of sale or refinance, and any replacement property, shall be the sole
and separate property of [CLIENT].

6.2 **Title.** Title shall vest in "[CLIENT], a married man, as his sole and separate property." [SPOUSE] shall sign
any interspousal transfer deed, quitclaim, or lender acknowledgment reasonably requested at acquisition or refinance to
confirm this. Her signature confirms this Article and does not transfer any interest from her.

6.3 **Waiver of Apportionment.** [SPOUSE] waives any community or separate interest in the Property arising from
community or her separate funds or labor applied to the down payment, mortgage principal, improvements, taxes, or
insurance, including any pro tanto interest under *In re Marriage of Moore* or *In re Marriage of Marsden*, and any
reimbursement under Family Code section 2640.

6.4 **Optional fallback (choose one):**
- [ ] **No reimbursement.** Community payments toward the Property are treated as contributions to the marital household
  and create no claim.
- [ ] **Dollar-for-dollar reimbursement.** [SPOUSE]'s separate funds, or her share of community funds, applied to
  principal or capital improvements are reimbursed at face value, with no interest and no share of appreciation.

6.5 **Residence.** If the Property is the marital residence, this Article governs ownership only. It does not limit any
court order about temporary use or occupancy during a dissolution proceeding.

## Article 9 — Travel Funds

9.1 No community or jointly held funds shall be used for international travel, including any travel to Jamaica, without
the prior written consent of both parties. Any such expenditure without consent shall be charged against the spending
party's share of the community estate on division. Nothing in this section limits either party's use of separate
property or personal freedom of movement.

## Article 10 — Mutual Non-Disparagement (enforceability uncertain; severable)

10.1 Neither party shall publish, post, or communicate to the press, to social media, or to the other party's employer,
clients, or professional contacts any statement intended to humiliate or disparage the other party. This Article
creates no forfeiture of property and does not affect classification or division of any asset. It is severable.

## Schedule C — Statement of Mutual Expectations (non-binding)

The parties state, as a moral commitment only and not as an enforceable obligation, that each will treat the other with
respect in public and private, and will refrain from triangulation, manipulation, and similar psychological tactics.
This Schedule creates no legal obligation, no property consequence, and no ground for relief. It is severable.

## Article 14 — Severability

If any provision is held unenforceable, the remainder shall be enforced. Articles 4, 5, 6, and 9 are independent of
Article 10 and Schedule C.
