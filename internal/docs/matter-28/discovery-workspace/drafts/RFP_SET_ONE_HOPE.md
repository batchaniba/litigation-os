## Requests for production under Rule 34

Plaintiff Brendan Ngehsi Newanforbi propounds these requests under Federal Rules of Civil Procedure 26 and 34 to Defendant HOPE Program, sued as HOPE Psychotherapy, Inc., d/b/a HOPE Program (“HOPE”).

### Definitions

1. **PLAINTIFF** means Brendan Ngehsi Newanforbi.
2. **HOPE** or **YOU** means the responding Defendant and its officers, employees, clinicians, contractors, agents, record custodians, and other persons acting on its behalf.
3. **SMITH** means Defendant Shannon Smith, LCSW.
4. **PALACIOS** means Katie Palacios.
5. **CDCR/DAPO** means the California Department of Corrections and Rehabilitation, Division of Adult Parole Operations, and their officers, employees, systems, and contractors.
6. **CTM** means any case, clinical, containment, treatment, or team meeting at which PLAINTIFF's treatment, progress, attendance, risk, supervision, retention, graduation, discharge, or aftercare was discussed.
7. **TREATANYONE** means the electronic-health-record or practice-management system used by HOPE, including audit data and successor systems.
8. **ARMS** means the CDCR Automated Reentry Management System and any linked or successor system.
9. **COMMUNICATION** includes email, text message, messaging-platform content, letter, memorandum, meeting note, voicemail, and any other transmission of information.
10. **DOCUMENT** has the full scope of Rule 34(a)(1) and includes electronically stored information, native files, attachments, metadata, database records, audio, and video.
11. **IDENTIFY**, when used for a document, means state its date, author, recipients, title or subject, document type, custodian, and Bates number or production location.
12. Unless a request states otherwise, the relevant period is **January 1, 2021 through the date of production**.

### Instructions

1. Produce responsive material in the form ordinarily maintained or in a reasonably usable form. Produce email with attachments and material metadata; produce spreadsheets and database exports in native form when usability or metadata matters.
2. Organize and label the production to correspond to each request or produce it as kept in the usual course of business.
3. For every objection, state whether responsive material is being withheld. Produce all nonobjected-to material.
4. If responsive material no longer exists, identify what existed, its former custodian, when and why it became unavailable, and any available copy.
5. Preserve family relationships between messages and attachments and include load or audit data sufficient to identify creation, modification, author, custodian, and transmission where available.
6. Redact protected third-party information rather than withholding an otherwise responsive document when a narrower production is feasible.
7. Produce a privilege log on the schedule required by Judge Cisneros's standing order, absent agreement or further order.
8. These requests are continuing under Rule 26(e).
