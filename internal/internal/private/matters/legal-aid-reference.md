# MODULE 4 — Maryland DV Legal-Aid and Advocacy REFERENCE Directory
*Public information only. Reference directory for Maryland counsel — NOT a contact list for this file. No outreach to any listed organization is authorized in connection with Matter 44.*

## 1. Maryland Legal Aid (MLA)
- **What:** Statewide nonprofit providing free civil legal services to financially eligible Marylanders (12 offices). Handles family law including domestic violence and protective orders, plus housing, benefits, consumer, and elder law.
- **Intake door:** https://www.mdlab.org/get-help-services/ — phone intake 1-888-465-2468; online intake; walk-in clinics. Financial eligibility generally ≤125% of federal poverty guidelines.
- **Needs served:** Family law ✓ / Protective orders ✓ / Immigration ✗ — MLA's own FAQ states it does **not** handle immigration cases. Flag: the U-visa track (Module 1) needs a separate immigration-legal-aid referral.
- Source: mdlab.org "Get Help & Services" page.

## 2. Women's Law Center of Maryland (WLC)
- **What:** Nonprofit providing holistic legal services to survivors of domestic violence: direct representation plus hotlines and advocacy. Flagship **CLAS Project** (Collateral Legal Assistance for Survivors) offers full representation to IPV survivors in divorce, custody, child support, and protective-order matters.
- **Intake door:** https://wlcmd.org — Family Law Hotline (weekdays, generally 9:30 a.m.–4:30 p.m.); Employment Law Hotline 1-877-422-9500 (advice only, no representation).
- **Needs served:** Family law ✓ (full rep via CLAS, income/eligibility screening applies) / Protective orders ✓ / Immigration ✗ (not an immigration provider).
- Sources: wlcmd.org; People's Law Library org listing (peoples-law.org).

## 3. House of Ruth Maryland
- **What:** DV victim-service provider (not a law firm): 24-hour hotline, emergency shelter, counseling — plus a **Domestic Violence Legal Clinic** offering legal advice and possible free representation for protective orders, divorce, custody, and child support for IPV victims.
- **Intake door:** https://www.hruth.org (older domain hruthmd.org also appears) — 24-hour hotline 410-889-7884; Legal Clinic 410-554-8463; out-of-area 888-880-7884.
- **Needs served:** Family law ✓ (clinic) / Protective orders ✓ (clinic; courthouse-based assistance in Baltimore City) / Immigration ✗.
- Eligibility note: legal-clinic services described for persons physically abused by an intimate partner. Sources: hruth.org; icarol resource listings.

## 4. Maryland Network Against Domestic Violence (MNADV)
- **What:** The federally designated **statewide domestic-violence coalition** — policy advocacy, training, and technical assistance for member victim-service programs. It is NOT a direct legal-services provider.
- **Intake door:** https://www.mnadv.org — use as a referral/umbrella resource to find local member programs, not as a legal-intake door.
- **Needs served:** Family law ✗ (no direct services) / Protective orders ✗ (no direct services) / Immigration ✗. Value here is systemic: training, fatality-review data, legislative advocacy.
- Source: mnadv.org; coalition testimony on mgaleg.maryland.gov.

## 5. Maryland Volunteer Lawyers Service (MVLS)
- **What:** Pro bono referral network matching income-eligible Marylanders with volunteer attorneys for the life of the case. Handles family law and other civil matters.
- **Intake door:** https://mvlslaw.org/free-legal-help/ — call Monday–Thursday 9 a.m.–12 p.m. or apply online; **family-law cases must complete intake by phone**. Income eligibility: gross household income ≤50% of Maryland median income. Serves every county EXCEPT Allegany, Caroline, Dorchester, Kent, Montgomery, Prince George's, Queen Anne, and Talbot (with some exceptions).
- **Needs served:** Family law ✓ / Protective orders ✓ (case-type dependent) / Immigration ✗ (not listed as a practice area).
- Source: mvlslaw.org "Free Legal Help."

## 6. Maryland Judiciary — Court Help Center
- **What:** Judiciary-run free legal help for **self-represented litigants** in civil cases. Staff attorneys give legal information/advice on protective and peace orders, divorce, custody, child support, guardianship, and other civil matters — by phone, live chat, and walk-in centers at courthouses statewide.
- **Intake door:** https://www.mdcourts.gov/helpcenter — phone **410-260-1392** and live chat, 8:30 a.m.–8:00 p.m., Monday–Friday (excluding court holidays); walk-in centers during courthouse hours.
- **Needs served:** Family law ✓ (advice/self-help) / Protective orders ✓ (advice/self-help) / Immigration ✗.
- **Hard limit:** Center lawyers **do not represent** anyone in court — advice and procedure only.
- Source: mdcourts.gov.

## Cross-cutting notes for Matter 44
- **Immigration gap:** None of the six organizations above handles immigration matters. Any U-visa/VAWA track (see Module 1) requires a separate qualified immigration-counsel referral — flag for counsel to source.
- **MNADV is not an intake door:** Do not list it alongside the others as a place to call for representation; it is the coalition umbrella.
- **Eligibility is per-program:** Each organization applies its own income, geography, and case-type screens — confirm at intake; do not assume eligibility.
