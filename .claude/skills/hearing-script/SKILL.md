# Zoom / court speaking notes

The user appears in propria persona, often on Zoom. The camera is on his face.
Long paragraphs, "If they say…" headings, and "Your Honor, the Attorney General
asks…" are a brief. If he reads them, the Court hears a script and sees him
looking down.

"Script" in his request never means a teleprompter. It means **glanceable notes**.

## Default deliverable

When he asks for a script, the **chat itself** is the Zoom card. One screen. No
PDF unless he asks. No memo unless he asks for homework.

If he also wants the full argument, that is a second artifact, labeled
`DO NOT OPEN ON CAMERA`. Headings like "If the AG contests count one" belong
only there.

If the hearing is today, live, or "about to start": Zoom card only. Stop.

## Zoom card (what may be in front of him on camera)

Looks like a sticky on the monitor bezel, not a pleading.

- No word "script," "read this," "opening," "Point A," "If they say," or "Q. / A."
- No complete "Your Honor, …" sentences. Those get recited.
- No string cites. One case name only if he already knows it cold.
- Opening: at most four short sentences, written so he can say them **without
  looking down**. Put those first, then a blank line, then cues.
- Cues: 3–6 words each, exhibit numbers, statute subdivisions
  (`Ex. 7 — 851.6 certificate`, `679.10(k) — no charge needed`).
- One-line ask at the bottom he can memorize.
- First person. His voice.
- Fit on one phone screen or one laptop window without scrolling. If it
  scrolls, it is too long. Cut it.

On Zoom, looking down to hunt for the matching "If they say" paragraph is
visible. Cues exist so he can glance and look back at the camera.

## Forbidden in anything he might have open on camera

- "If they say…" / "If the Court asks…" / "If the Attorney General contests…"
- Numbered rebuttal menus ("Point A / Point B / Point C")
- Full paragraphs indented as speech
- Case strings (*Blank*, *Redfearn*, *Thompson* in a row)
- Stage directions ("read this," "submit on the tentative," "do not say")
- A six-page PDF

Stage directions and traps go in the off-camera memo, or not at all if he did
not ask for homework.

## Worked example — Matter 20 demurrer, August 28, 2026

Retrospective. Hearing already held. This is the Zoom card he should have
had on camera. The six-page "If they say" memo is what not to do.

```
I submit on one and four.
Not asking you to order Form B signed today.
Overrule those two counts and set an answer date.

1 and 4 — live
2 and 3 — leave them
Ex. 5 vs Ex. 6 — conflict
Ex. 7 — 851.6, no complaint
can + PAS ≠ DUI on demurrer
679.10(k) — charge not required
Serrato signed the denial
Paul / Dutra — amend if thin

Ask: overrule 1 and 4. Answer date. CMC September 2.
```

## Chat while a hearing is live

Two or three spoken sentences. No labels. No menu of hypotheticals. Stop.

## After the hearing

Do not invent the ruling. Retire the calendar entry as held. Record "minute
order not yet in hand" until he supplies the order or a specific holding.
