To be eligible for the Autonomous Vehicle Operator role, you must meet all driver’s
license requirements below. 
1) You must have a current, valid driver’s license
Your driver’s license must be valid and not expired on:
The day you take the driving eval, and
your start date (or hire date, if applicable)
If your license is expired or not valid at the time of verification, we won’t be
able to proceed until it is resolved.
You must bring your current license with you to the driving evaluation 
2) You must have 3 consecutive years of licensure (no gaps)
You must be able to show that you have held a driver’s license for the three (3)
consecutive years immediately prior to your hire/start date.
“No gaps” means: there cannot be any period in those 3 years where you did not
hold a valid driver’s license (for example, a lapse, cancellation, suspension,
revocation, or an expired period).
3) Issuing State
In California, you will need a valid California driver’s license
In Texas, you will need a valid Texas driver’s license. If you have recently moved to
Texas, you must obtain a Texas driver’s license within 90 days of move date
4) You may need to provide additional documentation
You may be asked to provide extra documentation if:
your current license was issued less than 3 years before your hire/start date, or
you recently moved states, replaced your license, changed your name, or your
licensing history is otherwise unclear.
5) Examples of acceptable documents (if requested)
If we request documentation, acceptable examples include:
an official DMV driving record / Motor Vehicle Report (MVR), and/or
a DMV letter/history showing your licensing dates and status, and/or
a prior driver’s license (front/back) that covers the needed time period, and/or
temporary/interim license documentation that shows you remained licensed
during a renewal period.
We can’t accept verbal confirmation alone, and insurance cards generally don’t
prove license history.
