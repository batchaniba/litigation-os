                                       Brief Statement of the Case (Continued)


        On April 9, 2026, Petitioner submitted a formal request for Form I-918, Supplement B U
        Nonimmigrant Status Certification, together with extensive supporting evidence including a
        dashcam transcript, CHP arrest report, and CHP’s own § 851.6 Detention Certificate
        reclassifying the arrest as “a detention only” for “insufficient grounds.”

        On April 20, 2026, Respondent G. Serrato issued a one-page denial letter that is facially
        deficient under § 679.10(g) because it fails to provide “specific reasons” for the denial, does not
        identify which statutory element Petitioner allegedly fails, does not address any enumerated
        qualifying offense, does not engage with the dashcam evidence, does not reference the § 851.6
        certificate, and does not apply the rebuttable presumption of helpfulness under § 679.10(h).

        Relief sought: Peremptory writ of mandate vacating the April 20 denial; order compelling
        reconsideration under correct legal standards; compliance with § 679.10(g) reason-giving
        requirement; declaratory relief. No money damages are sought in this action (separate § 1983
        action pending).
