# Employment Damages Valuation Framework

**Workshopped:** October 13, 2026
**Status:** Planning framework — aggressive columns are planning-only and are never pleaded in a complaint.
**Matters covered (8):** Amazon (M29), UPS (M26), Home Depot (M37), Best Buy (M39), AT&T (M36), FedEx (M25), Staples (M42), Accurate (M29 co-defendant).

---

## The structure (all cases)

**C (compensatory pool)** = back pay + front pay + benefits (15%) + emotional distress

**Loaded** = C + registry additional (1× C) + exemplary (1× C) = 3 × C

**Opening demand** = 1.33 × working loaded

**Settlement target** = 32–53% of working loaded

Negotiation rule: concede the multiples, hold C.

### Layer details

- **Back pay / front pay.** Young-case frame: 52 weeks + 52 weeks future. Mature-case frame: 104 + 104. Staples models back pay through assumed judgment (2 years out) plus 2 years front.
- **Benefits at 15%** of wage loss — conservative against the ~30% BLS private-sector benchmark.
- **Emotional distress** is case-specific ($50,000–$125,000 working across the docket).
- **Registry additional (1× working / 3× high)** per Penal Code § 290.46(j)(4)(A)'s discretionary additional award.
- **Exemplary (1× working / 2× high)** — single-digit ratios sit inside the *State Farm v. Campbell* due-process safe harbor.
- **Civil-penalty route** (C + additional + $25,000) is the alternative where the statute offers exemplary *or* civil penalty — never stacked.
- **FCRA willful (§ 1681n) + ICRAA (§ 1786.50) layer:** $25,000–$50,000 working / $75,000–$150,000 high.
- **(j)(4)(A) floor:** the greater of actual damages or $25,000 per unlawful use.

### Case adaptations

- **AT&T (M36):** valued ex-§ 290.46 per instruction — C plus the FCRA/ICRAA statutory layer only, no registry/exemplary multiples.
- **FedEx (M25):** floor-based model rebuilt on the loaded structure using the September 25, 2026 offer's wage base ($51,435 back + $42,120 front). The $195,000 offer's 14-day term expired October 9, 2026 unaccepted; posture resets.
- **Home Depot (M37):** no civil-penalty row — expressly disclaimed in the verified complaint (¶41).
- **Accurate (M29):** standalone ICRAA/FCRA statutory range plus joint § 290.46 exposure on Amazon's compensatory pool; no separate demand — rides with Amazon.
- **Staples (M42):** the baseline from which the 1.33× / 32–53% ratios derive ($1.25M demand on $936,259 loaded; $300–500k target). Pre-dispute as of this writing — demand awaits confirmation of the loss.

## Caveats

- Every demand must be justifiable on its own facts the day defense counsel asks "says who?" — mechanical consistency across the docket is not a substitute for case-specific credibility.
- Distress and front-pay cells are the softest in every model; the registry 1×/3× multiplier is the load-bearing legal assumption behind all anchors.
- Flagged inputs: Best Buy hours (40/wk assumed — offer letter silent), Staples hours (40/wk assumed), AT&T hours (30/wk assumed) and prorated variable comp, Staples loss-start premise (Oct 5, 2026) ahead of a confirmed adverse action.
