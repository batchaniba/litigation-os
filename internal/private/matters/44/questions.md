# Open questions

## Consent and goals

- Has Njiubu asked for or consented to this planning?
- What does she want now: safety, no contact, divorce, support, property relief,
  criminal accountability, compensation, counseling, immigration help, or a
  combination?
- What is a safe way and time to communicate with her?
- Is she represented, and has she signed any agreement or release?

## Safety and recency

- Is anyone in immediate danger?
- What is the most recent alleged incident, with exact date, county, conduct,
  injury, threat, witness, and record?
- Are there firearms, stalking, digital monitoring, threats, or access to her
  home/work?
- Are children, elders, dependent adults, pets, or third parties at risk?
- Have police, medical providers, advocates, or courts been involved?

## Jurisdiction and divorce

- In which Maryland county does each spouse reside, and for how long?
- Marriage date/place; exact separation date; separate residences?
- Any prior or pending case in Maryland, another state, or another country?
- Minor/dependent children or support/custody issues?
- Assets, debts, income, retirement, real estate, businesses, insurance, taxes,
  transfers, and urgent financial needs?

## Civil and criminal screening

- For each alleged act: date, location, specific conduct, injury, knowledge,
  delay explanation, witnesses, proof, report, treatment, and loss?
- Was any conduct concealed or was Njiubu prevented from acting, and how?
- Which incidents occurred within the last year and last three years?
- Any written or recorded admission, threat, apology, demand, or settlement?
- Any reason a different jurisdiction's law may apply?
- What collectible assets or actual insurance exist?

## Capacity and special remedies

- Does age, disability, incapacity, dependency, or caretaker status implicate
  vulnerable-adult protection?
- Does immigration status make VAWA, U-visa, or other specialist review useful?
- Does present firearm danger support an ERPO screen?
- Are estate, beneficiary, tax, housing, public-benefit, or employment issues
  likely to change with separation or divorce?
