 1                        MEMORANDUM OF POINTS AND AUTHORITIES

 2         Respondent Eric Dutra (“Sgt. Dutra”) joins in Respondent California Highway Patrol’s

 3   (“CHP”) Demurrer to the Petition for Writ of Mandate as to all causes of action in the petition on

 4   the ground that the petition does not state facts sufficient to constitute a cause of action. (See

 5   Code Civ. Proc. § 430.10, subd. (e).)

 6         Specifically, CHP demurs to the Petition for these reasons: (1) CHP possesses no

 7   ministerial duty to certify Petitioner’s Form I-918, Supplement B, U Nonimmigrant Status

 8   Certification (“Form B”) (see CHP’s Demurrer at 11-13); (2) Petitioner has no clear, present and

 9   beneficial right to the performance of a ministerial duty (see CHP’s Demurrer at 13-15); (3) CHP

10   has no ministerial duty to provide a written explanation for its certification denial in the form

11   demanded by Petitioner (see CHP Demurrer at 15-16); (4) the rebuttable presumption of

12   helpfulness is not applicable on the facts alleged (see CHP’s Demurrer at 16-17); and (5)

13   Petitioner is not entitled to his requested declaratory relief because he seeks advisory opinions on

14   purely legal questions, declaratory relief cannot unwind a past administrative decision, and

15   because Petitioner is not entitled to a writ of ordinary mandate, mooting the declaration Petitioner

16   seeks (see CHP’s Demurrer at 17-20).

17         These reasons are relevant to Sgt. Dutra’s defenses and demurrer because the basis for

18   Petitioner’s requested relief, as articulated in his petition, is the same as to all Respondents,

19   including the individual Respondents. Further, the relief sought by CHP would benefit Sgt.

20   Dutra, as Petitioner alleges that Sgt. Dutra is a “certifying official” within the meaning of Penal

21   Code section 679.10(b). (Pet., ¶ 16.) For these reasons, Sgt. Dutra joins CHP’s demurrer to the

22   Petition for Writ of Mandate as to all causes of action in the petition, and adopts the points and

23   authorities filed in support of CHP’s Demurrer..

24         Sgt. Dutra demurs to the petition for one additional reason: Petitioner has not stated facts

25   sufficient to allege that Sgt. Dutra is a “certifying official” within the meaning of Penal Code

26   section 679.10(b). Specifically, the petition alleges that Sgt. Dutra “the assigned investigator and

27   designated recipient on the Supplement B.” (Pet., ¶ 16.) However, Section 679.10(b) provides

28   that a certifying official is (1) the head of the certifying entity; (2) a person in a supervisory role
                                                         2
                                                             Memorandum of Points and Authorities (26CV191136)
 1   who has been specifically designated by the head of the certifying entity to issue Form I-918

 2   Supplement B certifications on behalf of that agency; (3) a judge; and (4) any other certifying

 3   official defined under Section 214.14(a)(2) of the Title 8 of the Code of Federal Regulations.

 4   (See Penal Code § 679.10(b)(1), (b)(2), (b)(3), (b)(4).) Petitioner thus fails to allege that Sgt.

 5   Dutra falls into any of these four categories; accordingly, Sgt. Dutra is not a proper respondent for

 6   the relief requested by Petitioner.

 7         Sgt. Dutra therefore respectfully requests that the Court sustain his demurrer without leave

 8   to amend.
