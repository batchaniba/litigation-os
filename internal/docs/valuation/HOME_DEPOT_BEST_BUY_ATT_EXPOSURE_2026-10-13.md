## Why the earlier parity was wrong

Identical two-year future back-pay and front-pay horizons applied to all three cases made AT&T appear comparable to Best Buy and Home Depot. AT&T's loss started May 28, 2026. Best Buy's loss started October 17, 2023. The accrued column is the controlling difference. Future components are retained for stress testing but are shortened so they do not erase elapsed time.

## Proof status and exclusions

- Best Buy: written offer at $22.60, acceptance, drug screen (clean on information and belief), then ghosted. No report, no pre-adverse letter, hours not confirmed, arbitration clause noted.
- AT&T: written offer at $24.95, acceptance, same-day rescission over Spanish proficiency. Strongest non-registry paper. Registry and FCRA remain information and belief until the report is produced. Variable-comp target not modeled.
- Home Depot: no offer, no rate, no report. Pure information-and-belief on report contents and use. $22 proxy is illustrative only.
- Civil-penalty and exemplary routes are alternatives, not stacked.
