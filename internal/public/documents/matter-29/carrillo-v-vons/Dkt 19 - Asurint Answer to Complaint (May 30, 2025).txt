 5                                    SUMMARY OF ACTION
 6    COMPLAINT ¶1:
 7          Plaintiff, individually and on behalf of all others similarly situated, brings this
 8    action for damages and other legal and equitable remedies, based on: (i) Vons’ unlawful
 9    and unfair conduct in using information disclosed in the California Department of Justice
10    Megan’s Law Website (“Megan’s Law Website”) for employment purposes; (ii) Vons’
11    failure to conduct an individualized assessment of whether Plaintiff’s conviction has a
12    direct and adverse relationship with the specific duties of an Vons deli clerk that justified
13    denying him the position; and (iii) Asurint’s unlawful and unfair conduct in furnishing
14    consumer background reports to Vons while knowing that Vons unfairly and unlawfully
15    uses information contained in the consumer reports.
16    ANSWER:
17          With respect to those allegations directed toward Asurint, Asurint admits only that
18    Plaintiff purports to bring claims for “damages and other legal and equitable remedies” in
19    connection with a consumer report that Asurint prepared regarding Plaintiff at the request
20    of Vons. Asurint states that the referenced report is the best evidence of its contents and
21    denies any allegations in Paragraph 1 of the Complaint that are inconsistent with the
22    referenced report. Asurint further denies that it violated any laws, and denies that
23    Plaintiff or any potential putative class member is entitled to any damages whatsoever.
24    Asurint denies any remaining allegations in Paragraph 1 of the Complaint to the extent
25    they are directed toward Asurint, and specifically denies that class treatment is
26    appropriate in this action.

 2                          AFFIRMATIVE AND OTHER DEFENSES
 3          Without assuming the burden of proof where it otherwise rests with Plaintiff,
 4    Asurint asserts the following affirmative and other defenses to the Complaint, and
 5    reserves its right to raise additional defenses as may be discovered during the course of
 6    these proceedings:
 7                                        FIRST DEFENSE
 8          Any damages sustained by Plaintiff and/or putative class members were not
 9    proximately caused by Asurint.
10                                      SECOND DEFENSE
11          Asurint consistently followed reasonable procedures to assure maximum possible
12    accuracy in the information concerning Plaintiff and all other individuals on which it has
13    prepared consumer reports.
14                                       THIRD DEFENSE
15          Plaintiff’s claims and/or the putative class members’ claims are barred in whole or
16    in part to the extent they occurred and/or accrued outside of the applicable statute of
17    limitations.
18                                      FOURTH DEFENSE
19          To the extent Plaintiff’s individual or purported classes include individuals who
20    suffered no concrete harm, such individuals lack standing to bring suit against Asurint.
21                                        FIFTH DEFENSE
22          Asurint is not subject to general or specific personal jurisdiction in this Court as to
23    the claims belonging to some or all members of the putative class defined in the
24    Complaint.
25                                        SIXTH DEFENSE
26          This Court is not the proper venue as to the claims belonging to some or all
27    members of the putative class defined in the Complaint.
