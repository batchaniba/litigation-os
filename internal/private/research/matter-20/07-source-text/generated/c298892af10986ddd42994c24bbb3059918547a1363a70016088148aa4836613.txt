 1                        MEMORANDUM OF POINTS AND AUTHORITIES

 2         Respondent Gerardo Serrato (“Capt. Serrato”) joins in Respondent California Highway

 3   Patrol’s (“CHP”) Demurrer to the Petition for Writ of Mandate as to all causes of action in the

 4   petition on the ground that the petition does not state facts sufficient to constitute a cause of

 5   action. (See Code Civ. Proc. § 430.10, subd. (e).)

 6         Specifically, CHP demurs to the Petition for these reasons: (1) CHP possesses no

 7   ministerial duty to certify Petitioner’s Form I-918, Supplement B, U Nonimmigrant Status

 8   Certification (“Form B”) (see CHP’s Demurrer at 11-13); (2) Petitioner has no clear, present and

 9   beneficial right to the performance of a ministerial duty (see CHP’s Demurrer at 13-15); (3) CHP

10   has no ministerial duty to provide a written explanation for its certification denial in the form

11   demanded by Petitioner (see CHP Demurrer at 15-16); (4) the rebuttable presumption of

12   helpfulness is not applicable on the facts alleged (see CHP’s Demurrer at 16-17); and (5)

13   Petitioner is not entitled to his requested declaratory relief because he seeks advisory opinions on

14   purely legal questions, declaratory relief cannot unwind a past administrative decision, and

15   because Petitioner is not entitled to a writ of ordinary mandate, mooting the declaration Petitioner

16   seeks (see CHP’s Demurrer at 17-20).

17         These reasons are relevant to Capt. Serrato’s defenses and demurrer because the basis for

18   Petitioner’s requested relief, as articulated in his petition, is the same as to all Respondents,

19   including the individual Respondents. Further, the relief sought by CHP would benefit Capt.

20   Serrato, as Petitioner alleges that Capt. Serrato is a “certifying official” within the meaning of

21   Penal Code section 679.10(b) and has attached to his petition the certification denial letter signed

22   by Capt. Serrato. For these reasons, Capt. Serrato joins CHP’s demurrer to the Petition for Writ

23   of Mandate as to all causes of action in the petition, and adopts the points and authorities filed in

24   support of CHP’s Demurrer.

25         Capt. Serrato therefore respectfully requests that the Court sustain his demurrer without

26   leave to amend.
