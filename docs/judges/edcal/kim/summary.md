## Rules (S2)
- Motions and oppositions are limited to 20 pages and replies to 10. Sur-replies and supplemental briefs filed without leave "will not be considered." Requests for more pages are due 7 days before filing.
- **Remote (Zoom):**
  - For an Initial Scheduling Conference, remote appearance is generally permitted if counsel or the pro se party is outside the Sacramento region.
  - Otherwise it is disfavored absent hardship. "A pro se party without means to travel to the courthouse" is one of the examples given. Scheduling conflicts are insufficient.
  - File a written request at least 7 days ahead, after giving courtesy notice to the other parties.
  - There are no hybrid hearings. Video and audio must be on. Virtual backgrounds, avatars, and AI tools are barred.
- **Discovery:**
  - Where all parties are represented, conferral must be in person, by phone, or by video.
  - L.R. 251 joint statement limited to 20 pages; a 251(d) affidavit to 15 pages; 251(e) motions 20/20/10.
  - Informal telephonic conference: contact the CRD with dates 3 to 14 days out. Joint letter of 2 pages; exhibits are discouraged and capped at 5 pages. Due by 10:00 am PT two business days before the conference.
- Proposed orders in Word to CSKorders@caed.uscourts.gov, with "proposed order" and the case number in the subject line.
- "Parties, including parties proceeding without an attorney, should refrain from emailing Judge Kim or her staff except as provided."

  - Kim pairs screening with IFP scrutiny. In Robinson she recommended IFP be **denied** and the complaint dismissed **with** 30 days' leave to amend, with the fee due when the amended complaint is filed.
  - In Jones, a prisoner (PC) case, she granted IFP but recommended dismissal **without** leave for lack of jurisdiction.

## Leave to amend
- Kim grants leave when defects look curable (Robinson) and denies it for jurisdictional defects or futility (Jones; M07). See S5 and R1.
