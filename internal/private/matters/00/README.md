# Matter 0 — Ngwa Nforbi v. Ngwa Nforbi

## Posture

This is a private contingency workspace, not a lawsuit, not a decision to
divorce, and not part of the public litigation portfolio. It exists to improve
judgment before a crisis: preserve the marriage where possible, understand the
facts, stabilize finances and housing, and prepare an orderly path if separation
or dissolution becomes necessary.

## Operating tracks

### 1. Preserve

- Define what a workable marriage would look like.
- Identify recurring conflict patterns, repair attempts, and measurable changes.
- Build a communication and counseling plan if both spouses are willing.
- Distinguish temporary stress from structural incompatibility.
- Set review dates rather than making major decisions during a peak conflict.

### 2. Understand

- Complete the factual baseline in `facts-and-chronology.md`.
- Build the redacted financial inventory in `financial-inventory.md`.
- Record material events without argument, diagnosis, or embellishment.
- Separate verified facts from assumptions and feared scenarios.

### 3. Stabilize

- Protect ordinary access to housing, transportation, income, medication,
  identification, and essential records.
- Maintain routine bills and preserve records.
- Do not move, hide, drain, or destroy property or information.
- Identify what would need immediate temporary agreement if living arrangements
  changed.

### 4. Prepare

- Work through `decision-gates.md` in order.
- Collect questions for a confidential California family-law consultation.
- Prefer negotiated structure or mediation where workable.
- Prepare litigation materials only after the facts, goals, jurisdiction, and
  immediate-relief needs are clear.

## Source discipline

Every substantive entry must carry one label:

- **FACT** — supported by a document or direct observation.
- **USER REPORT** — stated by the user but not independently documented.
- **INFERENCE** — a conclusion drawn from facts; explain the chain.
- **QUESTION** — unresolved and material.
- **RECOMMENDATION** — an action option, not a command or prediction.

Do not infer a spouse's motives, diagnoses, finances, relationships, or future
conduct.

## Privacy and discoverability

A private GitHub repository is not a privilege system. Commits and deleted
history may remain recoverable and litigation discovery may reach relevant
materials. Keep this workspace strategic and factual. Never store credentials,
full account numbers, Social Security numbers, private keys, intimate media,
minor children's identifiers, or raw communications that can instead be
described and located in a separate encrypted archive.
