# [ALTERNATIVE PROPOSED] ORDER FOR FOCUSED MERITS FACTFINDING ON FIRST CAUSE OF ACTION
Superior Court of California, Alameda County — Case No. 26CV191136  
Newanforbi v. California Highway Patrol et al. — Hon. Jenna M. Whitman, Dept.25

The Court, having heard Petitioner's noticed motion and considered the parties' submissions, finds that the surviving First Cause of Action presents one or more factual disputes essential to evaluating the alleged qualifying unlawful personal restraint and the April 20, 2026 certification determination. The Court does not presently find that Petitioner was the victim of criminal false imprisonment or that the denial was an abuse of discretion.

Under Code of Civil Procedure sections 1090, 1091 and 1094 and the Court's applicable trial-management authority, **IT IS ORDERED**:

1. A trial-setting / evidentiary management conference is set for [DATE/TIME] in Dept.25. No merits writ is granted or denied in this procedural order.

2. Subject to final issue narrowing at that conference, material questions include:
  a. The location and condition of the stationary vehicle and whether a factual prerequisite of Vehicle Code section 40300.5(a)–(e) authorized warrantless misdemeanor DUI arrest without officer-observed driving;
  b. The relevant observations and circumstances actually known to the arresting officers before ~01:11–01:12 arrest, separate from later warrant and lab evidence;
  c. Whether the walk-and-turn test was declined by Petitioner or not administered at an officer's direction owing to unsafe terrain; if inaccurate, whether the report item was material to the factual arrest basis;
  d. Whether intentional compulsory restraint, lacking lawful privilege, constituted qualifying Penal Code section 236 activity or another genuinely applicable offense; and
  e. Whether a specific legal or materially erroneous factual premise infected the April 20 decision and, only as required, whether Petitioner's helpfulness meets section 679.10(g)–(h).

3. Within [___] days the parties shall meet and confer regarding contested facts, exact proposed stipulations, expected testimony of Melano and Meza-Gonzalez and any authorized certification reviewer, identification of CHP Form 202/DS367/CAD/log materials, scope of properly requested nonprivileged certification decision records, and authentic replay of the **native CHP recording** with stable timestamps and full context. Any request to subpoena a witness or challenge asserted privilege requires the otherwise prescribed motion or subpoena procedure; nothing here resolves privilege.
