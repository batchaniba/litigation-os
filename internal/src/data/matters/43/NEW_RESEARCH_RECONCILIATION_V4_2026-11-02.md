## Accepted into v4 complaint

Plead the **produced packet** as the non-standalone document; the native e-sign page boundary remains a proof target. A typo `The Herz Corporation` alone is not dispositive. *Gilberg v. California Check Cashing Stores*, 913 F.3d 1169 (9th Cir. 2019); Civ. Code §1786.16(a)(2)(B).
2. **State standing/remedy:** *Parsonage v. Wal-Mart Associates*, D083831 (Cal. Ct. App. 2026), holds no separate concrete injury necessary to seek the individual ICRAA §1786.50 statutory alternative in California court. The remedy is **actual damages or $10,000 whichever is greater**, not $10,000 multiplied by every page or subrequest. *Yeh v. Barrington Pacific*, B337904 (2026), consistent.
3. **Circumstantial registry use:** offer's "company standards" rescission term, registry adjudication contrasting `Client Review Required` and `Meets Company Standards`, employer-specific adjudication rules, DOJ/California registry notes citing §290.46, and subsequent preliminary eligibility notice form a factual basis for information-and-belief **employment use**. The notice actually specifies only alcohol-related information and the input may be from independent court/MVR sources. No final rescission or lost wages documented.
4. **Public source:** DOJ NSOPW vs. restricted NSOR distinction makes California public website origin plausible, but the report says only "through the U.S. Department of Justice"; actual feed, URL and transmission path require records.
5. **MVR:** current Class C status `VALID`, prior suspensions/disqualifications cleared, nonalcohol §4000(a) conviction shown. A valid license does not prove compliance with distinct driving-history standards, and administrative licensing restrictions are not themselves criminal convictions.

## Held out of v4 — why
- **Gemini unconditional-offer, forged excuse, proof of pretext:** the actual Oct. 22 written offer contains background contingencies; there is a legitimate MVR history and a court conviction. A preliminary `Alcohols related offenses` code does not itself prove fabrication or registry causation.
- **No-lose legal "pincer":** an independent MVR-driven assessment may be lawful and not trigger Government Code §12952 conviction-history duties, even when inaccurately described under a `Criminal Information` heading.
- **Parent Hertz Global Holdings as joint employer:** ADP WOTC name, shared branding and ATS are not sufficient proof of direct hiring decision/agency/alter ego.
- **FCRA federal counts:** separate federal form appears standalone; report and summary were delivered before final adverse action. An unfilled **California** Fair Chance deadline is not automatically an FCRA §1681b(b)(3) violation.
- **FCRA limbo:** Vibe paper acknowledges no published controlling deadline for employment decision. ERISA, insurance bad faith and APA agency-delay doctrines are only analogies, not a standalone FCRA cause. Preserve proof of practical exclusion if actual denial emerges.
- **Perceived alcohol-related disability:** nothing shows a perception of protected disability under FEHA.
- **Multiple $10k ICRAA awards or $25k per registry hit:** not established by text or cases.
- **Noncompete:** offer's required "Employee Confidentiality & Non-Competition" paperwork raises §16600.5(c)/(e) issue for prospective employees; no operative invalid restraint is yet shown; preserve without alleging cause now.
- **Labor Code §432.7:** DMV licensing history is not automatically an arrest-not-followed-by-conviction record.
- **FEHA Fair Chance count:** hold until CRD administrative prerequisites, a conviction-based preliminary adverse action and proof of material procedural defect; *McInerney v. Regents* (published Oct. 13, 2026) requires proof of harm for procedural-damages theory. No final decision currently evidenced.
- **No affirmative AI/automated-discrimination count:** Oct. 2025 regulations mandate preservation and forbid existing protected-trait discrimination; a ruleset name alone does not prove discriminatory automated hiring.
