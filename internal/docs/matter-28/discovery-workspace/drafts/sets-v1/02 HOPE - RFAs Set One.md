## REQUESTS FOR ADMISSION

**REQUEST FOR ADMISSION NO. 1:** During the RELEVANT PERIOD, HOPE provided sex-offender treatment to PLAINTIFF under a contract between HOPE and CDCR.

**REQUEST FOR ADMISSION NO. 2:** PLAINTIFF's participation in HOPE's program was a condition of his parole.

**REQUEST FOR ADMISSION NO. 3:** HOPE was certified by the California Sex Offender Management Board during the RELEVANT PERIOD.

**REQUEST FOR ADMISSION NO. 4:** HOPE was paid by CDCR for treatment services HOPE provided to PLAINTIFF.

**REQUEST FOR ADMISSION NO. 5:** HOPE reported PLAINTIFF's treatment status to CDCR at each CTM concerning PLAINTIFF.

**REQUEST FOR ADMISSION NO. 6:** At no CTM concerning PLAINTIFF did HOPE recommend that PLAINTIFF be found to have completed treatment.

**REQUEST FOR ADMISSION NO. 7:** Under HOPE's practice during the RELEVANT PERIOD, a parolee could not be found to have completed treatment without a HOPE recommendation communicated to CDCR.

**REQUEST FOR ADMISSION NO. 8:** Before October 2024, HOPE's policy was that a parolee on active parole supervision continued in treatment until parole ended.

**REQUEST FOR ADMISSION NO. 9:** Before October 2024, HOPE's treatment-completion policy for parolees followed a CDCR requirement.

**REQUEST FOR ADMISSION NO. 10:** HOPE's treatment-completion policy for parolees applied to all parolee clients at the STOCKTON SITE.

**REQUEST FOR ADMISSION NO. 11:** HOPE did not change any treatment-completion policy for parolees after April 17, 2025.

**REQUEST FOR ADMISSION NO. 12:** No HOPE parolee client at the STOCKTON SITE received a favorable COMPLETION DETERMINATION while on active parole supervision between January 1, 2021 and September 30, 2024.

**REQUEST FOR ADMISSION NO. 13:** HOPE's revenue for a parolee client ends when the client is discharged from HOPE's program.

**REQUEST FOR ADMISSION NO. 14:** HOPE characterized PLAINTIFF's homework as "partially completed" in at least one communication to CDCR.

**REQUEST FOR ADMISSION NO. 15:** HOPE did not provide PLAINTIFF a written explanation of the criteria he had to meet to complete treatment.

**REQUEST FOR ADMISSION NO. 16:** HOPE did not provide PLAINTIFF an opportunity to contest HOPE's COMPLETION DETERMINATIONS before they were reported to CDCR.

**REQUEST FOR ADMISSION NO. 17:** Before August 2025, PLAINTIFF complained to HOPE staff about HOPE's treatment-completion practices.

**REQUEST FOR ADMISSION NO. 18:** In 2025, a HOPE employee described PLAINTIFF to CDCR as "disruptive."

**REQUEST FOR ADMISSION NO. 19:** In 2025, a HOPE employee described PLAINTIFF to CDCR as a "ringleader."

**REQUEST FOR ADMISSION NO. 20:** In 2025, Katie Palacios stated, in substance, that when PLAINTIFF complains, other clients join in.

**REQUEST FOR ADMISSION NO. 21:** HOPE knew during the RELEVANT PERIOD that CDCR could treat a parolee's failure to attend HOPE sessions as a parole violation.

**REQUEST FOR ADMISSION NO. 22:** HOPE knew during the RELEVANT PERIOD that HOPE's reports to CDCR could affect the conditions of a parolee's supervision.

**REQUEST FOR ADMISSION NO. 23:** CTMs concerning PLAINTIFF were video recorded.

**REQUEST FOR ADMISSION NO. 24:** HOPE has not deleted or overwritten any recording of a CTM concerning PLAINTIFF since January 1, 2025.

**REQUEST FOR ADMISSION NO. 25:** Dr. Cristina Loree testified in the In re Dwyer habeas proceeding in San Joaquin County Superior Court.

**REQUEST FOR ADMISSION NO. 26:** HOPE was aware of the In re Dwyer proceeding before PLAINTIFF filed the SAN JOAQUIN ACTION.

**REQUEST FOR ADMISSION NO. 27:** Cal. Code Regs., tit. 15, section 3574(f), as amended effective April 17, 2025, requires that a Containment Team determination to retain a supervised person in a sex offender management program be supported by good cause and stated in writing, signed by both the sex offender treatment provider and the parole agent on CDCR Form 3043.

**REQUEST FOR ADMISSION NO. 28:** SMITH had final authority over HOPE's treatment-completion policies during the RELEVANT PERIOD.

**REQUEST FOR ADMISSION NO. 29:** HOPE's verified response to Special Interrogatory No. 7 in the SAN JOAQUIN ACTION stated that, before October 2024, CDCR policy required supervised persons to continue some form of treatment as part of their supervision.

**REQUEST FOR ADMISSION NO. 30:** HOPE's verified response to Special Interrogatory No. 38 in the SAN JOAQUIN ACTION stated that Responding Party "had no knowledge of Plaintiff's 'CDL employment obligations.'"

**REQUEST FOR ADMISSION NO. 31:** HOPE's response to Special Interrogatory No. 28 in the SAN JOAQUIN ACTION stated that HOPE had no knowledge of the Dwyer decision.

**REQUEST FOR ADMISSION NO. 32:** HOPE knew of PLAINTIFF's employment as a commercial driver before January 1, 2023.
