## C. JUDICIAL NOTICE / LODGING

- **Judicial notice (Evid. Code § 452):** Aug. 29, 2026 Order; Answer (as a pleading); statutes. No judicial notice is sought for disputed facts, for the federal screening order (omitted), or for any analyst's characterization.
- **Lodging:** Proposed judgments and writs are lodged SEPARATELY from the moving papers, with findings left for the Court (brackets only). Lodging is not admission. The notice of lodging, if used, must match the actual final papers, exhibit set, and service list — no "already on file" or "prepared to lodge" language that does not match reality.
- **What is NOT lodged as evidence:** Research memoranda (including this reconstruction), the 10-revised-packet/ and 12-review drafts, text sidecars as substitutes for original PDFs, and the branch manifest/inventory (administrative records, not evidence).
