## ⚠️ Chambers copies: the judge page and the SO conflict

| Source | Rule |
|---|---|
| **Civil SO § H.6 (Aug. 26, 2026)** | "Except for filings of less than **8 pages**, chambers copies of any filing must be provided within **two (2) days** of filing." The copy is double-sided, three-hole punched, and carries the ECF stamp on top. Exhibits are tabbed on the right; anything over 2 inches goes in a binder. Mark it "Chambers Copy" in an envelope addressed to "Judge Martínez-Olguín" with the case number, and deliver it to the Clerk's Office. Do not email filings to amocrd@. |
| **Judge page "Chambers Copy Requirements"** | "Except for administrative motions or stipulations, chambers copies of any filing must be provided within **three (3) days** of filing." |

**Safe course:** Meet the stricter combination. Deliver a chambers copy of every filing within **2 days**. That satisfies the SO for filings of 8 or more pages and the judge page for every non-administrative filing. The page itself states that "a judge's standing order supersedes the local rules," but it does not say whether the SO supersedes the page.

**Enforcement against litigants for a missing chambers copy: not found.**

## Calendar

- Civil motions: Thursdays at 2:00 p.m., in person.
- CMCs: Thursdays at 10:00 a.m., in person.
- Joint CMC statement due **10 days** before the conference. Separate statements are allowed when a party is unrepresented (§ D.1).
- Email the CRD a list of who will appear at least 2 court days ahead (§ C.1).
- Proposed trial date: no later than 18 months after filing (§ D.2).

## Filing checklist (AMO)

1. Seek any extension at least 7 days ahead, by motion or stipulation.
2. Oppose every motion. Silence is consent (§ M).
3. Include the AI-verification certification on any submission containing AI-generated content (§ H.4).
4. File a redline or highlighted comparison with any amended pleading, and file the amended pleading as a stand-alone docket entry (§ H.1).
5. Do not incorporate prior arguments by reference (§ H.3).
6. File exhibits as separate ECF attachments, text-searchable (§ H.2).
7. Deliver a chambers copy within 2 days (see the conflict above).
8. For Zoom, file a 7-11 motion at least 7 days ahead.
9. Email the appearance list to amocrd@ at least 2 court days ahead.
