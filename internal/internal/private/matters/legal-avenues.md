## Unavailable or unsafe by assumption

- The 2026 coercive-control bill is not enacted law and cannot be pleaded as a current standalone protective-order ground.
- Brendan does not acquire Njiubu's divorce, protective-order, tort, or statutory claims because he is family.
- A private person does not control a Maryland prosecution.
- Section 1983 is not a domestic-abuse cause of action against a private spouse absent legally sufficient state action.
- A protective order is not a damages judgment, and divorce does not automatically compensate tort injury.
- Historic conduct is not automatically timely because the marriage, separation, fear, or emotional effects continued.
- The same medical cost, wage loss, property loss, or emotional harm may not be recovered twice across divorce, tort, restitution, compensation, or insurance.
- Do not secretly record, access accounts, track, impersonate, destroy, publish intimate material, or manufacture contacts to gather proof.

## Sequencing principle

Safety relief need not wait for divorce or civil damages. Protective-order, divorce, civil, and criminal strategies must be coordinated because sworn statements, discovery, property possession, support, firearms, immigration, and testimony can overlap. A damages demand should wait until current danger, limitations, collectability, insurance, family-property consequences, and any criminal investigation have been reviewed.
