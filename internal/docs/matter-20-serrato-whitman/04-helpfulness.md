# Helpfulness under Penal Code § 679.10(h)

## Trigger

Section 679.10(h) supplies a rebuttable presumption that a victim is helpful, has been helpful, or is likely to be helpful if the victim has not refused or failed to provide information and assistance reasonably requested by law enforcement.

- Bucket pin: operative petition Ex. 14, Attorney General Information Bulletin DLE-2024-05, pp. 2 and 5.
- Bucket pin: operative petition Exs. 2–3 allege no refusal and tender information; Ex. 13 acknowledges receipt.
- Adverse pin: CHP's demurrer reply argues that the presumption does not arise absent an actual reasonable request for information or assistance.
- Controlling posture pin: the August 29 order holds that the helpfulness issue did not sustain Count 3 because CHP's denial rested on absence of qualifying criminal activity, not lack of cooperation. Count 3 is out; helpfulness matters here only as part of Count 1 if CHP puts cooperation at issue on remand.

## What CHP can use to rebut or avoid the presumption

| CHP position | Bucket support | Assessment |
|---|---|---|
| CHP made a reasonable request and Petitioner refused or failed to respond. | MISSING | No request, date, subject, refusal, or failure is pinned in the bucket. |
| No reasonable request was ever made, so subdivision (h) never triggers. | CHP demurrer reply, helpfulness argument | Legal argument preserved; the statutory text and AG bulletin can also be read to treat absence of refusal as satisfying the condition. |
| The denial did not rest on helpfulness at all. | April 20 denial embedded as petition Ex. 1; August 29 order | Strong under the present order. This avoids rather than rebuts the presumption. |
| No qualifying criminal activity occurred. | April 20 denial; AG demurrer papers | Threshold position. It is not evidence that Petitioner was unhelpful. |
| Petitioner refused the voluntary PAS or post-arrest chemical testing. | Operative petition Ex. 5 at 01:10:33–01:11:15 and 01:15:54–01:38:35 | The recording concerns DUI testing during the incident, not a later request for assistance in detecting, investigating, or prosecuting qualifying criminal activity. Whether it can count as noncooperation is unresolved. |
