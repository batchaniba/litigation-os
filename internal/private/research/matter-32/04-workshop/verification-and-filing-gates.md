> Historical scaffold. Read [the new research controls](../09-deep-analysis/research-controls.json) and [amendment/proof proffer](../09-deep-analysis/amendment-and-proof-proffer.md) first; no old checklist certifies the current docket, original media, or final DMV findings.

The workshop is intentionally not filing-ready. A completed checklist requires inspecting originals and current orders, not changing status labels because a draft sounds persuasive.

## Immediate procedural control

- [ ] Retrieve the current N.D. Cal. docket and ECF 22 entry/attachments from an authenticated source.
- [ ] Confirm the operative complaint and its exact ECF designation. The repository contains ECF 5 and a separately labeled ECF 6 amended complaint; do not assume chronology from filenames.
- [ ] Check current assigned judge, reassignment after defendants' ECF 23 declination, and all briefing/standing orders. Declination is not by itself proof that the hearing or deadline was vacated.
- [ ] Calendar a provisional opposition backstop of **November 9, 2026**, calculated as October 26 plus 14 calendar days. Current general Civil L.R. 7-3(a) uses filing, not service, and excludes the Rule 6(d) extra days; this is conditional on the operative docket and any different order, not a certified deadline. [N.D. Cal. Civil Local Rules](https://cand.uscourts.gov/rules-forms-fees/local-rules/civil-local-rules).
- [ ] Treat the December 9, 2026, 1:30 p.m. hearing on the supplied motion as a noticed setting only until current docket confirmation.
- [ ] Verify the current applicable page limit, signature, filing/service method, chambers-copy instructions, and judge-specific requirements. The general opposition limit is 25 pages, subject to applicable orders. [N.D. Cal. Civil Local Rules](https://cand.uscourts.gov/rules-forms-fees/local-rules/civil-local-rules).

No live procedural clock or matter data was changed in this branch. Reassignment must not be used as a reason to delay timely opposition preparation.
