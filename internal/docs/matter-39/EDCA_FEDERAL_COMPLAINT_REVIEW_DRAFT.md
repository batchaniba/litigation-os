# COMPLAINT FOR DAMAGES

**Unauthorized Employment Use of Information Disclosed Pursuant to California Penal Code § 290.46 — Penal Code § 290.46(j)(2)(E), (j)(4)(A)**

**DEMAND FOR JURY TRIAL**

Plaintiff BRENDAN NGWA NFORBI, appearing in propria persona, alleges:

# I. NATURE OF THE ACTION

1. This action arises from an accepted Best Buy warehouse hire at 6653 Hopkins Road, Tracy, California.

2. On October 17, 2023, Best Buy offered Plaintiff the General Warehouse Worker / 2nd Shift position at $22.60 per hour, requisition 928556BR.

3. Plaintiff accepted. Best Buy later wrote, “Congratulations on accepting your offer.”

4. The same afternoon, Best Buy required a background-check authorization that identified First Advantage as its screening vendor, and First Advantage issued a Best Buy drug-test order under account BEST BUY 11759586.

5. Plaintiff completed the drug-screening requirement. Best Buy then ceased communication and prevented Plaintiff from beginning the accepted warehouse job.

6. Plaintiff alleges on information and belief, and on the transaction-specific facts stated below, that First Advantage furnished Best Buy information concerning Plaintiff disclosed pursuant to Penal Code § 290.46, including Plaintiff's California registry information, and that Best Buy used that information for the employment decision.

7. Plaintiff does not allege that Best Buy was barred from using independently and lawfully obtained conviction information. His claim concerns use of information disclosed pursuant to § 290.46 itself.

8. Plaintiff does not possess Best Buy's transaction-specific report and does not allege its source-field wording as known fact.

# II. JURISDICTION AND VENUE

9. This Court has original jurisdiction under 28 U.S.C. § 1332(a).

10. Plaintiff is a citizen of California.

11. Best Buy Co., Inc. is incorporated in Minnesota and has its principal place of business in Richfield, Minnesota. It is therefore a Minnesota citizen for diversity purposes.

12. The amount in controversy exceeds $75,000, exclusive of interest and costs, based in good faith on the lost accepted employment compensation at the documented $22.60 hourly rate according to proof, other consequential economic loss, and the additional and exemplary relief authorized by Penal Code § 290.46(j)(4)(A).

13. The offer presently in Plaintiff's files does not establish weekly hours, an exact start date, or an assured employment duration. Plaintiff does not invent those figures.

14. Plaintiff does not rely on FCRA or ICRAA remedies, or on the optional civil-penalty remedy in § 290.46(j)(4)(A), to establish the amount in controversy.

15. Plaintiff has Article III standing because he alleges a concrete economic injury—the loss of an accepted job and resulting compensation—caused by Best Buy's alleged prohibited employment use and redressable by damages.

16. Venue is proper under 28 U.S.C. § 1391(b)(2) because a substantial part of the events occurred at or concerned the Tracy, California warehouse in San Joaquin County.

17. Under Eastern District of California Local Rule 120(d), civil actions arising in San Joaquin County are commenced on the Sacramento side of the District.

# IX. CAUSE OF ACTION
## Unauthorized Employment Use of Information Disclosed Pursuant to Penal Code § 290.46
### Penal Code § 290.46(j)(2)(E), (j)(4)(A)

54. Plaintiff realleges paragraphs 1 through 53.

55. Plaintiff is a person as to whom information is disclosed on California's Megan's Law internet website pursuant to Penal Code § 290.46.

56. Section 290.46(j)(2)(E) prohibits use of information disclosed pursuant to § 290.46 for a purpose relating to employment except where authorized by § 290.46(j)(1) or another provision of law.

57. On information and belief, information disclosed pursuant to § 290.46 concerning Plaintiff was furnished or made available to Best Buy through its employment-screening process.

58. On information and belief, Best Buy received, accessed, considered, and used that information for a purpose relating to employment.

59. The use was a substantial factor in Best Buy's decision to deny, withdraw, end, prevent, or not finalize Plaintiff's accepted Tracy employment.

60. Plaintiff is unaware of any law authorizing Best Buy to use information disclosed pursuant to § 290.46 for the General Warehouse Worker position.

61. Best Buy thereby violated Penal Code § 290.46(j)(2)(E).

62. Penal Code § 290.46(j)(4)(A) authorizes actual damages, an additional amount within the statutory range, attorney's fees, and exemplary damages upon the required showing, among other remedies.

63. Plaintiff does not seek preventive relief under § 290.46(j)(4)(B) in this opening complaint.
