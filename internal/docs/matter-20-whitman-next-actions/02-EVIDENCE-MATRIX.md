## Controlling legal issue
Penal Code section 679.10(g)(1) conditions certification on victim of **qualifying criminal activity** plus helpfulness. Subdivision (h) presumes helpfulness if applicable cooperation criteria satisfied; this does not prove victimhood. Subdivision (k) does not require criminal charges/prosecution/conviction; it does not itself prove unlawful arrest or a qualifying state crime. Count 1 asks whether the April 20 refusal was an unlawful exercise of agency discretion under CCP 1085, not whether federal civil-rights damages are warranted. The primary alleged qualifying activity is false imprisonment/unlawful criminal restraint: plead and prove the actual qualifying offense elements and any "substantially similar" route.
