**Assessment for the retaliation/due-process fight.** He is a volume state-defense generalist, not a retaliation specialist — and that is the exploitable gap: (1) His comfort zone is discretion + immunity; expect the reply to double down there. Don't expect him to deal — but do expect his office to recognize a bad pleading-stage position, since they live and die on 12(b)(6).

**Caveats.** Education (USC Gould / UC San Diego) is aggregator-sourced, moderate confidence. Exact AG section inferred, not confirmed.
