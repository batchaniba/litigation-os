## Interrogatories under Rule 33

Plaintiff propounds these interrogatories under Federal Rules of Civil Procedure 26 and 33 to Defendant HOPE Program, sued as HOPE Psychotherapy, Inc., d/b/a HOPE Program (“HOPE”). This set contains twenty interrogatories. HOPE must answer separately and fully in writing under oath.

### Definitions and instructions

1. **PLAINTIFF** means Brendan Ngehsi Newanforbi.
2. **YOU** or **HOPE** means the responding Defendant.
3. **SMITH** means Defendant Shannon Smith, LCSW.
4. **CDCR/DAPO** means the California Department of Corrections and Rehabilitation, Division of Adult Parole Operations.
5. **CTM** means any case, clinical, containment, treatment, or team meeting at which PLAINTIFF's treatment or supervision was discussed.
6. **IDENTIFY** a person by full name, employer, title or role, last-known business contact information, and relationship to the subject.
7. **IDENTIFY** a document by date, author, recipients, title or subject, type, custodian, and Bates number or production location.
8. **STATE ALL FACTS** means state the material facts presently known and identify the persons and documents supporting them. It does not require a narrative of every item of evidence.
9. Unless stated otherwise, the relevant period is January 1, 2021 through the date of response.
10. If relying on Rule 33(d), identify the responsive records in sufficient detail to permit the answer to be located as readily as HOPE can locate it.
11. Supplement the answers under Rule 26(e).
