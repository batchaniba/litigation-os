### 1. Affirmative defenses

Defenses 1, 4 through 11, 13, and 14 presently identify few or no supporting facts and do not identify the causes of action to which they are directed. Defenses 2, 12, 15, and 16 appear to state pleading objections, surplusage, or a reservation rather than new matter. See *FPI Development, Inc. v. Nakashima* (1991) 231 Cal.App.3d 367, 384; Code of Civil Procedure sections 430.20(a) and 431.30(g).

Please identify which defenses Defendants intend to maintain, the cause or causes of action to which each applies, and whether Defendants will amend to allege the material facts supporting those defenses. This includes the facts supporting the First Affirmative Defense under Penal Code section 490.5(f).

### 2. Form of certain responses

Section 431.30(d) requires denials to a verified complaint to be made positively or according to information and belief. Section 431.30(e) permits a defendant lacking sufficient information or belief to answer an allegation to state that deficiency and deny on that ground.

Numerous responses state only that Defendants “can neither admit nor deny.” Please advise whether Defendants will amend those responses to state a statutory denial and its basis.

### 3. Verification

The verification is signed by Rachel De la Cruz on behalf of both corporate defendants but does not identify her title or relationship to either entity.

Please identify Ms. De la Cruz's title and relationship to each defendant, whether she is an officer of either corporation, and, if she is not an officer, the statutory basis on which she verified for each corporation. Please also confirm that the text she reviewed was the answer ultimately filed.

### 4. Fictitiously named defendants and caption

The answer states that it is made for Ross Stores, Inc., Ross Dress for Less, Inc., DOE 1, and DOES 2 through 20, while the signature block identifies your clients as the two Ross entities.

Please confirm whether your firm intended to appear for any fictitiously named defendant. If so, please identify the client and your authority to appear. If not, please correct those references in any amended answer.

The answer and discovery captions also state “DOES 2 through 30.” The complaint names DOES 2 through 20. Please use the complaint's caption in future papers.

### 5. Attorney-fee prayer

Please identify the present contractual or statutory basis for the defense attorney-fee prayer or confirm that it is intended only to preserve any fee or sanctions remedy otherwise authorized by law.
