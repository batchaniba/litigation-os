**Status:** PROPOSED ONLY — NOT ENTERED. For use only if the Court finds a material fact question it cannot resolve on the papers. The order is limited to the five questions below; it is not a mini-trial of the DUI, the search, damages, immigration status, or any dismissed count. [PROPOSED] ORDER FOR FOCUSED EVIDENTIARY PROCEEDING (Code Civ. Proc., § 1090)

## 1. Purpose and scope

The Court finds that the following material factual questions cannot be resolved on the papers and require focused factfinding before judgment on Count One. [The Court strikes any question it finds resolvable on the papers and proceeds to judgment on the remainder.] No question beyond Q1–Q5 is presented by this order, and no party may expand the proceeding to damages, immigration status, dismissed counts, or the lawfulness of any search as an independent claim.

## 2. Questions presented

**Q1. Walk-and-turn.** Does the authenticated native MVARS recording contradict the Form 202 entry that petitioner "chose not to perform" the walk-and-turn because it was difficult (AGO 000031), and the "poor performance on the FST's" arrest rationale (AGO 000032)? The segment to be played is approximately 01:10:14–01:10:30 on November 1, 2024, together with such surrounding audio as the Court finds necessary for context.

**Q2. HGN / Romberg weight and foundation.** What weight and foundation do the horizontal-gaze-nystagmus and Romberg observations recorded in the Form 202 and affidavit (AGO 000030–000032, 000048–000049) carry, as distinct from the walk-and-turn question? No expert testimony is proposed on this question; it is argued on the papers and the recording unless the Court orders otherwise.

**Q3. Roadside geometry (§ 40300.5(b)).** What are the facts of vehicle position, lane clearance, and hazard-light use on the Interstate 580 shoulder on November 1, 2024, as shown by the Form 202, CAD log, beat sheet, and recording? This question decides only whether subdivision (b) (obstructing a roadway) is established; Vehicle Code section 40300.6 (liberal interpretation) is argued, not assumed.

**Q4. PAS vs. chemical chronology.** Which test was refused when: the voluntary preliminary alcohol screening (approximately 01:10:54 advisement; 01:11:15 refusal) or the post-arrest chemical test (approximately 01:15:54 discussion)? This question is reached only if the authenticated recording confirms the transcript chronology; if the recording contradicts it, Q4 drops out and the Answer's ¶ 38 account controls.

**Q5. Helpfulness (only if victimhood is found).** If the Court finds the qualifying-crime predicate, what is the helpfulness finding on the complaint and submission record (Decl. ¶¶ 5–6; Answer ¶ 57)? Q5 is not reached unless the Court finds victimhood or orders it reached in the alternative.

## 3. Witnesses

(a) **MVARS custodian (required for Q1, Q3, Q4).** A custodian of the native recordings, to authenticate each file (name, size, SHA-256, device, chain of custody, gaps and "no signal" periods) and to confirm whether the file identified as roadside audio contains roadside audio. The custodian is the only witness required if the recording resolves Q1.

(b) **Arresting officers (only if the Court finds a credibility dispute the recording cannot resolve).** Officers Meza-Gonzalez and Melano, limited to Q2 and to any Q1 dispute the recording leaves unresolved. No officer is called to re-litigate the search, damages, or immigration status.

(c) **Petitioner (only on personal-knowledge facts).** Limited to Q5 and to any personal-knowledge fact within the executed declaration. Petitioner is not called to authenticate the transcript unless he can truthfully do so (see Decl. ¶ 8).

## 4. Exhibits and authentication

Exhibits are limited to the exhibit plan (product 07). The native recording is played, not described; the transcript, if offered, is offered only as a demonstrative aid to the authenticated recording, with bracketed stage directions identified as petitioner's descriptions. Text sidecars are not offered as substitutes for original PDFs. The beat sheet is not quoted until OCR'd or visually read. The privilege log is not offered for the contents of any withheld document.

## 5. Burden and procedure

Petitioner bears the burden of establishing the qualifying-crime predicate and helpfulness by the preponderance of the evidence on the questions presented, and of authenticating any recording or transcript he offers. Respondents bear the burden of establishing any affirmative lawful-authority circumstance they invoke under Vehicle Code section 40300.5. The proceeding is conducted as a court trial of the questions presented under Code of Civil Procedure section 1090, in the form the Court determines (bench proceeding; no jury is proposed by this order). Briefing on Q1–Q5 is limited to [10] pages per side, due [14] days before the proceeding, addressing only the questions, witnesses, and exhibits above.

## 6. After the proceeding

The Court will enter findings on Q1–Q5 and proceed to judgment on the tiers pleaded (direct certification, vacatur and reconsideration, or further proceedings as the Court finds necessary). No finding in this order assumes that any disputed allegation has already been judicially established.
