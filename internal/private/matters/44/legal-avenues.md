# Maryland remedy map

This is an issue-spotting map, not a finding that any claim exists.

| Track | Potential relief | Gate before use |
|---|---|---|
| Safety / protective order | Emergency, interim, temporary, and final protective relief; no-contact/stay-away and other statutory terms; firearm surrender where ordered | Current qualifying abuse, eligible relationship, county and evidence |
| Divorce | Absolute divorce based on a statutory ground; allocation of marital property, alimony, fees, and child-related relief if applicable | Marriage proof, Maryland jurisdiction/residency, venue, separation facts |
| Civil tort | Compensatory and, where legally available and provable, punitive damages for incident-specific torts | Elements, timely accrual, evidence, causation, defenses, collectability |
| Criminal process | Police investigation or Application for Statement of Charges; prosecution by the State | Offense classification, probable cause, limitation period, victim/witness participation |
| Victim services | Safety planning, advocacy, counseling/referrals, and possible crime-victim compensation | Program eligibility, timely application, qualifying crime and losses |
| Firearm-risk relief | Extreme Risk Protective Order if statutory danger criteria are met | Specific present firearm-risk facts and eligible petitioner |
| Vulnerable-adult protection | Adult Protective Services or related relief if age/disability and statutory criteria apply | Age, disability, capacity, caretaker and risk facts |
| Immigration relief | VAWA self-petition, U-visa or other relief may warrant specialist screening | Status, relationship, qualifying conduct, cooperation/certification and federal eligibility |

## What is not available by assumption

- Brendan does not acquire Njiubu's divorce or tort claims because he is family.
- A private person does not control a Maryland criminal prosecution.
- Section 1983 is not a domestic-abuse cause of action against a private spouse
  absent legally sufficient state action.
- A protective order is not a damages judgment, and a divorce decree does not
  automatically compensate every tort.
- Historic conduct is not automatically timely because the relationship or
  emotional effects continued.
- The same medical cost, wage loss, property loss, or emotional harm must not be
  counted twice across divorce, tort, restitution, compensation, or insurance.

## Sequencing principle

Safety relief can proceed without waiting for a divorce or civil damages case.
Divorce and protective-order strategy should be coordinated because admissions,
testimony, discovery, residence, support, property possession, and firearm
issues can overlap. Criminal reporting belongs to Njiubu and public authorities;
civil counsel should not condition safety or truthful reporting on settlement.
