## Rules (S2, S3)
- Non-discovery motions and oppositions are limited to **30 pages** and replies to 10. Over-length papers "may be stricken."
- Proposed orders in Word or WordPerfect to ckdorders@caed.uscourts.gov, with /s/ and the date.
- **Discovery (informal):**
  - Confer first, "in voice to voice dialogue; other forms of communication are not sufficient."
  - Then contact the CRD with dates 3 to 14 days out.
  - Email a 2-page joint letter, including exhibits, 12-pt, to ckdorders@ at least 24 hours before. No paper copy.
  - Sanctions are available. She may order formal L.R. 251 briefing.
- Settlement (S4): confidential statements to CKDorders@ 7 days ahead, plus a public Notice of Submission (L.R. 270(d)). Parties with full authority must attend in person.
