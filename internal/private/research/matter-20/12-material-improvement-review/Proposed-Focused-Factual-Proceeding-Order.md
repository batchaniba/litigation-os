# [Alternative Proposed] Order Defining Essential Factual Issues

The Court finds that the present papers do not permit merits judgment without resolving the essential factual issues identified below. No inference of qualifying criminal activity, arbitrariness, certification entitlement or lack of lawful authority is made solely from the existence of a dispute.

## Issues for resolution

The Court will determine, to the extent essential to disposition of Count One:

1. Whether the authenticated recording confirms the submitted walk-and-turn exchange, and whether the report's attribution of nonperformance to Petitioner was inaccurate.
2. Which contemporaneous impairment observations and earlier-driving facts are established by competent evidence, what remains after correction of any report error, and what lawful custody authority follows from the totality of facts known at the arrest.
3. Whether the resulting findings establish qualifying criminal activity and a material error in the April 20 certification determination.
4. If qualifying victimhood is established and necessary to relief, whether helpfulness and any properly asserted rebuttal or remaining statutory condition are established.

The parties shall not treat postdecision material as proof of what the certifier actually knew without foundation. Nonprivileged receipt, routing and record-completeness evidence may be addressed as relevant; this order does not compel blanket disclosure of personnel files, private deliberations or privileged communications.
