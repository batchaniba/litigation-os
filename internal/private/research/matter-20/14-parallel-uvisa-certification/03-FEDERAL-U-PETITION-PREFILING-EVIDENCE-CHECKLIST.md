# Federal USCIS I-918 package — preparation before signed Supplement B
**CONFIDENTIAL WORKING CHECKLIST, NO FORMS SUBMITTED.** Never upload completed criminal history, full immigration history, A-numbers or medical records to the public GitHub repo or public court filings. A completed packet requires a **signed I-918B** from an eligible certifier, not just a report or a court transcript.

### Documentary contents
- [ ] Current official Form I-918 and instructions; verify edition, USCIS filing address, signature and identity information.
- [ ] Signed Form I-918 Supplement B, complete and signed by an authorized official **within 6 months of USCIS receipt**; preserve certifier title/delegation if needed. No wet signature or official status invented by petitioner.
- [ ] Petitioner's signed firsthand narrative; accurate victim-of-crime allegation and assistance rendered; date/location and officer account discrepancies shown with corroborated record, no speculation.
- [ ] Evidence of criminally qualifying activity: original CHP Form202/DS367, incident CAD log, actual 2024 recording where available, court materials and California Penal Code §§236,146 if needed, typed nonagency transcript labeled as such; note contrary evidence accurately.
- [ ] Evidence of information possession and actual helpfulness to investigation of **officer conduct**, including POST/CHP/DA submissions, dates and replies, witness contacts and invitations to assist.
- [ ] **Causal substantial physical or mental abuse** statement and authentic corroboration: dates, duration, severity, functional effects, original incarceration records, any existing professional/therapy notes, affected work/sleep/circumstances; no invented diagnoses. Separate federal threshold under 8 CFR §214.14(b)(1).
- [ ] Comprehensive confidential immigration and criminal-record screening by experienced immigration counsel/DOJ-accredited representative, including any prior orders and removal history; only if appropriate, fully completed I-192 waiver with statement and supporting rehabilitation/discretion evidence. No assurance waiver will be granted.
- [ ] Address protection, safe USCIS correspondence destination, biometrics and any current EAD filing rules; BFD-based EAD and deferred action **not guaranteed**, and BFD is **not actual U nonimmigrant status**.
- [ ] File before six-month certification validity ends; obtain dated USCIS receipt; do not file deliberately missing I-918B to attempt earlier queue placement.
