## Historical authority, not appellate precedent

Kim's April 20, 2026 recommendation is preserved as a retrieved public text reproduction. It is central adverse case history and must be compared to the original complaint, objections, orders, and current FAC; do not cite it as Ninth Circuit authority or assume its adoption without checking. [Public reproduction](https://www.courtlistener.com/opinion/10913465/brendan-ngehsi-newanforbi-v-state-of-california-et-al/).

The June 29, 2026 screening order in Matter 24, R066, concerns Serrato and U-visa-certification allegations. It repeats underlying-incident allegations as background; that is not a merits finding that the arrest, reports, or blood draw were unconstitutional.

## Research leads not cleared for filing

- **Spencer v. Peters / Devereaux / related cases:** Verify the exact rule and limits for a fabrication claim where no criminal charge was filed, the required liberty deprivation, actual/proximate causation, and any more-specific-constitutional-provision rule.
- **Nieves v. Bartlett:** Read the full majority and subsequent Ninth Circuit treatment for the actual pleaded adverse act, not merely an abstract retaliation standard.
- **Emergency aid:** Retrieve governing Supreme Court and Ninth Circuit cases concerning objective need for aid and unresponsive occupants; test initial entry independently from continued investigation.
- **Automobile search / plain view / cup:** Distinguish looking through a window, opening the door, handling the cup, and further search; sight of an ambiguous cup is not synonymous with authority for every action.
- **Smith v. Wade / Kay v. Ehrler:** Retrieve primary holdings, exact standards, and the distinction between punitive damages, pro se fees, costs, and any future counsel fees.
- **Amendment:** Verify Rule 15 and controlling Ninth Circuit futility/pro se amendment standards against the actual prior opportunities and orders.
- **Preclusion / Rule 41:** Obtain actual dispositions before claiming claim preclusion, issue preclusion, two-dismissal effect, law of the case, or lack of any such effect.
- **Tobey v. Jones:** The FAC's cited authority is Fourth Circuit and predates Nieves; it cannot be described as binding Ninth Circuit authority. It was identified in research but is not cleared here as the answer to retaliatory arrest.
- **Post-2024 merits updates:** Check current law through filing, while keeping the clearly-established-law cutoff fixed at the date of the challenged conduct.

## Citation discipline

Use full primary-source URLs and verified pinpoints in any derivative brief. A preserved opinion PDF does not prove that the case remains good law for every possible proposition, and a citation appearing in an old brief is only a research lead until independently checked.
