## Stipulations — permanent plaintiff-first rule

Before drafting, reviewing, redlining, recommending, or recording any stipulation or defense agreement in any current or future matter, read and follow [Stipulation Defense](internal/docs/STIPULATION_DEFENSE.md).

Draft terms most favorable to Plaintiff. Maintain or shorten defense timelines when supported and advantageous; preserve Plaintiff's preparation time and favorable hearing dates. Require an explicit deadline comparison and concession audit. Identify concrete Plaintiff benefit for exceptions; do not silently grant defense time, waive rights, expand defense options, or authorize Plaintiff's signature. Verify rules and sources before case-specific deadline conclusions. Proposed agreements do not automatically change operative calendar dates.

This is a standing instruction across the portfolio. Case-specific exceptions do not replace it. Preserve existing court orders and executed agreements as historical facts.
