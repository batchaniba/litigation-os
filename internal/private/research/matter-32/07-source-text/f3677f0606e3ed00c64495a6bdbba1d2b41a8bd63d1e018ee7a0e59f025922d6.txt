 7                          RELEVANCE TO THE PENDING OBJECTIONS

 8

 9          The Serrato Order is directly relevant to this Court's de novo review of the Findings and

10   Recommendations for the following reasons:

11

12          First, the Serrato Order arises from the same November 1, 2024 incident at issue in this

13   case. Judge Krishnan evaluated the same core factual allegations that underlie this action -- the

14   dashcam contradictions of the sworn CHP report, the fabrication of material statements regarding

15   an odor of alcohol and slurred speech, the involuntary blood draw procured by those fabrications,

16   and the Penal Code section 851.6 detention certificate reclassifying the arrest as a detention with

17   insufficient grounds for a criminal complaint.

18

19          Second, a coordinate Article III court has now concluded, on a substantially overlapping

20   factual record, that Plaintiff's allegations state a sufficient claim to survive section 1915

21   screening. The Findings and Recommendations in this case reached the opposite conclusion,

22   characterizing the complaint as presenting no "non-frivolous claim." The Serrato Order

23   demonstrates that, at a minimum, the claims have "an arguable basis . . . in law or in fact" within

24   the meaning of Neitzke v. Williams, 490 U.S. 319, 325 (1989).

25

26          Third, the Serrato Order undermines the Findings and Recommendations'

27   qualified-immunity ruling. Judge Krishnan's determination that the complaint is sufficient

28   necessarily reflects a finding that the underlying constitutional claims are plausible -- a

                                                    Page 2
            NOTICE OF SUPPLEMENTAL AUTHORITY -- NEWANFORBI v. STATE OF CALIFORNIA ET AL.
 1   conclusion inconsistent with resolving qualified immunity against Plaintiff at the screening stage

 2   on the same factual record.

 3

 4          Plaintiff does not contend that the Serrato Order is binding on this Court. Plaintiff

 5   respectfully submits, however, that a coordinate federal court's assessment of the same factual

 6   allegations is persuasive authority that weighs against adoption of the Findings and

 7   Recommendations and supports the relief requested in Plaintiff's Objections (ECF No. 24).
