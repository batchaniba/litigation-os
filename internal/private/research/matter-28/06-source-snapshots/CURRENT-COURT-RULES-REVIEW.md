## Appearance

Standing order A, page 1: motion hearings are in person unless ordered otherwise. Remote hearing requests require an administrative motion or stipulation and proposed order. Hybrid appearances are disfavored. Ordinary CMCs are Thursday Zoom proceedings, but ECF 29 specifically combines this CMC with the Tuesday motion hearing in Courtroom G. A public Zoom URL is not a remote-appearance order.

## Motion and proposed-order procedure

Standing order E.1, page 3: confer in person, by video, or phone before motions/non-stipulated requests and certify compliance. B.3, page 2: proposed orders requiring court action go in Word format to the proposed-order address on the filing date. This research branch neither submits a proposed order nor sends that email.

Rule 7-3(d): post-reply papers generally require prior approval. A reply-evidence objection has a seven-day filing period, a five-page text limit, and cannot become further merits argument. The repository's October 20 objection clock is now past and still open; actual filing status is unknown. Do not relabel October 25 as a new timely objection date. Any late submission requires a procedurally supported request, including applicable time-relief standards. FRE 201(e)'s opportunity-to-be-heard provision is not an automatic extension of the local filing deadline.

Rule 7-6: oral testimony is not received on a motion unless the judge orders otherwise. Rule 12(d) addresses conversion if outside matters are considered; ordinary judicial notice and incorporation exceptions need their own analysis and do not automatically trigger conversion.

## Protective orders and discovery

Standing order F.6, page 6: a modified model protective order needs the explanatory declaration and comparison redline, or the specified alternative explanation. The user history reports a redline request; no corresponding docket entry or revised signed order was retrieved in this pass. Verify completion rather than claim noncompliance or entry.

F.5, pages 4-5: discovery disputes follow the formal conferral/joint-letter process. The usual joint letter is five pages; the order also specifies required exhibits, dates, and timing. A CMC status request does not establish entitlement to a contested production order without the required discovery process.
