### Critical correction to prior research
**No officer-observed driving is not a complete unlawful-arrest argument.** *People v. Schofield* (2001) 90 Cal.App.4th 968, 973–975, construes Vehicle Code §40300.5(e)'s evidence-destruction exception to include metabolic dissipation; *Troppman v. Valverde* (2007) 40 Cal.4th 1121, 1137 fn.11 acknowledges it. The court must first address **reliable prearrest DUI probable cause** and objectively applicable arrest authority. Our older merits-rebuild version emphasized the roadway-obstruction limitation but insufficiently treated this important exception. This version fixes that.

Captain Serrato's April 20 letter said **the incident did not involve qualifying criminal activity**. It did **not** expressly say **false imprisonment is categorically excluded as a qualifying offense**. The report repeats that characterization several times; the integrated brief does not.
