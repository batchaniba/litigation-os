This is what the record supports, stated carefully:

1. **Demurrer-first instinct.** The one litigation win his firm chose to put on its
   website under his name is a demurrer sustained *without leave to amend* "at the
   outset of the case." Lawyers advertise what they want more of. Expect the RK
   demurrer to be his opening move, not a placeholder — and expect it to ask for
   no-leave on at least some causes of action.
2. **He writes for the bench.** Former superior-court research attorney. His demurrers
   will be organized the way judges like: statutory ground first (CCP § 430.10(e)),
   short, with the leave-to-amend standard addressed head-on rather than ignored.
3. **Meet-and-confer compliance.** CCP § 430.41 requires a pre-demurrer meet and
   confer identifying each challenged cause of action with legal support. The Matter 27
   record already shows § 430.41 in play (the September 9 email thread invoked it with
   Garcia). If the M&C is incomplete by the response deadline, the standard move is a
   § 430.41(a)(2) declaration taking the automatic 30-day extension — watch for that
   filing as an alternative to a September 14 demurrer.
4. **No adverse demurrer history found.** No overruled demurrers, no sanctions, no
   judicial rebukes surfaced in any source. The observable record is thin, not dirty.

## 7. Matter 27 tactical forecast

### What the demurrer will likely argue

Predicted grounds, in the order a former research attorney would present them:

1. **Failure to state facts sufficient — no employment relationship with RK**
   (CCP § 430.10(e)). The complaint's own facts: plaintiff applied via Indeed to an RK
   posting, but was recruited, processed, and terminated through Randstad (Dishon
   Bryant's texts, Randstad's July 15 termination). RK never hired, employed, or paid
   him. Joint-employer allegations are conclusory.
2. **FCRA claims misdirected.** The § 1681e(b) reasonable-procedures and § 1681i
   reinvestigation duties run against the consumer reporting agency (InfoMart), and
   end-user adverse-action duties (§ 1681b(b)(3)) against the party taking the adverse
   action (Randstad). RK as worksite/end-client is the wrong defendant for every
   FCRA cause of action pleaded.
3. **Uncertainty** (§ 430.10(f)) — "Randstad et al." shotgun pleading that doesn't
   distinguish which defendant did what.
4. **Relief sought:** sustain **without leave to amend** as to the FCRA causes (pure
   questions of statutory duty — no amendment cures "wrong defendant"); with leave, at
   most, on any employment cause where facts *could* theoretically be pleaded.
