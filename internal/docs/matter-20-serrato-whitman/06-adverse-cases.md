# Adverse cases

Only checked authorities appear here. “Checked” identifies the source used to verify the cited holding.

## *Common Cause v. Board of Supervisors* (1989) 49 Cal.3d 432, 442–446

- Holding: a court may require government action to conform to law but ordinarily may not choose the particular course committed to executive or legislative discretion.
- Effect: **Distinguishable** if the requested remedy is vacatur and remand for lawful reconsideration; potentially fatal to an order commanding CHP to certify.
- Checked: published California Supreme Court opinion text, especially the majority's discussion at pages 442–446.

## *Saleeby v. State Bar* (1985) 39 Cal.3d 547, 562

- Holding: traditional mandate does not compel a particular method of exercising discretion, but it may correct abuse of discretion or agency action exceeding legal powers.
- Effect: **Distinguishable** from a prohibited result-directing writ and affirmatively supports a remand remedy.
- Checked: published California Supreme Court opinion text at pages 560–563.

## *Espinoza v. Shiomoto* (2017) 10 Cal.App.5th 85, 97–102

- Holding: probable cause for a DUI arrest is evaluated from the totality known to the officer, and objective symptoms, driving facts, field-test conduct, and refusals may support reasonable cause even when individual facts are disputed.
- Effect: **Distinguishable**, but dangerous: CHP can aggregate the parked vehicle, container, test sequence, and refusals; Petitioner must answer the totality rather than isolate one fact.
- Checked: published Court of Appeal opinion on rehearing, including its reasonable-cause analysis.

## August 29, 2026 demurrer order (Whitman)

- Holding: Count 1 states an abuse-of-discretion theory, but Counts 2 and 3 fail and Count 4 was the only count given limited leave; the order did not adjudicate the factual truth of the report-versus-dashcam allegations.
- Effect: **Fatal** to any opening-brief attempt to revive Counts 2–3, treat Count 4 as live, or present the pleaded factual conflicts as already proved.
- Checked: filed order and notice of entry in the bucket.
