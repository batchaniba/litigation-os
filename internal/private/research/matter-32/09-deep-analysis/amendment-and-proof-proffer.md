## Conditional factual modules

### Entry sequence

If the original recording supports a response before entry, allege the exact response, observable conduct, time and officer, without converting an unverified transcript implication into fact. If the occupant remained unresponsive until after opening, do not plead the opposite; instead analyze whether the circumstances nevertheless plausibly lacked objective emergency indicators.

The door-opening claim does not require denying officers could approach or knock. The material distinction is the objectively justified scope of the physical intrusion, applying Ngumezi without treating its nonemergency facts as identical. ([Ngumezi](https://cdn.ca9.uscourts.gov/datastore/opinions/2020/11/20/19-10243.pdf))

### Warrant deception

For each challenged affidavit statement, complete this sentence internally: “At [location], Meza stated [verbatim text]; the specifically different fact is [supported fact]; he knew or recklessly disregarded it because [concrete basis]; without the distortion and with [material omitted context], the remaining affidavit does not establish probable cause because [legal explanation].” Do not use a statement absent from the actual affidavit unless a verified attachment was before the issuing judge.

Benavidez is useful because a detailed factual proffer can satisfy plausibility and the particularity standard discussed there without claiming discovery has already proved the case. Chism still requires a material change in the real corrected affidavit. ([Benavidez](https://cdn.ca9.uscourts.gov/datastore/opinions/2021/04/12/19-55274.pdf); [Chism, amended opinion](https://cases.justia.com/federal/appellate-courts/ca9/10-35085/10-35085-2011-11-07.pdf?ts=1411064324))

### DMV fabrication

Use the final hearing decision to identify the actual suspension and commercial-disqualification grounds, findings, effective dates, and evidence credited. If the false fact was necessary to the reasonable-cause or arrest finding, state that causal theory; if the final outcome rests on independent facts that would produce the same result, do not conceal that obstacle.

Do not merge voluntary PAS refusal with the required post-arrest chemical test. The distinction is statutory and potentially dispositive to both factual accuracy and causation. ([Vehicle Code § 23612](https://leginfo.legislature.ca.gov/faces/codes_displaySection.xhtml?sectionNum=23612.&lawCode=VEH); [Vehicle Code § 13353](https://leginfo.legislature.ca.gov/faces/codes_displaySection.xhtml?lawCode=VEH&sectionNum=13353.))

## Proposed alternative-relief paragraph

The following is synthesis for editing, not a quotation from a filed document. It should be used only after the record and applicable procedural authorities are checked.

> If the Court concludes that any count lacks sufficient specificity, plaintiff requests leave limited to curing the identified defect rather than dismissal of unrelated claims. The proposed amendments would correct the CHP form-label characterization, identify the actual two-page warrant affidavit and each genuinely disputed material assertion, separate the pre-parole entry and cup inspection from later events, specify each officer's participation, and identify the particular administrative finding and deprivation allegedly caused by fabricated information. Plaintiff does not seek to preserve an inference contradicted by the produced form, to insert later laboratory results into the earlier probable-cause decision, or to treat all post-search injury as automatically recoverable.

This paragraph is not a substitute for a proposed factual amendment. Its effectiveness depends on being able to supply the concrete facts identified above, especially where the court might otherwise find amendment futile.
