- The Clinical Director job description shows incentive eligibility, not the existence of a retention bonus.
- One duplicated message is one event.
- A copied complaint notice establishes notice, not Smith's causation.

- Verify OCR against the source image before quoting.
- Do not access provider-controlled links or systems without authorization.
