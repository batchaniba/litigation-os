**[PROPOSED] ORDER GRANTING PETITION ON FIRST CAUSE OF ACTION; JUDGMENT AND DIRECTIONS FOR PEREMPTORY WRIT OF MANDATE**

The petition on the surviving First Cause of Action under Code of Civil Procedure section 1085 and Penal Code section 679.10 came on for hearing on [DATE], in Department 25 before the Honorable Jenna M. Whitman. The Court has considered the verified petition, admitted evidence, the parties' moving/opposition/reply papers, and the argument, and finds:

1. Petitioner requested certification pursuant to Penal Code section 679.10 on April 9, 2026. CHP denied the request on April 20, 2026. The Court's August 29, 2026 demurrer order preserved Count One for merits determination.

2. Based on the facts determined in the Court's accompanying statement of decision or order, the Court concludes that [INSERT FACTUAL FINDINGS AND STATUTORY LEGAL ERROR/ABUSE ESTABLISHED BY THE ACTUAL HEARING RECORD, WITH SPECIFIC CITATIONS].

3. Petitioner has no plain, speedy and adequate remedy in the ordinary course of law to obtain the specific performance of Respondents' statutory certification duties. (Code Civ. Proc., §§ 1085–1086.)

**PRIMARY RELIEF — USE ONLY IF THE COURT FINDS ALL STATUTORY PREDICATES ESTABLISHED AND NO LAWFUL ALTERNATIVE:**

4A. The April 20, 2026 refusal is SET ASIDE. CHP, acting through an official authorized under Penal Code section 679.10(b), SHALL within **30 calendar days after service of the peremptory writ**, complete, sign and deliver to Petitioner the Form I-918 Supplement B certification requested April 9, 2026, without conditioning that act on a prosecution, conviction, or other prerequisite prohibited by section 679.10.

**ALTERNATIVE RELIEF — USE ONLY IF ABUSE/ERROR IS ESTABLISHED BUT LAWFUL AGENCY DISCRETION REMAINS:**

4B. The April 20, 2026 refusal is SET ASIDE. Within **30 calendar days after service of the peremptory writ**, CHP, through an authorized official, SHALL reconsider the April 9 request under Penal Code section 679.10(c), (g), (h), and (k), applying the Court's specific legal and factual findings and evaluating the qualifying-activity and helpfulness elements lawfully, without reliance on prohibited factors. The Court does not order an outcome on issues committed to Respondents' legally bounded discretion.

5. Under the selected relief above, Respondents shall file a verified return to the writ **45 calendar days after service**, setting out acts performed. The Court retains jurisdiction to determine compliance and to resolve matters properly within the writ proceeding.

6. The Court directs issuance of the peremptory writ in conformity with this order; any costs or other relief shall be addressed under applicable law on proper application. All references to remedies on dismissed causes of action are excluded.

IT IS SO ORDERED.
