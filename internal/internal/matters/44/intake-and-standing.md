## Minimum intake

### Identity, marriage, and forum

- Full legal names and prior names.
- Date and place of marriage; certified marriage record location.
- Each spouse's current county/state and residence history.
- Exact separation date; whether they live under separate roofs.
- Prior divorce, protective-order, peace-order, custody, support, criminal, or
  immigration matters anywhere; case numbers and dispositions.
- Any marital agreement, separation agreement, deed, title, beneficiary
  designation, or estate-planning instrument.

### Safety

- Most recent alleged act: date, place, conduct, injuries, threats, witnesses.
- Current contact, access to home/work, stalking or digital monitoring.
- Firearms or other weapons; threats of suicide, homicide, abduction, or arson.
- Children, dependents, elders, or vulnerable adults exposed to risk.
- Safe phone/email/address and whether communications may be monitored.
- Police, medical, shelter, hotline, or advocate contact already made.

### Proof

- Photos, videos, messages, emails, call logs, recordings, medical records,
  police reports, 911/CAD records, journals, repair bills, employment records,
  and witness identities.
- For each item: creator, date, original location, custodian, how obtained,
  alterations, export method, and hash.
- Identify evidence held by third parties before routine deletion periods run.

### Financial and divorce relief

- Income, employment, benefits, retirement, bank and brokerage accounts, real
  property, vehicles, businesses, valuable personal property, debts, taxes,
  insurance, transfers, and unusual withdrawals.
- Contributions to the marriage, health, age, employability, and current needs.
- Any dependent child, custody, support, or educational issues.

### Damages and services

- Injury dates, diagnoses, treatment, expenses, lost work, relocation, property
  loss, counseling, safety costs, and out-of-pocket expenses.
- Existing health, homeowner, renter, umbrella, professional, or other
  insurance information, without assuming coverage.

that implies representation, or submit his own affidavit about events he did
not personally perceive. Any document must identify its actual author and
