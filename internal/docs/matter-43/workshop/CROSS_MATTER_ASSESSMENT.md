The underlying MVR, any criminal-search results, and any employer adjudication remain **unknown**. Hertz's explicit MVR contingency is a material alternative explanation in a vehicle-related role; it must not be ignored in favor of a preferred inference.

## Legal research questions for independent verification

Do not assert these as claims from the current record. A reviewer must identify the **applicable 2026 statutory version**, jurisdiction, triggering conduct, statutory exceptions, available remedy, and source document:

1. **California Penal Code § 290.46(j)**: What exactly is prohibited about *information disclosed pursuant to the public Megan's Law website*, and which exceptions or causal/source requirements apply? Distinguish website-source information from a criminal courthouse search or a motor-vehicle record.
2. **California Fair Chance Act (Gov. Code § 12952)**: Which initial and post-conditional-offer rules apply to this employer and location? What specific notice, individualized assessment, response window, and exception would matter *only if* a conviction-based preliminary decision occurs?
3. **FCRA** (15 U.S.C. §§ 1681b, 1681e, 1681i, 1681m, 1681n/o): Is an actual consumer reporting agency/report involved? Which duties run to employer vs. vendor, and which facts trigger them?
4. **California ICRAA** (Civ. Code §§ 1786.16, 1786.22, 1786.40, 1786.50): Classify any investigation actually ordered before assessing disclosure, copy, adverse-action and damages questions.
5. **MVR / job-related driving standards**: What do Hertz's documented written terms, actual driving requirements, and any applicable driving-policy exceptions establish? Avoid assuming the position automatically requires any particular license classification or qualification.
6. **Entity identity / parent subsidiary**: The Hertz Corporation offered the position; Hertz Global Holdings, Inc. appears on ADP WOTC. Does any reliable corporate record, employer policy, contract or agency evidence connect a given challenged action to the entity to be named?

## Best defense-side arguments a review should confront

- The written offer is expressly conditional and at-will; no start date or guaranteed hours is established.
- Employment screening may legitimately involve MVR, education, employment history, or other qualifications, independently of any registry website.
- Administrative onboarding tasks may remain incomplete.
- ADP WOTC remains separate. HireRight is the confirmed CRA, but a disclosure listing search products is not proof that every product returned data or drove a decision.
- No changed hiring decision, unlawful source, notice failure, reliance, injury or damages has been shown.

## What would change the view

- **Offer honored / job started:** close the monitoring loop with earnings and start verification. Material employment success.
- **Onboarding delayed:** document exact communications/tasks before attributing cause.
- **Background report received:** compare public sources, dates, decisions and reporting duties, without presuming outcome.
- **Preliminary adverse decision:** assess the given reason, report and procedural response rights promptly.
- **Offer rescinded:** analyze documentary causation, exceptions, entity identity, and actual loss before any complaint/valuation.

**Current working conclusion:** Hertz is a **high-quality evidence baseline** and a **zero-established-liability monitoring matter**, not a settled damages theory or an existing filed lawsuit.
