# Plaintiff-protective clause library

Drop-in language for stipulation papers only. Adapt the bracketed terms.
Keep the operative words. Do not paste these recitals into status reports,
oppositions, or other ordinary Plaintiff filings.
Canonical skill: `.claude/skills/stipulation-defense/SKILL.md`.

## 1. Continue the event only

> The [initial case management conference] currently set for [date] is
> continued to [date certain]. **No other date or deadline in this action
> is modified by this stipulation.** All deadlines not expressly continued
> above remain in effect as previously set, including [Rule 26(a)(1)
> disclosures / the joint case management statement / ADR certification]
> due [date certain].

Use whenever the defense proposes moving a hearing or conference. Do not
add a second request item they did not ask for.

## 2. Date certain, never a floating anchor

> The following deadlines are reset as follows: [obligation] — [calendar
> date]; [obligation] — [calendar date].

Never accept: *"reset to run from the continued conference date,"*
*"reset accordingly,"* or *"to be determined following the Court's
ruling."* If they insist on tying a date to an event, add an outside date:

> …provided that in no event shall [obligation] occur later than
> [calendar date].

## 3. Not a stay of discovery

> This stipulation continues [the conference / the deadline] only. It is
> not a stay of discovery, does not stay or defer any party's discovery
> obligations, and no party waives any right to seek or oppose discovery.

## 4. Single extension — no further implied

> This is a single extension. It does not alter any date set by Court
> order. No further extension is implied. Any additional extension would
> need to be agreed separately, in writing, and presented to the Court
> if the rules require it.

## 5. Named party only

> This extension applies to [named party] only. It does not apply to
> [other named party] or to any other party. [Other named party]'s
> deadline is unchanged.

## 6. Default reserved

> It does not waive any defense, objection, or right of any party,
> including Plaintiff's right to seek default if no timely response is
> filed.

## 7. Reciprocity — simultaneous exchange

> [Obligation] shall be exchanged simultaneously on [date]. Neither
> party is obligated to serve before receiving the other's.

Use for initial disclosures, expert designations, and settlement
exchanges. It converts a one-way giveaway into a trade.

## 8. Price the continuance

> As consideration for the continuance, Defendant shall [serve initial
> disclosures under Rule 26(a)(1) / produce the documents described in
> Exhibit A / accept service on behalf of (defendant) / stipulate to
> the authenticity of (agency records)] on or before [date certain].

## 9. Reservation of rights

> Nothing in this stipulation constitutes a waiver of any claim,
> defense, objection, or right, including any right to seek [discovery /
> amendment / default / relief from the schedule], and no party concedes
> any issue by entering into it.

## 10. Pending motions unaffected

> This stipulation does not modify the briefing schedule or hearing date
> on [Dkt. __], which remain as set.

## 11. Stay — defined end, tolling, fallback

> "Final resolution" means entry of final judgment or a dispositive
> order, plus expiration of any appeal period. Applicable statutory
> deadlines as to Plaintiff are tolled during the stay. If the parties
> cannot agree on a response date when the stay lifts, the response is
> due [number] days after written notice that the stay has lifted. All
> claims, defenses, and rights are preserved, without prejudice.

Never open-ended. Never silent on which claims.

## 12. Per-document e-signature — only if he has read that file

Default: he signs his own counterpart. Counsel may not affix `/s/`.
If e-filing forces a single filed PDF and he has read that exact file:

> You are authorized to file [exact filename] with my electronic
> signature, /s/ [NAME]. This authorization is limited to that document
> as attached and does not extend to any revised version.

Require the filer's attestation:

> Pursuant to Civil Local Rule 5-1(i)(3), [filer] attests that
> concurrence in the filing of this document has been obtained from
> each signatory.

Then diff the conformed copy against what he authorized.

## 13. Withdrawing consent before entry

A stipulation binds as an order only once entered. Before entry, and
only if the facts have changed:

> Plaintiff respectfully notifies the Court that he withdraws his
> consent to the stipulation filed as Dkt. __. The circumstances that
> prompted it have changed: [the joint statement is now on file and
> initial disclosures have been served]. Plaintiff is prepared to
> proceed on [date].

Use sparingly. Withdrawing consent to something he signed costs
credibility, and costs more if he expanded it.
