# Dossier: U.S. Magistrate Judge Sean C. Riordan — 2026 Docket Audit

**Who:** Sean C. Riordan, U.S. Magistrate Judge, Eastern District of California, Sacramento —
Courtroom 27, 8th Floor, 501 I Street. Appointed **August 1, 2024**; still relatively new (~2
years on the bench). Suffix **-SCR**.
Source: [court bio page](http://www.caed.uscourts.gov/caednew/index.cfm/judges/all-judges/united-states-magistrate-judge-sean-c-riordan-scr/united-states-magistrate-judge-sean-c-riordan-scr/)

**Pre-bench background (unusual and relevant):** 12+ years as a civil-rights plaintiffs' lawyer —
Senior Staff Attorney at the **ACLU of Northern California** (complex civil litigation; earlier
ACLU of San Diego & Imperial Counties), starting on a Skadden Foundation Fellowship — plus
several years as an **Assistant Federal Public Defender** in the E.D. Cal. UCLA School of Law;
master's from the American University in Cairo; bachelor's from Whittier College.

**Method honesty:** Sampled audit, not a census — built from ~15 matters with observable 2026
activity (Justia/govinfo orders, the court's own site, docket metadata). The FedEx matter
(2:26-cv-02881-DAD-SCR) is carried as repo-verified; it is not yet publicly indexed.

## Observable patterns — for a pro se plaintiff bringing employment civil-rights claims

1. **Civil-rights plaintiffs' lawyer on the bench.** Twelve years at the ACLU doing complex
   civil litigation is an unusual pedigree and the most important single fact here. He reads
   civil-rights claims like someone who used to plead them — but the screening orders show he
   still enforces pleading standards rigorously. Sympathetic reader, not a soft touch.
2. **"Pro Se Plaintiff Summary."** His screening orders end with a plain-language section
   addressed directly to the unrepresented litigant: what was decided, why, what to do next,
   and the deadline. Pedagogical and patient by design.
3. **Liberal, guided leave to amend.** The ADEA steer in 2:24-cv-03172 is the exemplar:
   rather than just dismissing, he identified the viable theory in the plaintiff's own facts
   and told him precisely what to allege. Expect the same treatment — but note the corollary
   in *Johnson v. Phong*: when amendment is genuinely futile, he recommends dismissal with
   prejudice and says so plainly.
4. **Enforces preclusion and procedure against pro se plaintiffs too** (*Barnett I*
   claim-preclusion dismissal; strict page limits; remote appearances "generally disfavored"
   absent hardship).
5. **Rules against the government when the law supports it.** Two recommended habeas grants
   against ICE detention in June 2026; no visible pattern of reflexive deference to
   institutional defendants.
6. **Settlement-oriented.** Limited-purpose counsel appointments for settlement, pre-discovery
   stays for settlement, detailed settlement-conference standing order.
7. **Discovery discipline:** strictly enforces real meet-and-confer (in person/phone/video —
   "written communication alone does not satisfy"), with an informal telephonic
   dispute-resolution track via 2-page joint letters.
8. **Role clarity for the FedEx case:** as the -SCR referral magistrate under District Judge
   Drozd in 2:26-cv-02881-DAD-SCR, his lane is pretrial management — scheduling (the Oct. 8
   Zoom proceeding), discovery, settlement, and findings & recommendations on dispositive
   motions. Drozd decides the F&Rs.
