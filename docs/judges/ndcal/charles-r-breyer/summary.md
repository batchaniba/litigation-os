## Courtesy copies: the key rule for this judge

> "Parties must file a single (no duplicates) courtesy chambers copy of all filings. The courtesy copy must be delivered to the Clerk's Office no later than noon on the court day following the day that the document was electronically filed." (GSO § I.E)

- **Format:** three-hole punched on the left side. Each page carries the ECF header (case number, docket number, date, and ECF page number) along the top.
- **Exhibits:** tabbed on the right side. Exhibits more than 3 inches thick go in a binder.
- **Where:** Clerk's Office, 450 Golden Gate Ave., 16th Floor (judge page).
- **Local-rule conflict:** The GSO itself notes that this deadline differs from the one in Civil L.R. 5-1. Under Civil L.R. 5-1(d)(7), chambers copies are not required unless a standing order requires them. This one does, for **all** filings.
- **Enforcement history:**
  - The rule is long-standing. A 2015 order quotes the same requirement: *Rubio-Delgado v. Aerotek*, 3:13-cv-03105-CRB, Dkt. 70. https://www.govinfo.gov/content/pkg/USCOURTS-cand-3_13-cv-03105/pdf/USCOURTS-cand-3_13-cv-03105-7.pdf
  - The Court has relied on a chambers copy when the ECF version was unreadable: *Chevron v. Donziger*, 3:13-mc-80038-CRB (Mar. 5, 2013). https://www.govinfo.gov/content/pkg/USCOURTS-cand-3_13-mc-80038/pdf/USCOURTS-cand-3_13-mc-80038-1.pdf
  - **Sanctions or strikes for a missing courtesy copy: not found** in GovInfo full-text search or web search.

## Filing checklist (Breyer)

See the README for the cross-judge checklist. The items specific to Breyer:

1. Keep the brief to 15 pages or less. Add a summary of argument if it runs over 10 pages.
2. Attach a proposed order to every motion **and every opposition**. Email the Word version to crbpo@.
3. Deliver **one** paper chambers copy of **every** filing to the Clerk's Office, 16th Floor, **by noon the next court day**. It must be three-hole punched, carry the ECF header on top, and have exhibit tabs on the right.
4. File amended pleadings with a redline against the prior pleading.
5. For discovery disputes, file a notice of discovery dispute, not a motion.
