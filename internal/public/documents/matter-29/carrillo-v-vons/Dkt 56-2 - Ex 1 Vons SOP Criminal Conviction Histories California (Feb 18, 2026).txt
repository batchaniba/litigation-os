          Standard Operating Procedures Regarding Use and Review of Retail Job
                  Applicants’ Criminal Conviction Histories - California

       This document sets forth standard operating procedures to be implemented in
 adjudicating criminal conviction histories for retail job applicants in order to ensure
 compliance with California law.

    (1)      The Company currently conducts criminal background checks on retail job
             applicants after a conditional offer of employment has been made.
             a. Prior to making a conditional offer of employment, no application for
                employment shall include any question that seeks the disclosure of an
                applicant’s conviction history. Reference: Cal. Gov’t Code § 12952(a)(1).
             b. Prior to making a conditional offer of employment, there shall be no
                inquiry into or consideration of the conviction history (if any) of any
                applicant. Reference: Cal. Gov’t Code § 12952(a)(2).

    (2)      While conducting a conviction history background check in connection with
             any application for employment, no consideration shall be afforded to any
             of the following:
             a. an arrest not followed by conviction;
             b. referral to or participation in a pretrial or post-trial diversion program;
                 and
             c. convictions that have been sealed, dismissed, expunged, or statutorily
                 eradicated pursuant to law. Reference: Cal. Gov’t Code § 12952(a)(3).

    (3)      After a conditional offer of employment is extended, if the intent is to deny
             an applicant a position of employment solely or in part because of the
             applicant’s conviction history, an individualized assessment shall be made
             of whether the applicant’s conviction history has a direct and adverse
             relationship with the specific duties of the job that justify denying the
             applicant the position, which shall include consideration of the following:
             a. the nature and gravity of the offense(s) or conduct;
             b. the time that has passed since the conviction for a criminal offense(s) or
                conduct and/or completion of any related sentence; and
             c. the nature of the job held or sought. Reference: Cal. Gov’t Code §
                12952(c)(1)(A), subds. (i)-(iii).

    (4)      If, after undertaking the individualized assessment described above, a
             preliminary decision is made that an applicant’s conviction history

          a. notice of the disqualifying conviction or convictions that are the basis for

          d. notice that the applicant’s response to the preliminary decision may
             include submission of evidence challenging the accuracy of the
             conviction history report that is the basis for rescinding the job offer,
             evidence of rehabilitation and evidence of mitigating circumstances, or
             both. Reference: Cal. Gov’t Code § 12952(c)(2), subds. (A)-(C).

    (5)   If a final decision is made to deny an applicant employment solely or in part
          because of an applicant’s conviction history, written notification shall be
          provided to the applicant (the “Adverse Action Letter”) of the following:
          a. the final denial or disqualification;
          b. any existing procedure for the applicant to challenge the decision or
             request reconsideration with Company; and
          c. the applicant’s right to file a complaint with the Department of Fair
             Employment and Housing regarding the final decision. Reference: Cal.
             Gov’t Code § 12952(c)(5), subds. (A)-(C).

 The Company also will comply with applicable local laws, including local laws in the
 City of Los Angeles and the City of San Francisco.

 Nothing in this standard operating procedures document is intended to impose
 obligations on the Company beyond or greater than what applicable law specifically
 requires.
