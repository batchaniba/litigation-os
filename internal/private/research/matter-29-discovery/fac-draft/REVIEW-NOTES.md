Complaint ¶¶26-27 and 60 expressly identify the inference and its basis, including contrary waiting-for-results messages. Obtain the transmission and decision records; a completion date or pending status alone is insufficient. The state notice count likewise depends on actual procurement and a qualifying denial.
2. Original complaint ¶37 supplies the preexisting allegations of employment loss, uncertainty and emotional distress. The new draft attributes October distress and corrective time to the alias. Plaintiff must confirm these new personal-knowledge injury allegations and their causal allocation. No medical symptoms, expenses, rejected alternative jobs, recipient reactions, guaranteed seasonal duration, or alias-caused hiring decision have been invented. Mere statutory noncompliance or an internal field is not enough for federal standing.

Three raw San Joaquin entries are distinguished from the final no-record component. No positive registry payload is alleged. No allegation accuses Accurate of lying in a September answer because an October report was later created.
