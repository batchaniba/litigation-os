## Calendar
CMCs 1st & 3rd Tuesdays 1:30 p.m. by **Zoom webinar** (in-person request ≥7 days before, in the CMC statement); civil L&M 1st/3rd/5th Wednesdays 1:30 p.m. in person; pretrial conferences 2nd & 4th Thursdays 1:30 p.m. Joint CMC statement **7 days** before (Civil L.R. 16-9) (SO § 6; judge page).

## Filing checklist (Krishnan)
1. Within 7 days of each defendant's appearance: serve the SO; file a certificate of service.
2. File consent/declination promptly.
3. No chambers copies (except lodged settlement statements).
4. Proposed orders in Word to askpo@ (needed only for non-7-2 motions or injunctive relief).
5. Exhibits as separate ECF attachments, searchable.
6. Redline with any amended pleading.
7. No discovery before 26(f); disputes via meet-and-confer → 5-page joint statement.
8. Joint CMC statement 7 days before Zoom CMC.
