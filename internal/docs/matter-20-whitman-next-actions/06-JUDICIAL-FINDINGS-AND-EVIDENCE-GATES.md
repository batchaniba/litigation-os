## Evidence-first rule
For every proposed factual sentence in the eventual memorandum, fill **source + precise exhibit page/Bates + recording HH:MM:SS + actual CHP position + materiality + uncertainty + admissibility**. A video quotation in a litigant-generated transcript must be verified against native recording. A signed Attorney General discovery response can establish production, not its truth. A verified Answer admission must be quoted precisely, not from a summary.

## Seven affirmative litigation questions — invite an opposing-model objection
1. Does the November 2024 record demonstrate **absence** of probable cause, or merely contradictory observations that a factfinder could resolve in CHP's favor?
2. Which precise facts make detention criminal false imprisonment under PC 236, not just a disputed arrest under civil law? Address PC 847(b) distinction.
3. What statutory condition of Vehicle Code 40300.5 was invoked or reasonably available: collision, roadway obstruction, immediate apprehension, safety risk, or evidence destruction? Why does the shoulder placement and contemporaneous evidence matter?
4. Is there an objectively false or misleading fact in the report instead of a disputed sensory conclusion? Is it material to both the **arrest** and the **April 2026 certification decision**?
5. What did Serrato actually have/review on April 20, and what legal standard did he apply? A denied request plus a five-sentence letter alone cannot answer.
6. Even assuming qualifying victimhood, did Petitioner assist or stand willing to assist investigation of the **alleged officer crime**? Distinguish DUI testing.
7. If the writ should issue, what precisely should Whitman command—certification or a reconsideration on legal grounds—and why is the order within CCP 1085 power?

## Filing decision gate / opposing judgment exercise
The reviewing model should first write a 1-page **defense motion opposition**, then a 1-page **proposed denial rationale**, then identify the few missing documents/verified facts that would defeat its own best arguments. It must not fill evidentiary gaps by hypothesizing the contents of AGO 000055–000060 May 9 withheld personnel-review memoranda.

**Ready for motion:** only when correct procedure and hearing are court-confirmed, RFP written responses/verifications and any deadline are secured, full original video checked and accessible to Whitman, actual April 20 letter and original October 12 Answer quoted correctly, agency review/routing evidence ascertained or transparently identified as unavailable, and each requested writ directive is supported by binding law and admissible primary evidence.

**Not ready:** if the motion depends on (a) no odor because it was never spoken, (b) all FSTs were passed, (c) sub-0.08 at 02:38 disproves 01:12 PC, (d) no criminal charge proves officer guilt, (e) a short denial proves no review, (f) a post-denial May 9 memo is evidence of Serrato's April beliefs, or (g) demurrer survival establishes merits.
