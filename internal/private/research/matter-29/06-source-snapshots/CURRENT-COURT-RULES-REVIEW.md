## FRCP / local rules (black letter; no case law)

- FRCP 26(f)(1)–(3): conference ≥21 days before the scheduling conference; must discuss disclosures, discovery plan, ESI, preservation, privilege, settlement, and other listed topics.
- FRCP 26(a)(1): initial disclosures.
- FRCP 26(d)(1): no discovery before the 26(f) conference except as allowed.
- FRCP 26(d)(2): early Rule 34 delivery; deemed served at the 26(f) conference.
- FRCP 26(g): signature/certification on discovery.
- FRCP 34(b)(2)(A): 30 days to respond (from deemed service for early RFPs).
