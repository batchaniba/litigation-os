## Practical sequence to request

1. Determine whether claims survive and whether amendment is allowed; set that deadline first.
2. Confirm PO status and whether a modified-model declaration/redline is complete. Identify what remains to be filed, by whom, and by what date.
3. Direct prompt conferral over a lawful prior-production path. Favor fresh federal production by the producing party under applicable protection, or a properly supported agreement/order that resolves the actual state restrictions. Respect the issuing court's authority; a new generic federal order is not enough by itself.
4. Set a production target and focused supplement plan once authorization is settled. Seek November 13 if still realistic, or a short period tied to the actual ruling/PO entry. Neither alternative is already ordered.
5. Target policy versions and contracts, plaintiff-specific CTM/Form 3043/reporting/decision records, communications relevant to Smith and retaliation, and causation/damages. Preserve ESI and native records. Use the already drafted discovery sets as candidates, not as proof they were served.
6. Propose ADR after the motion decision and useful document exchange, consistent with plaintiff's existing request for mediation within 45 days after resolution plus prompt production (ECF 28 p.6). No new settlement number is offered.

## CMC oral report - approximately 45 seconds

“The parties held their Rule 26(f) conference, and I served initial disclosures on October 14. The joint statement identifies an existing documentary production from the state action. I request a prompt, lawful re-production or authorization process under appropriate protection, followed by focused supplemental discovery. I would like to confirm the status of the protective order and any required redline. The November 13 date was my proposal, not an entered deadline. I am prepared to work from the surviving claims and any amendment deadline the Court sets, and I request a schedule that avoids unnecessary delay before ADR.”

If defense disclosures were received, report the actual date and status. If not verified, do not state they are overdue or deliberately withheld.

## Conditional responses to defense requests

| Defense position | Proposed response |
|---|---|
| Stay everything pending MTD decision | Ask which discovery and for how long; pending MTD alone is not a stay. If a ruling is imminent, propose narrowly defined sequencing without surrendering existing deadlines. Any actual stay requires its own record and analysis. |
| Existing state production solves all discovery | It is substantial but cross-use, missing categories, native sources, authenticity, and current versions require resolution. Avoid claiming every asserted omission is established without the requests/responses. |
| No production until PO | Ask what genuinely protected material awaits entry and what nonconfidential disclosures can proceed. Verify the actual order and any written agreement first. |
| Trial July 2028 | Point to the bounded parties/issues, existing production, and focused discovery plan; request Court-selected dates after its ruling. Do not insist an unentered June 2027 date is binding. |
| Broad cross-use stipulation proposed live | Ask to review exact language and actual state restrictions before agreement. This memo does not authorize a new substantive waiver or stipulation. |

Contested discovery relief should follow standing order F.5's conferral/joint-letter procedure. A CMC request to establish the path is not a shortcut around that process.
