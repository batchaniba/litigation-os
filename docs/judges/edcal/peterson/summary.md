## Chambers and rules (S1, S2)
- Courtroom 9, 13th floor. CRD Nic Cannarozzi, ncannarozzi@caed.uscourts.gov, (916) 930-4172. Chambers email: jdporders@caed.uscourts.gov.
- Civil matters are on Thursdays at 10:00 am; contact the CRD for dates. Parties **generally appear via Zoom**; scheduling requests go to the CRD at least a week ahead.
- He may submit matters without a hearing under L.R. 230(g).
- **No paper courtesy copies** unless requested.
- If a scheduling conference is set, the joint report is due a full week before it.
- **Discovery: no motion without a pre-motion conference.**
  - Confer by phone, video, or in person; email is insufficient.
  - Then contact the CRD for a Zoom conference.
  - Each side files an informal brief of no more than 3 pages, with **no attachments**, 48 hours before.
  - Formal motions require permission. They must quote each request and response, follow the L.R. 230 schedule, and are capped at 20 pages.
- Proposed orders in Word to jdporders@ with /s/ and the date; put the case name and number in the subject line.
