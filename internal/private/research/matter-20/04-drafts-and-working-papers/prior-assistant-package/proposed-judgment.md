## Judgment and directions

Judgment is entered for Petitioner on the First Cause of Action to the extent stated here. A peremptory writ shall issue directing Respondent California Highway Patrol, acting through its authorized certifying official, and Respondent G. Serrato in his official capacity, as applicable, to:

1. Vacate the April 20, 2026 denial of the April 9, 2026 certification request.

2. Reconsider and decide the request within 30 days after service of the writ. In doing so, Respondents shall apply Penal Code section 679.10 and comply with the findings and legal determinations actually made by this Court, rather than perpetuate any material error identified above. [[S679]]

3. Evaluate the qualifying-activity issue on an accurate factual basis, including the evidence submitted with the request and such additional evidence as may properly be considered consistently with this judgment. Consider the complete circumstances bearing on lawful authority for custody, including any objectively supported alternative ground, rather than treating the asserted DUI ground as the only possible source of authority.

4. If the qualifying-activity predicate is met, evaluate helpfulness under subdivisions (g) and (h), and complete and deliver the certification if the applicable statutory conditions are satisfied. A new determination shall not rest on a factor prohibited by subdivision (k). Any denial shall comply with subdivision (i)(2), as applicable. [[S679]]

5. File and serve a return within 45 days after service of the writ stating the steps taken to comply and attaching the new decision or completed certification, with any legally necessary redactions or protective procedures.

The 30-day and 45-day periods are terms of this judgment and writ. They do not constitute a finding that the original statutory processing period was violated. No certification shall be deemed issued merely because a return is late.

The Court retains jurisdiction to determine compliance and any timely objection to the return. No discovery-compulsion or disclosure order is entered by this judgment.
