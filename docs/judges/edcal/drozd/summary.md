## Chambers and calendar (S1, S2)
- Courtroom 4, 15th floor, Sacramento. CRD Pete Buzo, pbuzo@caed.uscourts.gov, (916) 930-4016.
- Civil law and motion is the 1st and 3rd Mondays at 1:30 pm, **by Zoom only**. The Zoom ID is emailed by the CRD. No date clearing is required.
- The court may take matters under submission under L.R. 230(g). There are no tentative rulings.

## Briefing and motion rules (S2)
- Times New Roman, 12-pt or larger.
- Opening and opposition briefs are limited to 25 pages; replies to 15. Over-length briefs "may not be considered."
- No supplemental briefs without prior leave.
- Meet and confer plus a certification applies "in a case in which the parties are represented by counsel."
- Proposed orders are required only with TRO or preliminary-injunction motions (and bench-trial findings). Email them in Word to dadorders@caed.uscourts.gov.
- Courtesy copies: send none unless directed. The exception is TRO papers, which require a lodged courtesy copy.
- L.R. 233 administrative motions need a proposed order.
- A pro se party may request authorization to e-file.
- At the final pretrial conference, separate pretrial statements are allowed only if a party is not represented by counsel.

## Judicial-emergency practice (S4)
- In Feb 2020, while in Fresno, Judge Drozd issued an emergency civil standing order. It deemed all motions submitted under L.R. 230(g), set no new civil trial dates, and pushed more motions to magistrate judges for F&Rs. He then carried about 1,050 civil actions and 625 criminal defendants.
- The current Rev. 04/17/26 order (S2) restores Zoom hearings, though submission on the papers remains available.

## F&R review habits (S5)
