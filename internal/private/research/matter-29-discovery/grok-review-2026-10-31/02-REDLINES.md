## RFP 8 — matching and alias rules

**Served** (`sources/RFP_Accurate_Set1.txt` lines 196–205, PDF page 4):

> Produce the policies, matching criteria, field-mapping specifications, name-normalization rules, and vendor specifications in effect at any time from July 1, 2026 through October 31, 2026, limited to those applied to Plaintiff’s Screening Order 496623722 and the National Sex Offender Database Search performed for Plaintiff under Search ID 496623723.

**Replacement:**

> Produce the policies, matching criteria, field-mapping specifications, name-normalization rules, alias-generation rules, rules that assign an alias source (including the label "Provided by Applicant"), date-of-birth-only and partial-identifier inclusion and exclusion rules, and vendor specifications in effect at any time from July 1, 2026 through the date of production, that govern criminal-history searches, national criminal database searches, or national sex-offender database searches in employment reports. Include every version operative on July 7, 2026, September 3, 2026, and October 14, 2026. You may designate trade-secret material under the protective order entered in this action. Do not withhold the rules that govern how a name, alias, date of birth, or Social Security number is transmitted to a vendor or is included in or excluded from a report.

**Why.** "limited to those applied" lets Accurate say a general rule was not "applied" to this order, which is the fact in dispute. The Santa Clara request used `first_name` `NEWANSORBI` and `last_name` `NGWA` (disclosure extract p. 31, line 1248). The San Joaquin return flagged three cases `identified_by_name: false` and `identified_by_dob: true` and the report said `NO RECORD FOUND` (extract pp. 18 and 28–29). Those are matching events. The rule is relevant whether or not a person wrote "apply policy X" in the order notes.

**Objection to expect.** Trade secret, and burden of "all employment reports." The replacement is limited to the three search types in this order, and to versions on the three dates that matter. It does not ask for every client's package.

## RFP 16 — agreement, certification, adjudication

**Served** (lines 293–299, PDF page 6):

> Produce Amazon’s permissible-purpose and employment-law certifications applicable to order 496623722 and the parts of the account configuration, order instructions, or screening service agreement showing the scope of the package ordered and the respective compliance responsibilities.

**Replacement:**

> Produce the complete screening services agreement, statement of work, order form, package definition, and account configuration between Accurate and Amazon that governed order 496623722, report Y94611404, and the package identified on that report as "General Package" for requestor "Amazon HVH," including every amendment in effect on July 7, 2026. Produce Amazon’s permissible-purpose certification, employment-purpose certification, and any certification that the user would comply with the FCRA and applicable state law, including the certification described in 15 U.S.C. § 1681b(b)(1) and the certification and written agreement described in Civil Code § 1786.20(a). Produce the adjudication criteria, eligibility matrix, and score-to-decision rules that Accurate used or made available to Amazon for this package, including any rule that treats a sex-offender-registry search, a criminal-record hit, a "pending" status, or an unanswered conviction question as eligible, review, or ineligible. You may designate trade-secret or pricing material under the protective order. Pricing schedules may be produced with dollar figures redacted if the adjudication and compliance terms are produced in full.

**Why.** Pleaded liability is the certification. Complaint ¶ 61. Section 1786.20(a), as posted on leginfo on October 31, 2026, also requires the agency to keep a record of the purpose the user stated and forbids furnishing a report without a written agreement that use will stay within § 1786.12. "parts of" the agreement is how a certification page gets left behind. Adjudication criteria are how a "pending" screen becomes a hiring hold. They are relevant to Penal Code § 290.46, the Fair Chance count, and Defense 10.

**Do not concede.** Redacting prices is a courtesy on a true price schedule. It is not a basis to redact the compliance exhibit or the matrix.

## RFP 18 — dispute file

**Served** (lines 313–317, PDF page 6):

> Produce the ordinary-course dispute-case file, intake logs, investigation activity logs, communications, findings, corrections, and status records concerning Plaintiff’s October 14, 2026 dispute, reference 496623722, and service request 20261014-15421955, through the date of production, excluding communications between Accurate and its counsel protected by the attorney-client privilege and attorney work product.

**Replacement:**

> Produce the ordinary-course dispute-case file for Plaintiff’s dispute of report Y94611404 / order 496623722 and service request 20261014-15421955, from the date the dispute was received through the date of production. Include intake, the dispute as recorded, assignment, activity logs, search queries run or not run, communications with any vendor or furnisher (including Tauxbe Data Inc.), findings, the decision to remove the AKA "Ngwa Nforbi, Newansorbi," the decision that the National Sex Offender Database Search had no reported information, the system note "2026-10-19 09:42:29: Dispute has been initiated," each version of the report saved during the dispute, and the transmittal of the revised report to Amazon. Produce the description of the procedure used to determine the accuracy and completeness of the disputed information, including the business name, address, and (if reasonably available) telephone number of any furnisher contacted. Exclude communications between Accurate and its counsel that are protected by the attorney-client privilege or the work-product doctrine. If any such communication is withheld, produce a log under Rule 26(b)(5) stating the date, authors, recipients, and the specific privilege. A communication with a vendor, with Amazon, or with Plaintiff is not a communication with counsel merely because counsel was later copied.

**Why.** The served request is already good. The replacement names the two decisions the October 28 letter actually announces, the October 19 stamp, and the furnisher. It blocks the move of logging nothing because "the file was shared with Seyfarth." Ordinary-course notes created before counsel's involvement, and vendor operational mail, stay producible. Counsel's advice about those notes can be logged.

**Privilege cutoff to offer at the conference.** Log entries after September 22, 2026 (the date Dkt. 21 was filed) may require a closer look. Do not agree that everything after the dispute was sent to Seyfarth is work product. The consumer dispute file is an ordinary-course FCRA file. Section 1681i(a)(7) requires a procedure description on request. That description is not work product.

## RFP 19 — communications with Amazon

**Served** (lines 331–335, PDF page 6):

> Produce nonprivileged business communications between Accurate and Amazon about Plaintiff’s screening status, identity inputs, search results, exceptions or delivery, excluding post-litigation attorney-client communications and attorney work product.

**Replacement:**

> Produce nonprivileged communications between Accurate and Amazon concerning Plaintiff or order 496623722 / report Y94611404, from July 1, 2026 through the date of production, about any of the following: screening status, a "pending" status, identity inputs, aliases, search results, exceptions, delays, eligibility, adjudication, score, a clear / review / ineligible result, furnishing or access, the October 14, 2026 report, the revised report described in Accurate’s October 28, 2026 letter, or any statement that a result was or was not available. Include portal messages, API payloads, status files, and email. Exclude communications between Accurate and its counsel, and between Amazon and its counsel, that are protected by the attorney-client privilege or the work-product doctrine, and log any withholding under Rule 26(b)(5).

**Why.** The served list omits the words Amazon's system actually used (pending, eligibility) and omits the one communication the October 28 letter asserts: that a revised report was forwarded to the employer. `Closing_Letter_1028.txt` p. 2, lines 28–29. Defense 2 and Defense 10 are about what Amazon received. This request is how that gets proved or retired.

## What not to change

Do not add a definition of "registry" that assumes a hit. Defense 2 is that no registry status was reported. The sex-offender component says no record. The requests should ask for the search, the inputs, and the communications, and let the documents answer.

Do not extend the Set One response date in the redline. A clarification is not a new clock.
