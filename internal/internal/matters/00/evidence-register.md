## Rules

- Preserve originals and metadata.
- Do not edit screenshots or exports except to create clearly labeled redacted
  working copies.
- Do not delete unfavorable material.
- Do not obtain material through unauthorized account access.
- Check recording law before recording any conversation.
- Store intimate media and minor-related material outside Git, if preservation
  is legally necessary at all.
- A lawyer-directed collection may be treated differently; record who directed
  it and when.
