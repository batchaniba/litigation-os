# Rehearsal and outcomes

All preparation dates are targets, not new court clocks. Do not file this checklist.

## Sunday, October 25 - organize

1. Read the command sheet, ECF 30, and state signed order before older favorable analysis.
2. Mark the existing complaint paragraphs used in the argument map. Resolve Count I/II and section 3574 pinpoints.
3. Make a one-page list of facts the complaint actually pleads and facts that would require amendment. Keep them separate.
4. Rehearse the opening once, then answer the ten hardest judge questions without reading a full brief.

## Monday, October 26 - verify

1. Check the current federal docket and notices for setting/format, tentative ruling, opposition, October 20 objection, consents, PO/redline, and later filings. Check the state disposition record.
2. Confirm current facts relevant to any injunction, actual defense disclosures, and what documents can lawfully be used.
3. Print or save offline the public court-use tabs and personal notes. Verify travel and arrival plan if the hearing remains in person.
4. Rehearse two branches: judge agrees on finality; judge rejects finality argument and moves to claim sufficiency. Practice asking for claim-specific leave without repeating the opening.

## Tuesday, October 27 - execute

1. Recheck new notices before leaving. Attend in the ordered format.
2. Identify yourself, answer questions, cite the actual record, and keep the requested relief clear.
3. Ask about amendment scope/deadline, production/PO next steps, and whether a written order will follow.
4. Make contemporaneous notes of the ruling. Obtain the entered order before updating source-of-truth clocks or claiming final deadlines.

## Outcome branches

| Result | Immediate next work |
|---|---|
| MTD denied | Obtain order/notice; confirm response deadline under the order and Rule 12(a)(4); begin the ordered discovery/PO sequence. Do not calculate from oral memory alone. |
| Some claims dismissed with leave | Record surviving claims, exact defects, permitted amendment scope, due date, and service requirements. Draft a complete supported amended pleading under the actual order. |
| Some claims dismissed without leave, others survive | Preserve which claims/theories are affected and whether any final/partial judgment is entered. Proceed on survivors under the order; do not assume appealability from a partial ruling. |
| All claims dismissed with leave | Focus on the permitted cure and actual deadline. Discovery/ADR status depends on the order; do not assume an automatic production stay or entitlement. |
| Case-ending dismissal / preclusion | Obtain order and any separate judgment, notice/entry dates, and magistrate-jurisdiction basis. Audit postjudgment/appellate deadlines immediately from the real instruments. Do not invent a deadline or file a reflex reconsideration request. |
| Taken under submission | Record that no merits ruling was announced; ask if existing CMC/discovery dates remain. No dismissal or victory inferred. |
| Continued / vacated | Preserve actual notice/order and replacement date if set. Continue independent obligations unless changed. |
| PO entered without cross-use language | Read the actual terms; resolve state restrictions or obtain lawful fresh production before relying on the old production. |
