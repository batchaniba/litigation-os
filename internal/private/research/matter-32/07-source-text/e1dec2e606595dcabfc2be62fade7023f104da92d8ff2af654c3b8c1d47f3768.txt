        This action has been assigned to Magistrate Judge Chi Soo Kim. Because all parties on one


side proceed without the assistance of counsel, the Local Rules dictate the Magistrate Judge will


(1) resolve all non-dispositive matters, and (2) conduct all hearings and issue findings and


recommendations on any dispositive matters. See Local Rule 302(c)(21).


        Should the parties wish to consent to the jurisdiction of the Magistrate Judge for all purposes,


including for the entry of final judgment, they may do so using the Court's "Consent to Assignment or


Request for Reassignment" form. See 28 U.S.C. § 636(c). There is no obligation to consent, and under


Federal Rule of Civil Procedure 73(b)(1), the judges will not be notified of a party's choice unless all

parties have consented.
