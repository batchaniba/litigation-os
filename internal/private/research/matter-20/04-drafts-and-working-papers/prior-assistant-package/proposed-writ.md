# [PROPOSED] PEREMPTORY WRIT OF MANDATE

THE PEOPLE OF THE STATE OF CALIFORNIA:

To Respondent CALIFORNIA HIGHWAY PATROL, acting through its authorized certifying official, and Respondent G. SERRATO, in his official capacity as Captain and Commander of the California Highway Patrol Dublin Area, as applicable:

Pursuant to the judgment entered in this action on ____________________, YOU ARE COMMANDED:

1. To vacate the April 20, 2026 denial of Brendan Ngehsi Newanforbi's April 9, 2026 request for Form I-918 Supplement B certification.

2. Within 30 days after service of this writ, to reconsider and decide that request in accordance with Penal Code section 679.10 and the specific findings and legal determinations in the Court's judgment. A new decision must correct the material error identified in that judgment and may not simply repeat it. [[S679]]

3. To evaluate qualifying criminal activity on an accurate factual basis, considering the submitted incident evidence and the full circumstances relevant to lawful authority for custody, including objectively supported alternative authority, consistently with the judgment.

4. If qualifying criminal activity is established, to apply the helpfulness provisions in subdivisions (g) and (h), and to complete, sign and deliver the certification if the statutory conditions are satisfied. Any new decision must avoid prohibited grounds under subdivision (k), and any denial must comply with subdivision (i)(2), as applicable. [[S679]]

5. Within 45 days after service of this writ, to file and serve a return identifying the actions taken to comply, accompanied by the resulting decision or certification, subject to legally required confidentiality and appropriate protective procedures.

This writ does not itself certify Petitioner's form, grant immigration status, compel disclosure of privileged material, or revive a dismissed cause of action. No certification is deemed issued automatically by lapse of a compliance or return deadline.
