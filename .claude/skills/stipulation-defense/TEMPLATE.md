## Recitals and terms

Plaintiff [name], appearing in propria persona, and [named party only],
through its counsel of record, stipulate as follows:

1. [Operative clock as it stands today, with the rule that sets it.]
   [Named party's] time to [answer / otherwise respond / do X] is
   [existing date].

2. The parties agree to [extend that deadline / stay / continue the
   [CMC / hearing] only / advance the hearing], as to [named party /
   named event] only, [through and including [shortest agreed date] /
   to [earlier hearing date] / to [date certain]].

3. This [extension / stay / continuance / advance] does not apply to
   [each other named party]. [Other party]'s [deadline / hearing] is
   unchanged.

4. **Continue the event only (if a CMC or hearing is moving).** No other
   date or deadline in this action is modified. [Disclosures / ADR /
   joint statement / briefing on Dkt. __] remain as set on [date
   certain]. This is not a stay of discovery.

5. This is a single [extension / stay / continuance / advance]. It does
   not alter any date set by Court order[, except the hearing date being
   advanced]. No further [extension / stay / continuance] is implied.
   It does not waive any defense, objection, or right of any party,
   including Plaintiff's right to seek default if no timely response is
   filed.

6. [If they want the continuance:] As consideration, [Defendant shall
   serve initial disclosures / produce [named documents] / accept
   service on [unserved defendant] / stipulate to authenticity of
   [agency records]] on or before [date certain].

7. [If a stay:] "Final resolution" means entry of final judgment or a
   dispositive order, plus expiration of any appeal period. Applicable
   statutory deadlines as to Plaintiff are tolled during the stay. If
   the parties cannot agree on a response date when the stay lifts, the
   response is due [fallback number] days after written notice that the
   stay has lifted. All claims, defenses, and rights are preserved,
   without prejudice.

8. [If advancing a hearing:] The [later dates] are advanced to [earlier
   live date] so the matters are heard together. Plaintiff asks that
   they not be split onto a later calendar.

9. This stipulation is made under [rule]. [It shall be promptly filed.
   No Court order is required. / A proposed order is lodged.] Read the
   proposed order separately; it must not be broader than these terms.

IT IS SO STIPULATED.
