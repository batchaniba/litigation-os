17            Plaintiff has moved under Civil Local Rule 7-11, Federal Rule of Civil Procedure 4(e)(1)

18   and 4(m), and California Code of Civil Procedure section 413.30 for an order directing Deputy

19   Attorney General Haiyang A. Li to accept service of process on behalf of Defendant Santiago

20   Meza-Gonzalez.

21

22            The Court has considered the motion, the supporting declaration, and the record.

23   Defendant Jose Melano has been served. The AO 440 return filed as Dkt. 5-1 and entered as

24   Document 12 establishes service on Melano at CHP South Sacramento on August 14, 2026. No

25   Rule 4(l) return has been filed as to Meza-Gonzalez. The record further shows that Deputy

26   Attorney General Li has represented Meza-Gonzalez and Melano in prior civil actions arising

27   from the same November 1, 2024 incident, and that he is counsel of record for Captain G. Serrato

28   in the related action before this Court, No. 4:26-cv-05953-ASK.

 2          Delivery of the summons and operative complaint to the Deputy Attorney General who

 3   has represented this defendant throughout is a manner of service reasonably calculated to give

 4   actual notice. (Fed. R. Civ. P. 4(e)(1); Cal. Code Civ. Proc. § 413.30; Mullane v. Central

 5   Hanover Bank & Trust Co., 339 U.S. 306, 314 (1950); Rio Properties, Inc. v. Rio Int'l Interlink,

 6   284 F.3d 1007, 1016-17 (9th Cir. 2002).)

 7

 8          GOOD CAUSE APPEARING, IT IS ORDERED that:

 9

10      1. Plaintiff's administrative motion is GRANTED.

11

12      2. Deputy Attorney General Haiyang A. Li is authorized and directed to accept service of

13      process on behalf of Defendant Santiago Meza-Gonzalez in this action.

14

15      3. Plaintiff's delivery to Deputy Attorney General Li, by electronic mail at

16      Haiyang.Li@doj.ca.gov and by first-class mail at 300 South Spring Street, Suite 1702, Los

17      Angeles, California 90013-1230, of (a) the issued summons as to Meza-Gonzalez (Dkt. 3),

18      (b) the operative First Amended Complaint (Dkt. 5), and (c) the Order Setting Initial Case

19      Management Conference and ADR Deadlines (Dkt. 4), shall constitute completed service on

20      Defendant Santiago Meza-Gonzalez under Federal Rule of Civil Procedure 4(e)(1) and

21      California Code of Civil Procedure section 413.30 as of the date of the email transmission.

22

23      4. Plaintiff shall file a proof of that delivery under Federal Rule of Civil Procedure 4(l)

24      within seven (7) days of the email transmission.

25

26      5. Completed service on Defendant Jose Melano is unaffected by this Order.

27

28          IT IS SO ORDERED.
