## The instructed four-tier prayer — **one served motion packet**

1. **Tier 1: Direct CHP certification.** Conditional traditional-mandate remedy requiring CHP/Serrato to sign I-918B if all statutory predicates are established and there is no lawful alternative.
2. **Tier 2: Vacatur/remand for reconsideration.** Alternative traditional-mandate relief for proved material legal error or abuse where lawful agency evaluation remains.
3. **Tier 3: Judge Whitman's own independent judicial certification.** Concurrent request to the presiding judge to evaluate the record and sign a **new Form I-918 Supplement B** in her individual statutory judicial-certifier capacity; not a writ running against the judge and not contingent on denying Tiers 1 or 2.
4. **Tier 4: Focused factual proceeding.** Preserve the existing proposed limited evidentiary order as the procedural fallback if genuinely material factual disputes prevent judgment.

**Authority:** Penal Code § 679.10(a)(3), (b)(3), (c), (d), (g), (h), (k)(1); 8 C.F.R. § 214.14(a)(3)(ii); USCIS U Visa Law Enforcement Resource Guide (judges may identify qualifying criminal activity in a civil proceeding). California law's conditional certification duty does not itself prove victimhood or helpfulness. A signed I-918B, not a judicial judgment alone, supplies the federal certification.

### New § X (between helpfulness and traditional mandate remedies)
> **X. CONCURRENT REQUEST FOR INDEPENDENT JUDICIAL U-VISA CERTIFICATION.** Penal Code § 679.10(a)(3) and (b)(3) recognizes a judge as an authorized certifying entity and official; 8 C.F.R. § 214.14(a)(3)(ii) similarly names a federal, state, or local judge. USCIS recognizes that judges can sign after detecting qualifying crime in a civil proceeding or reviewing records involving a victim. On the evidence properly before the Court, Petitioner requests an independent assessment of qualifying false imprisonment/unlawful criminal restraint, Petitioner's victimhood, information and actual or likely helpfulness. If the criteria are met, Petitioner requests Whitman's personal completion and signature of the fresh unexecuted Attachment JC-1. This is a distinct statutory capacity, not traditional mandate against the judge, and not a claim that the August 29 pleading ruling established incident facts. No charges or conviction are required merely to request certification. (Pen. Code § 679.10(k)(1).) A prior judicial-certification motion before Judge Fickes in Alameda No. 26CV168913 reportedly was denied June 18, 2026. Its actual signed ruling and materially distinguishing evidence/procedure must be presented accurately before final submission.
