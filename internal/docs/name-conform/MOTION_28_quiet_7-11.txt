TO THE COURT AND TO SHANNON SMITH, LCSW, AND HOPE PSYCHOTHERAPY,
INC., THROUGH COUNSEL:

Plaintiff Brendan Ngwa Nforbi, appearing in propria persona and
presently identified on this docket as Brendan Ngehsi Newanforbi,
moves under Civil Local Rule 7-11 for an order conforming the docket
name so that one PACER account can be linked for pro se e-filing.
This is Plaintiff's request. It is not a Civil L.R. 7-12 stipulation.

I. RELIEF REQUESTED

The Clerk shall amend the docket, the short title, the CM/ECF party
record, and the caption of this action so that Plaintiff is identified
as Brendan Ngwa Nforbi — first name Brendan, last name Ngwa Nforbi —
matching Plaintiff's existing PACER account (login bngwanforbi).
Subsequent papers shall use that caption.

II. THE TWO NAMES ARE ONE PERSON

Brendan Ngwa Nforbi is the same natural person as Brendan Ngehsi
Newanforbi. The names are one person. This motion does not add, drop,
or substitute any party. It is not an amendment of any claim, defense,
or operative pleading. It is not a Fed. R. Civ. P. 25 substitution.

III. THE ECF HELP DESK CANNOT LINK THE ACCOUNT UNTIL THE DOCKET MATCHES

On September 1, 2026, the CAND ECF Help Desk advised that it cannot
link this action to PACER login bngwanforbi until the docket reflects
the name on the account, and that a name change requires a request to
the Court. Exhibit A is that reply. After the Clerk updates the party
name, Plaintiff will ask the Help Desk to link that login for pro se
e-filing. This motion does not itself activate e-filing.

IV. CIVIL L.R. 7-11

Conforming a pro se party's docket name so one PACER account can be
linked is a miscellaneous administrative matter. A 35-day noticed
motion is unnecessary. Civil L.R. 7-11(a) requires a proposed order
and either a Civil L.R. 7-12 stipulation or a declaration explaining
why a stipulation could not be obtained. Plaintiff conferred with
Blaze Van Dine and Eric T. Martinez. Mr. Martinez stated he has no
intention of opposing this request. He did not agree to a Civil L.R.
7-12 stipulation. The accompanying declaration satisfies Civil L.R.
7-11(a).

V. THIS REQUEST IS LIMITED TO THE DOCKET NAME

This request is made so that the Clerk can identify Plaintiff by the
name on his PACER account and the ECF Help Desk can link that account
for pro se e-filing. It does not add, drop, or substitute any party.
It does not amend any claim or defense. All prior filings, proofs of
service, acceptances of service, and orders remain effective as to the
same person. No party need be re-served by reason of this caption
change.

Nothing in this motion is a waiver of any claim, defense, objection,
or right except the identity of Plaintiff as the same natural person
and the docket name requested in Section I.

A declaration, Exhibit A, and a proposed order accompany this motion.
