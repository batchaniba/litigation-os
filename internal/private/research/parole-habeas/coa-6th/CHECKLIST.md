## 2. Choose paper or TrueFiling (pro se may do either)

Official: CRC 8.71(b); Sixth District Local Rule 2(10)(1); [E-Filing page](https://appellate.courts.ca.gov/district-courts/6dca/rules-forms-filing/electronic-filing). Self-represented parties **are not required** to e-file.

### Paper (walk-in or mail)

- [ ] Original **+ 4 copies** of the petition/memorandum (CRC 8.44(b)(3)).
- [ ] **1 set** of tabbed, consecutively numbered supporting documents with the original (CRC 8.44(b)(5), 8.486(c)), plus extra exhibit sets for each real party served (AG, DA). **No** exhibit volume on the superior court.
- [ ] **Red** cover on the petition (CRC 8.40(a)).
- [ ] Ask the clerk on arrival whether they want the original bound or unbound (**not specified** in Local Rule 2).
- [ ] Walk-in: **333 W. Santa Clara Street, Suite 1060, San Jose, CA 95113**, posted hours **9:00 a.m.–5:00 p.m.** Call **(408) 277-1004** that morning. Parking: Almaden Boulevard garages / meters (court contact page).
- [ ] Mail: Court of Appeal, Sixth Appellate District, ATTN: Clerk’s Office, same address. **No cash.** Original proceedings are **filed when the clerk receives them**, not when mailed (CRC 8.25(b)(1), (b)(4) — mailbox rule does **not** apply).
- [ ] Serve RPIs **before** filing (CRC 8.25(a)). Attach the POS to the original.

### TrueFiling (optional)

- [ ] Register at **https://tf3.truefiling.com/register**.
- [ ] PDF is text-searchable, paginated from the cover as page 1, bookmarked at every heading / exhibit (CRC 8.74; Local Rule 2(2), (3)(4)).
- [ ] 13-point serif, 1.5 spacing, 1.5-inch left/right margins if the petition is prepared for e-filing (CRC 8.74(b)).
- [ ] File ≤ 100 MB; exhibit files ≤ 25 MB.
- [ ] Finish the upload **before midnight** for that court day (Local Rule 2(5)). After 11:59 p.m. = next court day.
- [ ] New original proceeding: opponents are **not** yet TrueFiling users in this case. **Mail or personally serve** the AG, DA, and superior court anyway.
- [ ] If a fee waiver is on file, ImageSoft EFS fees are waived (Local Rule 2(9)). If there is no court fee and he e-files without a waiver, **FLAG:** TrueFiling may still try to collect a vendor fee — ask the clerk or TrueFiling prompt before submitting.

## 5. After filing (do not do these until a file-stamped copy exists)

- [ ] Keep the file-stamped original or TrueFiling acceptance. The Court of Appeal number will be an **H** number. There is none until the clerk assigns it. Do not invent one.
- [ ] Calendar: real parties **may** file a preliminary opposition within **10 days** of filing (CRC 8.487(a)(1)). Petitioner **may** reply 10 days after that. The Court may act without waiting (*Palma* / rule 8.487(a)(4)).
- [ ] If the Court issues an alternative writ or OSC: return due in **30 days** unless ordered otherwise; Petitioner’s reply **15 days** after the return (CRC 8.487(b)).
- [ ] If Santa Clara **rules** while this writ is pending: notify this Court immediately and lodge the ruling. The mandate may become moot; that is a **win** on the delay issue.
- [ ] Do **not** start a federal 2254 from this filing. Exhaustion still requires a California Supreme Court denial of the **merits** habeas. A mandate grant is not that denial.
- [ ] Update Matter 19 `events.jsonl` / `documents.json` only after there is a real file stamp. This checklist does not move `clocks.json`.
