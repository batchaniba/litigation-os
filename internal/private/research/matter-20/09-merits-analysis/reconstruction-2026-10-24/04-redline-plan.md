# 04 — DRAFT REDLINE PLAN

**Baseline:** The 30-page working motion packet (00-user-inputs/Newanforbi_26CV191136_Writ_Motion_Packet.pdf; 31-page version in 10-working-motion/ with the federal-order insertion). **Method:** Dispositions are by paragraph cluster (Notice / MPA section / Declaration / proposed orders / proof of service), because the working packet's argumentative unit is the section, and a literal per-paragraph table of 30 pages would obscure the consequential changes. Every cluster that carries a legal proposition is dispositioned. **Dispositions:** KEEP / STRENGTHEN / MOVE / CONDENSE / REMOVE / REPLACE.

**Conventions applied to every cluster:** (1) "Answer to Verified Petition" replaces "Verified Answer" throughout; admissions are party admissions, not sworn testimony. (2) No transcript quotation is offered as proof of April 9 contents. (3) No finding is pre-filled that the court has not made. (4) Bracketed material in the replacement is court-findings language or execution blanks only.
