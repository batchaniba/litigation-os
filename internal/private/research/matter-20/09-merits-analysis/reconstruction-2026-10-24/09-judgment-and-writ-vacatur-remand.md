## 2. Findings [for the Court to make, strike, or modify]

[The Court finds as follows:]

(a) Captain G. Serrato denied petitioner's April 9, 2026 certification request on April 20, 2026, by a five-sentence letter (Pet. Ex. 1) that identifies no qualifying crime considered, no element that failed, and no facts supplying lawful authority for the November 1, 2024 restraint, as Penal Code section 679.10, subdivisions (c), (d), and (g) require the certifying official to determine.

(b) The denial states that "[a] thorough review of this incident was conducted" and therefore rests on the agency's incident account. That account, in the Form 202, states that petitioner "chose not to perform" the walk-and-turn because it was difficult and lists "poor performance on the FST's" as an arrest basis (AGO 000031–000032). [The Court finds, on the authenticated recording presented at the hearing / in the lodged exhibits, that the walk-and-turn exchange was as follows: (state the Court's finding). If the Court cannot make this finding on the current record, it should strike subparagraph (b) and proceed under § 3's reconsideration directions without it, or order the focused proceeding in product 10.]

(c) The walk-and-turn inaccuracy, if found as in (b), is material because the "poor performance" rationale is part of the stated arrest basis, and lawful authority is the contested element of the qualifying-crime determination.

(d) A decision that supplies no reviewable reasoning and rests on a materially inaccurate agency account, as found above, is arbitrary and lacks evidentiary support, and cannot be sustained on judicial review without the Court supplying the reasoning the decision omitted. [The Court makes no finding concerning what Captain Serrato subjectively reviewed, considered, or intended, and no finding that any prosecution prerequisite was imposed or that any federal eligibility outcome was predicted.]

(e) The Court does not, in this judgment, find that the direct-certification predicates are established or that certification must issue. Those questions are reserved for the reconsideration directed below, on the corrected record.

## 3. Judgment

IT IS ORDERED, ADJUDGED, AND DECREED that the April 20, 2026 denial is VACATED, the petition for writ of mandate is GRANTED IN PART as to Count One, and a peremptory writ shall issue as stated below.

## 4. Peremptory writ — controlled reconsideration

TO THE CALIFORNIA HIGHWAY PATROL AND ITS CERTIFYING OFFICIAL:

YOU ARE COMMANDED to reconsider petitioner's April 9, 2026 certification request, and to issue a new written decision, as follows:

(a) **Corrected record.** The reconsideration shall be conducted on the corrected record, including the authenticated recording (if presented) and the Form 202 as corrected by the Court's finding in § 2(b) above. No finding in this judgment concerning the walk-and-turn exchange shall be disregarded.

(b) **Element-by-element determination.** The new decision shall determine, element by element, whether the November 1, 2024 restraint was false imprisonment under Penal Code section 236, unlawful criminal restraint under Penal Code section 679.10, subdivision (c)(18), or substantially similar activity under subdivision (d), stating the facts found for each element.

(c) **Arrest-authority findings.** The new decision shall make circumstance-by-circumstance findings under Vehicle Code section 40300.5, subdivisions (a)–(e), and shall address Vehicle Code section 40300.6, stating the facts found for each circumstance addressed.

(d) **Helpfulness determination.** The new decision shall determine helpfulness separately under Penal Code section 679.10, subdivisions (g) and (h), on the complaint and submission record, distinguishing cooperation with the investigation of alleged officer misconduct from compliance with the officers' separate DUI investigation.

(e) **Statutory bars.** The new decision shall address Penal Code section 679.10, subdivisions (k)(1) and (k)(3), expressly, stating whether any ground under those subdivisions is relied on and the facts supporting any such reliance.

(f) **Identified evidence.** The new decision shall state what evidence was considered, including whether the decision file produced in response to Request for Production No. 8 was considered and, if any identified evidence was not considered, why not.

(g) **New decision and return.** The new written decision shall be issued within [30] days of service of this writ [or such other period as the Court sets], shall state the elements, facts, and lawful-authority analysis on which it rests, and shall be filed with this Court together with a verified return within [45] days of service of this writ.

[Optional direction, for the Court's discretion — strike if not adopted: The reconsideration shall be conducted by a certifying official other than Captain G. Serrato.]

This Court retains jurisdiction to enforce this writ, to determine any dispute over compliance, and to adjudicate any further petition concerning the new decision.
