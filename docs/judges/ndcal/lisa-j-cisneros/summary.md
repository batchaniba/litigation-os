## Calendar

- Civil motions: Tuesdays at 10:30 a.m., or Thursdays at 1:30 p.m. during criminal duty.
- CMCs: Thursdays at 1:30 p.m. by Zoom webinar.
- CMC statement due 7 days before the conference. Separate statements are allowed when a party is unrepresented (SO § D.1).
- Zoom links are on the judge page and are not reproduced here.

## Filing checklist (Cisneros)

1. Meet and confer (in person, video, or phone) before **every** motion or non-stipulated request, and include the certification.
2. Email a proposed order in Word to ljcpo@ on the day of filing for anything that needs Court action.
3. Provide a chambers copy only if the filing needs Court action **and** exceeds 50 pages: within 48 hours, or 24 hours for a TRO or PI.
4. Verify every citation. AI errors are imputed to the signer.
5. Oppose on time.
6. Appear personally at the CMC (Zoom).
7. For discovery disputes, follow the formal meet and confer, then the 5-page joint letter.
