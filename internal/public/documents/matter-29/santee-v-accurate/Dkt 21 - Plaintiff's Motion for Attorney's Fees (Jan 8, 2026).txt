 1                                        II. LEGAL STANDARD
 2           Rule 68 provides that where a defendant makes an offer of judgment and the plaintiff
 3   accepts, “the clerk must then enter judgment.” Fed. R. Civ. P. 68(a). When the Rule 68 offer
 4   includes attorneys’ fees and costs to be determined by the Court, the Court must award
 5   reasonable fees and costs as part of the judgment. Marek v. Chesny, 473 U.S. 1 (1985).
 6           Plaintiff is now the prevailing party and is entitled to recover reasonable attorneys’ fees

 7   and costs. The reasonableness of fees is determined using the lodestar method: the number of

 8   hours reasonably expended multiplied by a reasonable hourly rate. Hensley v. Eckerhart, 461

 9   U.S. 424, 433 (1983).
