# Qualifying-crime tests

These ratings measure what the present bucket can support. They do not adjudicate criminal liability.

## False imprisonment — SUPPORTABLE

- Test: intentional confinement without lawful privilege; the U-visa category also includes unlawful criminal restraint.
- Bucket pin: operative petition ¶¶ 34–42 and Exs. 5–7; August 29 order's Count 1 analysis states that, if the pleaded no-odor, no-impairment, successful-test, and false-report facts are credited, probable cause could be found lacking and abuse of discretion could follow.
- Hole: the § 851.6 certificate says “detention only” and “insufficient grounds for making a criminal complaint,” but it does not by itself adjudicate lack of probable cause or every element of false imprisonment. The report and certificate lack standalone bucket objects.

## Felonious assault — OVERCLAIMED

- Test: qualifying “felonious assault” requires a felony-level assault analogue, not merely an unwanted touching or the fact of a blood draw.
- Bucket pin offered: operative petition ¶¶ 39–41 and Ex. 5.
- Hole: the bucket does not pin use of a deadly weapon, force likely to produce great bodily injury, or another felony-assault element. A warrant-based blood draw, even if later challenged, does not establish felonious assault on this record.

## Obstruction of justice — OVERCLAIMED

- Test: the alleged conduct must satisfy obstruction of justice or a substantially similar activity, including the required intent and nexus.
- Bucket pin offered: operative petition ¶¶ 43–48 and Exs. 5–6.
- Hole: discrepancies between a report and recording do not alone prove knowing falsity, corrupt intent, or the necessary nexus to an investigation or proceeding. The bucket contains allegations, not an adjudication or a complete agency investigative file.

## Perjury — OVERCLAIMED

- Test: a material statement known to be false, made under an oath or declaration authorized by law.
- Bucket pin offered: operative petition ¶¶ 43–48 and Ex. 6.
- Hole: the bucket does not pin the report's operative oath language or establish that each challenged statement was knowingly false rather than observation or characterization. The scanned report is embedded, not a searchable standalone object.
