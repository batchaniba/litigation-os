# Open questions

Answer only what is known. Unknown is better than invented.

## Preservation and goals

1. Is the primary goal reconciliation, clarity, preparation, separation, or an
   undecided combination?
2. What would have to change for the marriage to remain workable?
3. Is there an immediate safety, housing, financial, immigration, or child issue?
4. Is there a deadline or event forcing a decision?

## Legal and jurisdictional intake

5. Where and when was the marriage entered?
6. Where has each spouse lived during the last six months?
7. Has either spouse clearly communicated an intent that the marriage is over?
8. Are there children, dependents, agreements, prior cases, or protective orders?
9. Are any immigration rights tied to the marriage?

## Financial intake

10. What income, real property, vehicles, accounts, retirement, businesses,
    digital assets, debts, insurance, and pending claims exist?
11. Which assets or debts predate marriage, arise from inheritance/gift, or may
    require tracing?
12. Are any unusual transfers, withdrawals, new debts, or changes underway?

## Process choice

13. Would both spouses exchange information voluntarily?
14. Is mediation realistic?
15. What terms matter more than money?
16. What outcome must be prevented?
17. What facts require a California family-law lawyer before any action?
