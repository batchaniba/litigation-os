 8                                      FACTUAL ALLEGATIONS

 9    9.     Defendant Accurate Background, Inc ("Accurate") is a consumer reporting agency that

10    provides background checks to employers, including Jeronimo Logistics ("Jeronimo"). Accurate
11
      prepared a background check report on Plaintiff, Stephen Varela, for the purpose of assessing his
12
      suitability for employment with Jeronimo.
13
      10.    Plaintiff applied for a position at Jeronimo Logistics company in September 2025.
14
      11.    On or about September 4, 2025, Accurate issued a background check report on Plaintiff,
15
16    which was later provided to Jeronimo. The report contained inaccuracies, including a criminal

17    conviction that was over seven years old.
18    12.    Specifically, the report listed a felony for which Plaintiff was released from prison in 2017,
19
      which is older than seven years. This is prohibited from being reported under California law,
20
      which restricts the reporting of criminal convictions older than seven years for jobs that pay less
21
      than $125,000 per year (California Civil Code Section 1786.18). Plaintiff’s background check
22
23    was for a position with Jeronimo, which does not meet this salary threshold.

24    13.    Plaintiff was not hired by Jeronimo because of the incorrect background check report.

25    14.    As a result of this refusal to hire him, Plaintiff remained unemployed for three months
26    before finally finding another position that matched his skills and career goals.
27
