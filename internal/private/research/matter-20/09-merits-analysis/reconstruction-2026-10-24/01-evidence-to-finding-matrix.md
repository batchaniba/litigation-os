# 01 — EVIDENCE-TO-FINDING MATRIX

**Purpose:** For every finding Judge Whitman would need to make to grant relief, the original evidence, its admissibility status, its pinpoint, and its remaining proof gap. This matrix is the decision test (assignment § II) in tabular form. "Admissibility" means the showing still required — nothing in this matrix is represented as already admitted into evidence.

**Key to status codes:**
- ESTABLISHED — proved by an authenticated record or an unqualified party admission, subject only to conforming to the filed copy.
- ADMITTED (party admission) — stated in the Answer to Verified Petition; bounded by what the paragraph actually admits.
- SWORN (agency record) — stated in a CHP record (Form 202, affidavit); admissible as a party admission for what CHP said, but its truth is disputed where noted.
- CONDITIONAL — depends on native-recording verification; falls away if the recording contradicts it.
- UNKNOWN — depends on an unproduced record (RFP No. 8) or an uncompleted task.
- DISPUTED — genuinely contested; requires a credibility or weight finding the papers alone cannot supply.
