16   Fourth Amendment -- Judicial Deception and Unlawful

18   Materially False or Misleading Statements

19   (42 U.S.C. § 1983 -- Against Defendant Meza-Gonzalez and DOES 1-10)

20

21          38. Plaintiff re-alleges paragraphs 1 through 37.

22

23          39. An officer violates the Fourth Amendment by submitting a warrant application

24   containing statements that are deliberately or recklessly false, or that omit material facts, if the

25   application -- stripped of the false statements and supplemented with the omitted facts -- would

26   not support probable cause. Franks v. Delaware, 438 U.S. 154, 155-56 (1978); Chism v.

27   Washington State, 661 F.3d 380, 386 (9th Cir. 2011).

28

                                                   Page 12
               COMPLAINT FOR DAMAGES -- NGWA NFORBI v. MEZA-GONZALEZ, ET AL. (N.D. Cal.)
 1          40. Defendant Meza-Gonzalez executed under penalty of perjury the DS-367 and arrest

 2   report containing the four contradicted representations identified in paragraph 26 and the material

 3   omissions identified in paragraph 27. Those documents formed the basis of the warrant

 4   authorizing the involuntary extraction of Plaintiff's blood. When the contradicted representations

 5   are set aside and the omitted facts are restored, the remaining application does not plausibly

 6   support probable cause to believe Plaintiff was driving under the influence.

 7

 8          41. Defendant Meza-Gonzalez acted with at least reckless disregard for the truth. An

 9   officer who executes a sworn statement while a contemporaneous recording of the same

10   encounter -- stored on his own patrol vehicle -- contains information materially inconsistent with

11   that sworn statement acts with reckless disregard, at minimum. The right to be free from a

12   warrant obtained through materially false or misleading statements was clearly established before

13   November 1, 2024. Franks, 438 U.S. at 155-56; Chism, 661 F.3d at 386; Galbraith v. County of

14   Santa Clara, 307 F.3d 1119, 1126 (9th Cir. 2002).

15

16          42. As a direct and proximate result, Plaintiff suffered a compelled blood extraction

17   without valid probable cause, and the further damages alleged in paragraphs 36-37.
