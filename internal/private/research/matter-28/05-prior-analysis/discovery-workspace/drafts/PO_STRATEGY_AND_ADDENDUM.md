# Protective-Order Strategy (Matter 28): DRAFT / INTERNAL

## The problem
- San Joaquin Stipulated PO (signed June 2026) §VI: Confidential material may be used solely for that litigation,
  plus the named related action *Newanforbi v. Derrick*, E.D. Cal. 2:26-cv-00004 (Matter 3, closed Aug 26, 2026).
  **Matter 28 is not covered.**
- §VII: modification by application to the San Joaquin court for good cause. §IX: that court retains jurisdiction.
- §VIII: return or destroy within 60 days after the later of the conclusion of the San Joaquin action or *Derrick*.
  ("Conclusion" = 30 days after the time to appeal or challenge any final judgment.) Earliest estimate is ~Dec 20–24,
  2026, depending on whether the San Joaquin action has "concluded" without a §581d judgment. Verify; not a clock.
- §X: a regulatory-reporting carve-out (agencies and licensing boards) is limited to within 90 days of termination.
  Check whether it is still open before relying on it.
- Federal proposed PO (filed by SLB 10/14): the N.D. Cal. model, with no clause about prior productions.

## Options, ranked
1. **Stipulated "prior production" paragraph in the federal PO** (best; cheap; before entry). Proposed text below.
2. **RFP No. 1:** HOPE re-produces HOPE_000001–542 in Matter 28. The state PO restricts only the receiving party, so
   no state-court order is needed. Takes 30 days; HOPE can designate under the federal PO.
3. **Written consent letter from HOPE** waiving §VI as to Matter 28 (a short stipulation; could also be filed in San
   Joaquin).
4. **Application to San Joaquin Superior Court** to modify §VI (Foltz, 331 F.3d 1122, persuasive only; the court
   retains jurisdiction under §IX). Slowest. Note the court ruled against Plaintiff on 7/15, and filing there could
   re-open the finality fight that Dkt 30 relies on (it would show the court is still acting in the case; it could
   cut either way, so consider carefully).
5. Do NOT ask Judge Cisneros to "modify" the state order directly. Under Foltz the issuing court controls
   modification; at most ask her to order re-production under Rule 34 (which is option 2).

## Proposed addendum paragraph (draft for the meet-and-confer; not sent)

> **Prior Productions.** Documents produced by any Party in *Newanforbi v. Palacios et al.*, San Joaquin County
> Superior Court (the "State Action"), including documents Bates-numbered HOPE_000001 through HOPE_000542, are deemed
> produced in this action as of the date of entry of this Order, and retain any confidentiality designation made in
> the State Action, which shall be treated as a "CONFIDENTIAL" designation under this Order. As to such documents,
> the Parties agree that use in this action shall be governed by this Order, and the Producing Party consents to
> such use notwithstanding any use limitation in the protective order entered in the State Action. Nothing in this
> paragraph waives any objection to admissibility.

Also ask for: (a) a stipulation that the San Joaquin return-or-destroy clause is suspended while Matter 28 is pending;
(b) keep the order single-tier to avoid "attorneys' eyes only" designations that a pro se
party cannot access.
