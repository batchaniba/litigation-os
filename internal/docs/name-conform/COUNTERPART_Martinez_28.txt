Plaintiff Brendan Ngwa Nforbi, appearing in propria persona and presently
identified on this docket as Brendan Ngehsi Newanforbi, and Defendants
Shannon Smith, LCSW, and HOPE Psychotherapy, Inc., through their counsel
of record, stipulate and jointly request as follows:

1.  Plaintiff has appeared in this District under more than one name form.
    Brendan Ngwa Nforbi and Brendan Ngehsi Newanforbi refer to the same
    natural person — the Plaintiff in this action. Plaintiff's existing
    PACER account (login bngwanforbi) records his first name as Brendan and
    his last name as Ngwa Nforbi. On September 1, 2026, the CAND ECF Help
    Desk advised that it cannot link this action to that account until the
    docket reflects the name on the account. That is the reason for this
    request.

2.  The Clerk shall amend the docket, the short title, the CM/ECF party
    record, and the caption of this action so that Plaintiff is identified
    as Brendan Ngwa Nforbi — first name Brendan, last name Ngwa Nforbi.
    Subsequent papers shall use that caption.

3.  This stipulation does not add, drop, or substitute any party. It is not
    an amendment of any claim, defense, or operative pleading. It is not a
    Fed. R. Civ. P. 25 substitution.

4.  All prior filings, proofs of service, acceptances of service, and
    orders remain effective as to the same person. No party need be
    re-served by reason of this caption change.

5.  This request is limited to the docket name. It does not seek any other
    relief.

6.  Nothing in this stipulation is a waiver of any claim, defense,
    objection, or right, except the identity of Plaintiff as the same
    natural person and the docket name requested in paragraph 2.

IT IS SO STIPULATED.

                    [PROPOSED] ORDER

The Court has considered the parties' Civil L.R. 7-12 stipulation and
request to conform Plaintiff's name on the docket. Good cause appearing,
IT IS ORDERED that:

1.  The Clerk shall amend the docket, the short title, the CM/ECF party
    record, and the caption of this action so that Plaintiff is identified
    as Brendan Ngwa Nforbi (first name Brendan; last name Ngwa Nforbi).
    Subsequent papers shall use that caption.

2.  Brendan Ngwa Nforbi is the same natural person previously identified
    on this docket as Brendan Ngehsi Newanforbi. This Order does not add,
    drop, or substitute any party and does not amend any claim or defense.

3.  All prior filings, service, acceptances of service, and orders remain
    effective. No party need be re-served by reason of this Order.

4.  This Order grants no other relief.

IT IS SO ORDERED.
