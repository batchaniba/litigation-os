# XVI. COUNT FOUR

119. 124. Plaintiff does not presently seek preventive relief under § 290.46(j)(4)(B).

125. This Count is limited to use of information disclosed pursuant to § 290.46, as shown by the SOURCE OF RECORD field on Order WPS-361214148.
