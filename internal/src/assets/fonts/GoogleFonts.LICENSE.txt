Both files are the latin subset of the variable (weight 400-700) build, fetched
from fonts.gstatic.com and committed so the app does not depend on a
third-party origin at render time. Regenerate by re-requesting the Google Fonts
CSS2 API with a modern browser User-Agent and taking the `/* latin */` src URL:

  curl -H "User-Agent: Mozilla/5.0 ... Chrome/120.0.0.0 ..." \
    "https://fonts.googleapis.com/css2?family=Cormorant+Garamond:wght@400;500;600;700&family=DM+Sans:wght@400;500;600;700&display=swap"
