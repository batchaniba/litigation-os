 5   On September 1, 2026 the CAND ECF Help Desk advised that it cannot link this action to

10

11   Plaintiff recently learned that this District permits pro se litigants to e-file. Good cause exists to

12   conform the docket name so that login can be linked. Among other things, e-filing will prevent

13   the delay inherent in mailing papers to the Clerk and to counsel, wherever those papers are sent

14   from; it will put each paper on the docket the day it is filed rather than days later in the mail; it

15   will let counsel of record receive service the same day; and it will let Plaintiff prosecute these

16   five open N.D. Cal. actions on one account instead of mailing five copies of every paper.

17   Mailing also carries weekend and holiday lag and the risk that a paper reaches the Clerk after

18   the date it was sent.

13

14   Conforming a pro se party's docket name so one PACER account can be linked is a

15   miscellaneous administrative matter. A 35-day noticed motion is unnecessary. Civil L.R.

16   7-11(a) requires a proposed order and either a Civil L.R. 7-12 stipulation or a declaration

17   explaining why a stipulation could not be obtained. Plaintiff conferred with Deputy Attorney

18   General Haiyang A. Li. He stated he will not oppose this request. He has not appeared in this

19   action. His statement is non-opposition to this caption request — not an appearance and not

20   acceptance of service. Dkt. 13 remains pending. A Civil L.R. 7-12 stipulation could not be

21   obtained. The accompanying declaration satisfies Civil L.R. 7-11(a).
